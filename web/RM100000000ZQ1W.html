INTEGRATION RELAY INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT NO. 1 INTEGRATION RELAY


    1. IG1 NO. 3 RELAY:


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (No. 1 Integration Relay)

        Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        1A-4 - 1A-3 Auxiliary battery voltage not applied between terminals 1A-2 and 1A-1 10 kΩ or higher

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 1A-2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 1A-1

        		 Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 1 integration relay.
