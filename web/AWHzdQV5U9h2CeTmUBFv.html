PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL ELECTRICAL KEY AND TIRE PRESSURE WARNING ECU AND RECEIVER

    Note


    • Do not drop, strike or otherwise subject the electrical key and tire pressure warning ECU and receiver to impact.

    • If the electrical key and tire pressure warning ECU and receiver is subjected to an impact, replace it with a new one.


    1. Connect the connector.

    2. Engage the 2 guides and install the electrical key and tire pressure warning ECU and receiver with the bolt.

      Torque:
      8.3 N*m  { 85 kgf*cm, 73 in.*lbf }
