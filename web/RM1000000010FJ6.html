REAR DOOR COURTESY SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT REAR DOOR COURTESY LIGHT SWITCH ASSEMBLY


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Rear Door Courtesy Light Switch Assembly)
      *b Switch Body

      Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - Switch body Not pushed (ON) Below 1 Ω
      Pushed (OFF) 10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear door courtesy light switch assembly.
