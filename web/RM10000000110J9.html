ELECTRONICALLY CONTROLLED BRAKE SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. DTC CHECK/CLEAR


    1. Check for DTCs.


      1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

      2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      3. Turn the GTS on.

      4. Read the DTCs following the prompts on the GTS screen. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / Trouble Codes.


        Chassis > ABS/VSC/TRC > Trouble Codes

      5. Check the details of the DTCs.

        Click here

    2. Clear the DTCs.


      1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

      2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      3. Turn the GTS on.

      4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / Trouble Codes.

      5. Press the DTC clear button.


        Chassis > ABS/VSC/TRC > Clear DTCs

  2. END OF DTC CHECK/CLEAR


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    2. Check that the ABS warning, brake warning / yellow (minor malfunction) and slip indicator lights go off when the power switch is turned on (READY).
