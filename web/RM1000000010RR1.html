AUTOMATIC HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVEL CONTROL SYSTEM Headlight Beam Level Control Actuator Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assemblies operate the headlight leveling motors according to the vehicle conditions.

WIRING DIAGRAM

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

If the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH has been replaced, it is necessary to synchronize the vehicle information and initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.

Click here

    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (READY).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / HL AutoLeveling / Active Test.

    5. Perform the Active Test according to the display on the GTS.
