GRILLE SHUTTER REASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL NO. 5 RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL

    Tech Tips

    When installing the No. 5 radiator grille seal, heat the radiator shutter using a heat light.

    Heating Temperature
    Item Temperature
    Radiator Shutter 20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)

    Note

    Do not heat the radiator shutter excessively.


    1. Clean the radiator shutter surface.


      1. Using a heat light, heat the radiator shutter surface.

      2. Remove any remaining double-sided tape from the radiator shutter.

      3. Wipe off any tape adhesive residue with cleaner.

    2. Using a heat light, heat the radiator shutter.

    3. Remove the release paper from a new No. 5 radiator grille seal.

      Tech Tips

      After removing the release paper, keep the exposed adhesive free from foreign matter.

    4. *a Line

      Install the No. 5 radiator grille seal as shown in the illustration.

      Tech Tips

      Install a No. 5 radiator grille seal along the line on the radiator shutter.
