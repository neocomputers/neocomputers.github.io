SEAT HEATER SYSTEM SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


  1. FRONT SEAT HEATER


    1. By operating the seat heater switch, the temperature can be controlled within the range of 36 to 41°C (96.8 to 105.8°F).

    2. The seat heater switch indicator lights indicate the seat heater operating states.

    3. The seat heater indicator (indicator light assembly) indicate the seat heater operating status for the front passenger seat.
