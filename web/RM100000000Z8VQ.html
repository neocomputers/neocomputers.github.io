AIRBAG SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. DTC CHECK (USING SST CHECK WIRE)


    1. Check for DTCs (Current DTCs).


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

      2. Using SST, connect terminals TC and CG of the DLC3.

        SST
        09843-18040

        Note

        Connect the correct terminals to avoid a malfunction.

    2. Check for DTCs (History DTCs).


      1. Turn the power switch off.

      2. Using SST, connect terminals TC and CG of the DLC3.

        SST
        09843-18040

        Note

        Connect the correct terminals to avoid a malfunction.

      3. Turn the power switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

    3. Read the DTCs.


      1. Read the blinking patterns of the DTCs. As an example, the blinking patterns for the normal system code and DTCs 11 and 31 are shown in the illustration.


        • Normal system code indication (without history DTCs)

          The light blinks twice per second.

        • Normal system code indication (with history DTCs)

          When a history DTC is stored in the airbag sensor assembly, the light blinks only once per second.

        • DTC indication

          The first blink(s) indicate the first number of the DTC. The second blink(s) indicate the second number of the DTC and occur after a 1.5-second pause.

        Note

        When a DTC includes a letter (such as DTC 8A), the light blinks 8 times and then blinks 10 times to indicate the A after a 1.5-second pause.

        Comparison Table
        Number of Blinks Indicated Letter
        10 A
        11 B
        12 C
        13 D
        14 E
        15 F

        If there is more than 1 code, there will be a 2.5-second pause between each code. After all codes are shown, there will be a 4.0-second pause, and then all codes will be repeated.

        Tech Tips


        • If 2 or more malfunctions are detected, the indication begins with the smaller-numbered code.

        • If DTCs are indicated without connecting the terminals, refer to TC and CG Terminal Circuit.

  2. DTC CLEAR (USING SST CHECK WIRE)


    1. Clear the DTCs.


      1. When the power switch is turned off, the DTCs are cleared.

        Tech Tips

        Depending on the DTC, the code may not be cleared by turning the power switch off. In this case, proceed to the next step.

      2. Using SST, connect terminals TC and CG of the DLC3, and then turn the power switch on (IG).

        SST
        09843-18040

      3. Disconnect terminal TC of the DLC3 within 3 to 10 seconds after the DTCs are output, and check if the SRS warning light comes on after 3 seconds.

      4. Within 2 to 4 seconds after the SRS warning light comes on, connect terminals TC and CG of the DLC3.

      5. The SRS warning light will go off within 2 to 4 seconds after connecting terminals TC and CG of the DLC3. Disconnect terminal TC within 2 to 4 seconds after the SRS warning light goes off.

      6. The SRS warning light will come on again within 2 to 4 seconds after disconnecting terminal TC. Reconnect terminals TC and CG within 2 to 4 seconds after the SRS warning light comes on.

      7. Check if the SRS warning light goes off within 2 to 4 seconds after connecting terminals TC and CG of the DLC3. Also check if the normal system code is output within 1 second after the SRS warning light goes off.

        If DTCs are not cleared, repeat this procedure until the codes are cleared.

  3. DTC CHECK (USING GTS)


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the power switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / SRS Airbag / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > SRS Airbag > Trouble Codes

    6. Check for DTCs by following the prompts on the GTS screen.

      Tech Tips

      Refer to the GTS operator's manual for further details.

  4. DTC CLEAR (USING GTS)


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the power switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / SRS Airbag / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > SRS Airbag > Clear DTCs

    6. Clear the DTCs by following the prompts on the GTS screen.

      Tech Tips

      Refer to the GTS operator's manual for further details.
