  1. RECOVER REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM

  2. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK ASSEMBLY

  3. REMOVE NO. 1 HEATER AIR DUCT SPLASH SHIELD SEAL (for LHD)

    Click here

  4. REMOVE NO. 2 HEATER AIR DUCT SPLASH SHIELD SEAL (for RHD)

    Click here

  5. REMOVE WATER GUARD PLATE

    Click here

  6. REMOVE COWL BODY MOUNTING REINFORCEMENT LH

    Click here

  7. REMOVE OUTER COWL TOP PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  8. REMOVE MAGNET VALVE BRACKET


    1. for LHD:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Disengage the clamp and claw.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Disengage the clamp and claw.

    3. Remove the 2 bolts and magnet valve bracket.

  9. REMOVE ELECTRIC EXPANSION VALVE (COOLING)


    1. Remove the 2 bolts.

    2. Disconnect the discharge tube J and discharge tube G to remove the electric expansion valve (cooling).

    3. Remove the 2 O-rings from the discharge tube J and discharge tube G.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.
