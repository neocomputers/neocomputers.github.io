SEAT BELT WARNING SYSTEM(w/o Occupant Classification System) Driver Side Seat Belt Warning Light does not Operate

DESCRIPTION

The combination meter assembly blinks or turns off the seat belt warning light on the combination meter assembly in accordance with the state of the front seat inner belt assembly (driver seat).

WIRING DIAGRAM

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

The seat belt warning system uses the CAN communication system. First, confirm that there is no malfunction in the CAN communication system. Refer to the How to Proceed with Troubleshooting procedure.

Click here

Tech Tips

The seat belt warning light on the combination meter assembly is used for both the driver seat and front passenger seat.Check that the operation of the seat belt warning light is normal first.

Click here

If the seat belt warning light does not operate for both the driver seat and front passenger seat, replace the meter circuit plate.

Click here
