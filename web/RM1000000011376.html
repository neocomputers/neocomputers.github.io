BACK DOOR OPENER SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT BACK DOOR OPENER SWITCH ASSEMBLY


    1. Check the operation of the opener switch.


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Back Door Opener Switch Assembly)

        Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        2 - 3 Back door opener switch not pushed 10 kΩ or higher
        2 - 3 Back door opener switch pushed Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the back door opener switch assembly.

    2. Check the operation of the lock switch. (w/ Entry System Back Door Open Function)


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        1 - 2 Back door lock switch not pushed 10 kΩ or higher
        1 - 2 Back door lock switch pushed Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the back door opener switch assembly.
