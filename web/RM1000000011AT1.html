HEADUP DISPLAY SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK FOR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Head Up Display / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Head Up Display > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Head Up Display / Clear DTCs.


      Body Electrical > Head Up Display > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
