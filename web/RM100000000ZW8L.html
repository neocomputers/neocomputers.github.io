PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 FLOOR UNDER COVER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE FUEL VAPOR CONTAINMENT VALVE (FUEL TANK SOLENOID MAIN VALVE ASSEMBLY)


    1. Disconnect the fuel vapor containment valve (fuel tank solenoid main valve assembly) connector.

    2. Disconnect the fuel tank vent hose sub-assembly and fuel tank vent hose from the fuel vapor containment valve (fuel tank solenoid main valve assembly).

      *1 Fuel Tank Vent Hose Sub-assembly *2 Fuel Tank Vent Hose
      *3 Pipe (Fuel Vapor Containment Valve (Fuel Tank Solenoid Main Valve Assembly)) - -

      Pinch Pull
      Pull off - -

      Note


      • Remove any dirt or foreign matter on the tube connectors before performing this work.

      • Do not allow any scratches or foreign matter to get on the parts when disconnecting them as the tube connectors has an O-ring that seals the pipe.

      • Perform this work by hand. Do not use any tools.

      • Do not forcibly bend, twist or turn the fuel tank vent hose sub-assembly and fuel tank vent hose.

      • Protect the disconnected parts by covering them with plastic bags after disconnecting the fuel tank vent hose sub-assembly and fuel tank vent hose.

      • If the tube connector and pipe are stuck, push and pull to release them.

      Tech Tips

      Do not remove the retainer.


      1. Pinch the tabs of the retainer to disengage the lock claws and pull it down.

      2. Pull off the fuel tank vent hose sub-assembly and fuel tank vent hose.

    3. Remove the 2 nuts and fuel vapor containment valve (fuel tank solenoid main valve assembly) from the vehicle body.
