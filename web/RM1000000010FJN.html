FRONT TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH

    *a

    Component without harness connected

    (Front Turn Signal Light Assembly LH)

    1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the front turn signal light assembly LH and check that the light illuminates.

      OK
      Condition Specified Condition

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 5

      		 Turn signal light illuminates

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 5

      		 Fog light illuminates

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 4

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 5

      		 Daytime running light illuminates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the front turn signal light assembly LH.

  2. INSPECT FRONT TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

    *a

    Component without harness connected

      		 Turn signal light illuminates

      		 Fog light illuminates

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 4

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 5

      		 Daytime running light illuminates
