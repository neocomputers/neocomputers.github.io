LANE DEPARTURE ALERT SYSTEM(w/ Steering Control) DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / LKA/LDA / Trouble Codes.

    5. Check for details of the DTCs.

      Click here


      Chassis > LKA/LDA > Trouble Codes

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / LKA/LDA / Clear DTCs.

    5. Clear the DTCs.


      Chassis > LKA/LDA > Clear DTCs
