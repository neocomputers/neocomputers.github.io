FRONT DOOR SPEAKER INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT NO. 1 SPEAKER ASSEMBLY


    1. With the speaker installed, check that there is no looseness or other abnormalities.

    2. Check that there is no foreign matter in the speaker, no tears on the speaker cone or other abnormalities.

    3. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Front No. 1 Speaker Assembly)

      Measure the resistance of the speaker.

      Standard Resistance (for 6 Speakers)
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 Always 3.2 to 4.8 Ω
      Standard Resistance (for 10 Speakers)
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 Always 4.6 to 7.0 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the speaker.
