PRE-CRASH SAFETY SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:C1A4A

DTC Code DTC Name
C1A4A Skid Control Buzzer Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The driving support ECU assembly operates the pre-crash warning by sending a buzzer request signal to the skid control buzzer.

If the driving support ECU assembly detects a malfunction in the skid control buzzer circuit, it will store DTC C1A4A.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1A4A Skid Control Buzzer Circuit

When the power switch is on (IG) and the pre-crash warning is operating, either of the following conditions is met:


  • A buzzer request signal is sent to the skid control buzzer but the buzzer does not sound for 1 second or more.

  • A buzzer request signal is not sent to the skid control buzzer but the buzzer sounds for 1 second or more.

  • Skid control buzzer

  • Driving support ECU assembly

  • Wire harness or connector

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note


  • Inspect the fuses for circuits related to this system before performing the following procedure.

  • When replacing the driving support ECU assembly, always replace it with a new one. If a driving support ECU assembly which was installed to another vehicle is used, the information stored in the driving support ECU assembly will not match the information from the vehicle. As a result, a DTC may be stored.
