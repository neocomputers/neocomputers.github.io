FORWARD RECOGNITION CAMERA SYSTEM SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


  1. GENERAL DESCRIPTION


    1. The forward recognition camera processes the image captured by the monocular camera to detect lane markers, lights, vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, etc. The obtained information is sent to the systems that use the forward recognition camera.
