  2. OSCILLOSCOPE WAVEFORMS

    Tech Tips

    Oscilloscope waveform samples are provided here for reference only. Noise and fluttering waveforms have been omitted.


    1. Waveform 1 (Rotation angle sensor signal)

      Item Contents
      Terminal

      CH1: A41-16 (RA) - A41-44 (E2)

      CH2: A41-29 (RB) - A41-44 (E2)
      Equipment setting 2 V/DIV., 200 msec./DIV.
      Condition

      Power switch on (IG),

      changing from park (P) to neutral (N), or neutral (N) to park (P)

      Tech Tips

      When changing from park (P) to neutral (N), or neutral (N) to park (P) with the power switch on (IG), a waveform will be generated for up to 1.5 seconds.

    2. Waveform 2 (CAN communication signal)

      Item Contents
      Terminal

      CH1: I2-7 (CA3P) - I3-3 (E1)

      CH2: I2-20 (CA3N) - I3-3 (E1)
      Equipment setting 1 V/DIV., 50 μs/DIV.
      Condition Power switch on (IG)

    3. Waveform 3 (Transmission control communication signal)

      Item Contents
      Terminal I3-6 (PPOS) - I3-3 (E1)
      Equipment setting 10 V/DIV., 10 ms/DIV.
      Condition Engine stopped (hybrid system stopped), power switch on (IG)
