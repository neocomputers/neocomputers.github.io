PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 HEATER TO REGISTER DUCT SUB-ASSEMBLY (for RHD)

    Click here

  3. REMOVE NO. 2 HEATER TO REGISTER DUCT SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  4. REMOVE NO. 2 KNEE PROTECTOR BRACKET (for LHD)

    Click here

  5. REMOVE NO. 3 KNEE PROTECTOR BRACKET (for LHD)

    Click here

  6. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. for LHD:


      1. Disengage the 6 clamps and remove the antenna cord sub-assembly.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Disengage the 4 clamps and remove the antenna cord sub-assembly.

  7. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (for RHD)


    1. w/ Navigation Antenna:


      1. Disengage the 6 clamps and remove the antenna cord sub-assembly.

  8. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  9. REMOVE NO. 2 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. w/o Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:


      1. Remove the adhesive tape from the roof headlining assembly.

        Adhesive Tape Front

    2. w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:


      1. Disengage the clamp.

        Adhesive Tape Front

      2. Remove the adhesive tape from the roof headlining assembly.

    3. Remove the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly from the roof headlining assembly.

  10. REMOVE NO. 5 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna)


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the clamp and remove the No. 5 antenna cord sub-assembly.

  11. REMOVE NO. 3 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the claw.

    3. Disengage the 6 clamps and remove the No. 3 antenna cord sub-assembly.

  12. REMOVE BACK DOOR UPPER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  13. REMOVE NO. 4 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. w/o Glass Breakage Sensor:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Disengage the 2 clamps.

      3. Disconnect the connector.

      4. Disengage the clamp.

    2. w/ Glass Breakage Sensor:


      1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

      2. Disengage the 2 clamps.

      3. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

      4. Disengage the 4 clamps.

    3. Disengage the 8 claws to separate the 2 grommets.

      Note

      When reusing the No. 4 antenna cord sub-assembly, make sure to replace the No. 1 wire harness protector and No. 2 wire harness protector with new ones as reusing them may cause water ingress.

    4. Remove the No. 4 antenna cord sub-assembly.

    5. When reusing the No. 4 antenna cord sub-assembly:


      1. for Back Door Side:


        1. Tape

          Remove the tape to disconnect the antenna cord from the No. 2 wire harness protector.

        2. Tape

          Remove the tape to disconnect the rubber protrusion of the grommet from the No. 2 wire harness protector.

      2. Remove in this Direction

        Separate the No. 1 wire harness protector from the grommet as shown in the illustration.

        Tech Tips

        Use the same procedure for the No. 2 wire harness protector.

      3. Remove in this Direction

        Disengage the claw as shown in the illustration and remove the No. 1 wire harness protector.

        Tech Tips

        Use the same procedure for the No. 2 wire harness protector.
