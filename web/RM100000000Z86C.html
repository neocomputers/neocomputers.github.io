NAVIGATION SYSTEM(for Radio and Display Type) Microphone Circuit between Radio Receiver and Extension Module

DESCRIPTION

This circuit sends a microphone voice signal from the navigation ECU to the radio and display receiver assembly.

Using this circuit, the navigation ECU disables the microphone function of the radio and display receiver assembly to receive a microphone voice signal from the telephone microphone assembly.

WIRING DIAGRAM

*a (Shielded)
*b Radio and Display Receiver Assembly
*c Navigation ECU

PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (RADIO AND DISPLAY RECEIVER ASSEMBLY - NAVIGATION ECU)


    1. Disconnect the o12 radio and display receiver assembly connector.

    2. Disconnect the o13 navigation ECU connector.

    3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance

      o12-5 (MIC+) or o13-16 (MIC+) - Body ground Always 10 kΩ or higher
      o12-6 (MIC-) or o13-17 (MIC-) - Body ground Always 10 kΩ or higher
