RAIN SENSOR REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE RAIN SENSOR COVER


    1. *a Stopper
      Release
      Remove in this Direction

      Release the stopper by pulling it out as shown in the illustration.

    2. Disconnect the rain sensor with rain sensor cover from the windshield glass as shown in the illustration.

    3. Disengage the 2 claws and guide to remove the rain sensor cover.

  2. REMOVE RAIN SENSOR


    1. Disconnect the connector to remove the rain sensor.
