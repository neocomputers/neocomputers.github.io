  15. DISCONNECT ENGINE WIRE (w/o Canister Pump Module)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Do not allow any foreign matter or water to enter the inverter with converter assembly.


    1. *a Push
      *b Lock

      Raise the lever while pushing the lock on the lever, and disconnect the ECM connector.

      Note

      After disconnecting the ECM connector, make sure that dirt, water or other foreign matter does not contact the connecting parts of the ECM connector.

    2. Remove the No. 1 relay block cover.

    3. Disconnect the 3 No. 1 engine room relay block and No. 1 junction block assembly connectors.

    4. Disengage the 2 claws.

    5. Move the 2 lock levers as shown in the illustration and disconnect the 2 inverter with converter assembly connectors.

      Note


      • Do not damage the terminals, connector housing or inverter with converter assembly during disconnection.

      • Cover the hole where the cable was connected with tape (non-residue type) or equivalent to prevent entry of foreign matter.

      • Do not allow any foreign matter or water to enter the inverter with converter assembly.

      • Insulate the disconnected terminals with insulating tape.

      • Do not touch the waterproof seal or terminals of the connector.

    6. Remove the 3 bolts.

    7. Disengage the 4 clamps and disconnect the engine wire.

  22. SEPARATE INVERTER WITH CONVERTER ASSEMBLY

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. Remove the 5 bolts and 2 nuts and separate the inverter with converter assembly.

      Bolt Nut

      Note


      • To prevent damage due to static electricity, do not touch the terminals of the inverter with converter assembly connector.

      • Make sure to seal the inverter with converter assembly with the connector cover assembly or tape (non-residue type) etc., to prevent entry of foreign matter and water.

    2. *a Rope

      Using a rope or equivalent, secure the inverter with converter assembly with the hood hinge.

      Note


      • Be careful not to damage the surrounding components when securing the inverter with converter assembly.

      • To prevent damage to the inverter with converter assembly, do not hold the coolant pipe, bracket or connector when securing the inverter with converter assembly.

      • To prevent damage due to static electricity, do not touch the terminals of the inverter with converter assembly connector.

      • Make sure to seal the inverter with converter assembly with the connector cover assembly or tape (non-residue type) etc., to prevent entry of foreign matter and water.

  24. REMOVE EGR VALVE ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the EGR valve assembly connector.

    2. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 6 water by-pass hose from the EGR valve assembly.

    3. Slide the clip and disconnect the water by-pass hose from the EGR valve assembly.

    4. Remove the 3 nuts.

    5. Using an E8 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the stud bolt from the camshaft housing sub-assembly.

      Tech Tips

      If a stud bolt is deformed or the threads are damaged, replace it.

    6. Remove the EGR valve assembly from the EGR pipe with cooler sub-assembly.

    7. Remove the EGR valve gasket from the EGR valve assembly.
