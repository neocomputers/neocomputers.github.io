GRILLE SHUTTER SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / (desired system) / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Grill Shutter > Trouble Codes
      Powertrain > Engine > Trouble Codes

    5. Check the details of the DTC(s).

      Click here

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / (desired system) / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Grill Shutter > Clear DTCs
      Powertrain > Engine > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
