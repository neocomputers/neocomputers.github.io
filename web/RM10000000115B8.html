SOC INDICATOR INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT INDICATOR LIGHT ASSEMBLY


    1. Inspect illumination


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Indicator Light Assembly)

        Apply auxiliary battery voltage between the terminals of the switch, and check the illumination condition of the indicator light assembly.

        OK
        Measurement Condition Specified Condition

        Auxiliary Battery positive (+) → 3 (INL1)

        Auxiliary Battery negative (-) → 5 (LP)

        		 Illuminates

        Auxiliary Battery positive (+) → 2 (INL2)

        Auxiliary Battery negative (-) → 5 (LP)

        		 Illuminates

        Auxiliary Battery positive (+) → 1 (INL3)

        Auxiliary Battery negative (-) → 5 (LP)

        		 Illuminates

        If the result is not as specified, replace the indicator light assembly.
