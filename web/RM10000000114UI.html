FRONT CRANKSHAFT OIL SEAL INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL TIMING CHAIN COVER OIL SEAL


    1. Apply MP grease to the lip of a new timing chain cover oil seal.

      Note

      Keep the lip free from foreign matter.

    2. *a Tap in Depth

      Using SST and a hammer, tap in the timing chain cover oil seal until its surface is flush with the timing chain cover assembly edge.

      SST
      09223-22010
      Timing Chain Oil Seal Tap in Depth
      -1.0 to 1.0 mm (-0.0394 to 0.0394 in.)

      Note


      • Keep the lip free from foreign matter.

      • Do not tap in the timing chain cover oil seal at an angle.

  2. INSTALL CRANKSHAFT PULLEY


    1. Align the crankshaft timing gear key with the crankshaft pulley set key groove of the crankshaft pulley.

    2. *a Hold
      *b Turn

      Using SST to hold the crankshaft pulley in place, tighten the crankshaft pulley set bolt.

      SST
      09213-58014   ( 91551-80840 )
      09330-00021
      Torque:
      190 N*m  { 1937 kgf*cm, 140 ft.*lbf }
