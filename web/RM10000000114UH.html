  33. REMOVE CHAIN SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. *a Loosen the chain sub-assembly
      *b Place the chain sub-assembly on the non-toothed portion of the camshaft timing gear assembly

      Using a wrench to hold the hexagonal portion of the camshaft, turn the camshaft timing gear assembly counterclockwise to loosen the chain sub-assembly between the camshaft timing sprocket and camshaft timing gear assembly.

    2. With the chain sub-assembly loosened, separate the chain sub-assembly from the teeth of the camshaft timing gear assembly and place it on the non-toothed portion of the camshaft timing gear assembly.

    3. Turn the camshaft timing gear assembly clockwise to return it to its original position and remove the chain sub-assembly.
