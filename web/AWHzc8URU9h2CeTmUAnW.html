HV RELAY ASSEMBLY INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT NO. 1 HV BATTERY JUNCTION BLOCK ASSEMBLY


    1. Inspect SMRG:


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        *a

        Component without harness connected

        (No. 1 HV Battery Junction Block Assembly)

        		 - -
        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        i1-1 (-) - S2-1 (CEI) Auxiliary battery voltage not applied between terminals t8-3 (SMRG) and t8-2 (GND) 10 kΩ or higher
        Auxiliary battery voltage applied between terminals t8-3 (SMRG) and t8-2 (GND) Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 1 HV battery junction block assembly.

      2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        t8-3 (SMRG) - t8-2 (GND) -40 to 80°C (-40 to 176°F) 20.6 to 40.8 Ω
