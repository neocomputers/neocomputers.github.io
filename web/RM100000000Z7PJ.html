WINDOW DEFOGGER SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. CHECK WINDOW DEFOGGER SYSTEM


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    2. Check that the rear window defogger wire becomes warm by operating the rear window defogger switch of the air conditioning control assembly.

    3. When the vehicle is stopped, confirm that the window defogger system operation stops after approximately 15 minutes.

      Note

      If the auxiliary battery voltage is low, the window defogger system may not operate due to operation of auxiliary battery load control, check the Data List item "Battery Control Count (Body ECU)".
