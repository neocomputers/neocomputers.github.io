  25. REMOVE SOLAR ENERGY CONTROL UNIT

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. *a No. 2 Solar Energy Control Bracket

      Disconnect the 2 solar energy control unit connectors in the order shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the clamp and remove the floor wire from the No. 2 solar energy control bracket.

    3. Insert a screwdriver into the solar energy control unit high-voltage connector as shown in the illustration to disengage each lock and disconnect each high-voltage connector.

    4. Insulate each disconnected 2 high-voltage connectors with insulating tape. Wrap the connector from the wire harness side to the end of the connector.

    5. Remove the 3 bolts to remove the solar energy control unit.
