  2. CHECK CERTIFICATION ECU (SMART KEY ECU ASSEMBLY)


    1. Disconnect the I1 certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) connector.

    2. Measure the voltage and resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Terminal No. (Symbol) Wiring Color Terminal Description Condition Specified Condition
      I1-4 (+B) - Body ground GR - Body ground Auxiliary battery power supply Power switch off 11 to 14 V
      I1-18 (E) - Body ground W-B - Body ground Ground Always Below 1 Ω

    3. Reconnect the I1 certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) connector.

    4. Measure the voltage and check for pulses according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Terminal No. (Symbol) Wiring Color Terminal Description Condition Specified Condition
      N54-18 (RCO) - I1-18 (E) G - W-B

      Output to electical key and tire pressure monitoring system receiver assembly

      (Power supply for electical key and tire pressure monitoring system receiver assembly. Certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) outputs 5 V when receiver starts operating.)

        Procedure:

      1. Turn power switch off

      2. Bring electrical key transmitter sub-assembly outside detection area but within wireless function operational area

      3. Press lock or unlock switch of electrical key transmitter sub-assembly

      		 Below 1 V → 4.5 to 5.5 V
      N54-19 (RDAM) - I1-18 (E) P - W-B Electical key and tire pressure monitoring system receiver assembly communication circuit

        Procedure:

      1. Turn power switch off

      2. All doors locked

      3. Bring electrical key transmitter sub-assembly outside detection area but within wireless function operational area

      4. Press lock or unlock switch of electrical key transmitter sub-assembly

      		 11 to 14 pulse generation at regular intervals → Pulse generation
