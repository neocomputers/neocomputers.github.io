AUTOMATIC HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVEL CONTROL SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B2417, B2418

DTC Code DTC Name
B2417 Headlight Beam Level Control Motor LH
B2418 Headlight Beam Level Control Motor RH

DESCRIPTION

The headlight leveling motor receives a signal from the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly to operate. The No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly receives a signal indicating the operating conditions of the headlight leveling motor. If either headlight leveling motor malfunctions, the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH stores DTC B2417 and B2418.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Note
B2417 Headlight Beam Level Control Motor LH

  • Malfunction in headlight leveling motor LH

  • Open or short in headlight leveling motor LH circuit

  • Headlight unit LH

  • Headlight unit assembly LH

  • No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH

 w/ Adaptive High Beam System
B2418 Headlight Beam Level Control Motor RH

  • Malfunction in headlight leveling motor RH

  • Open or short in headlight leveling motor RH circuit

  • Headlight unit RH

  • Headlight unit assembly RH

  • No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly RH

 w/ Adaptive High Beam System

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

If the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH has been replaced, it is necessary to synchronize the vehicle information and initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.
