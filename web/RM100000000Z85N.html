NAVIGATION SYSTEM(for Radio and Display Type), Diagnostic DTC:B158F

DTC Code DTC Name
B158F AV Signal Stoppage (Low Battery Voltage)

DESCRIPTION

This DTC is stored when a video or audio signal is interrupted due to auxiliary battery voltage input to the radio and display receiver assembly dropping temporarily.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B158F AV Signal Stoppage (Low Battery Voltage) A video or audio signal is interrupted when the auxiliary battery voltage drops

  • Radio and display receiver assembly

  • Harness or connector

WIRING DIAGRAM

*a from Auxiliary Battery
*b RADIO
*c Radio and Display Receiver Assembly
