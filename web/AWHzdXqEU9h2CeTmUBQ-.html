SEAT BELT WARNING SYSTEM(w/o Occupant Classification System) SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


  1. SEAT BELT WARNING SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


    1. Seat belt warning light operation for driver seat belt:

      The seat belt warning light on the combination meter assembly blinks or turns off in accordance with the driver seat belt state and vehicle speed.

    2. Seat belt warning light operation for front passenger seat belt:

      The seat belt warning light on the combination meter assembly blinks or turns off in accordance with whether the front passenger seat is occupied, the front passenger seat belt state and vehicle speed.

    3. Rear seat belt warning light:

      The rear seat belt warning lights on the radio receiver assembly illuminate or turn off in accordance with the rear door state, vehicle speed and rear seat belt state.

    4. Seat belt warning buzzer:

      When the vehicle is driven at 20 km/h (12 mph) or more with the front passenger seat occupied and the driver, front passenger or a rear seat belt not fastened, the seat belt warning buzzer in the combination meter assembly will sound at an interval of 1.2 seconds for 30 seconds.

      After that, the seat belt warning buzzer in the combination meter assembly will sound at an interval of 0.4 seconds for 90 seconds.

  2. FUNCTION OF MAIN COMPONENTS

    Component Function
    Front seat inner belt assembly (driver seat) Informs the main body ECU (multiplex network body ECU) of the driver seat belt condition (fastened or unfastened).
    Front seat inner belt assembly (front passenger seat) Informs the combination meter assembly of the front passenger seat belt condition (fastened or unfastened).
    Occupant detection sensor (separate type front seat cushion pad (front passenger seat)) Informs the combination meter assembly of the front passenger seat condition (occupied or unoccupied).
    Combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate)

    • Blinks or turns off the seat belt warning light.

    • Illuminates or turns off the rear seat belt warning light.

    • Sounds the seat belt warning buzzer.

    • Rear seat 3 point type belt assembly LH

    • Rear seat 3 point type belt assembly RH

    		 Informs the combination meter assembly of each rear seat belt condition (fastened or unfastened).
    Rear door courtesy light switch assembly (LH/RH) Informs the main body ECU (multiplex network body ECU) of the front door condition (open or closed).
    Main body ECU (multiplex network body ECU) Sends the driver seat belt buckle switch signal to the combination meter assembly via CAN communication.
    Skid control ECU (brake booster with master cylinder assembly) Sends the vehicle speed signal to the combination meter assembly via CAN communication.
    Hybrid vehicle control ECU Sends the shift position signal to the combination meter assembly via CAN communication.
