OIL PRESSURE SWITCH INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL ENGINE OIL PRESSURE SWITCH ASSEMBLY


    1. *a Adhesive
      *b Oil Inlet Port

      Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the engine oil pressure switch assembly.

      Adhesive
      Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1344, Three Bond 1344 or equivalent

      Note


      • Do not apply adhesive to the oil inlet port of the engine oil pressure switch assembly.

      • Install the engine oil pressure switch assembly within 3 minutes of applying adhesive.

    2. Using a 24 mm deep socket wrench, install the engine oil pressure switch assembly.

      Torque:
      15 N*m  { 153 kgf*cm, 11 ft.*lbf }

      Note

      Do not start the engine for at least 1 hour after installation.

    3. Connect the engine oil pressure switch assembly connector.

  2. INSPECT FOR OIL LEAK
