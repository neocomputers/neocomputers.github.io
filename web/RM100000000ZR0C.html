LIN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Main Body or Entry&Start / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Main Body > Trouble Codes
      Body Electrical > Entry&Start > Trouble Codes

    5. Check the details of the DTCs.

      Click here

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Main Body or Entry&Start / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Main Body > Clear DTCs
      Body Electrical > Entry&Start > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
