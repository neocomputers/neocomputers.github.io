PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER

    Click here

  2. REMOVE FRONT CENTER FLOOR BRACE


    1. Remove the 4 bolts and front center floor brace.

  3. REMOVE HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR

    CAUTION:

    To prevent burns, do not touch the engine, exhaust pipe or other high temperature components while the engine is hot.


    1. *1 Wire Harness Clamp Bracket

      Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

    2. Remove the wire harness clamp.

    3. Remove the wire harness clamp bracket from the heated oxygen sensor.

    4. Using SST, remove the heated oxygen sensor from the front exhaust pipe assembly (TWC: Rear Catalyst).

      SST
      09224-00012

      Note

      If the heated oxygen sensor has been struck or dropped, replace it.
