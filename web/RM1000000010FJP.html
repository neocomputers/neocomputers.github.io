  29. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT COVER


    1. Insert Moulding Remover Here
      Remove in this Direction

      Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 claws as shown in the illustration.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws to remove the rear combination light cover as shown in the illustration.

  30. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape around the rear combination light assembly as shown in the illustration.

    3. Remove the 2 screws.

    4. *a Pin
      *b Guide
      Remove in this Direction

      Pull the rear combination light assembly toward the rear of the vehicle as shown in the illustration to disengage the pin and 2 guides and separate the rear combination light assembly.

      Note

      To prevent the rear combination light assembly from falling when disengaging the pins and guides, lightly hold the rear combination light assembly.

    5. Disengage the grommet to remove the rear combination light assembly.
