AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B14AC

DTC Code DTC Name
B14AC High Pressure Magnetic Valve Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The high pressure magnetic valve is installed to the accumulator and accessory assembly and is open when the power switch is off. When the power switch is on (IG), the high pressure magnetic valve opens or closes according to the heat pump air-conditioning control. When the heat pump air-conditioning control is in cooling/series heating/heating/gas injection heating mode, the high pressure magnetic valve is closed based on the signal from the heat pump ECU assembly. When the heat pump air-conditioning control is in parallel heating mode, the high pressure magnetic valve is open.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Memory
B14AC High Pressure Magnetic Valve Circuit Open or short in high pressure magnetic valve circuit

  • Heat pump ECU assembly

  • High pressure magnetic valve

  • Harness or connector

Memorized

(4 sec. or more)*

  • *: The heat pump ECU assembly stores this DTC if the malfunction has occurred for the period of time indicated in the brackets.
