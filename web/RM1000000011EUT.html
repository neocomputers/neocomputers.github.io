LIGHTING SYSTEM SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


  1. ILLUMINATED ENTRY SYSTEM


    1. The illuminated entry system has the following control functions:

      Control Outline Lights that Operate
      Actuation Area-linked When a registered key is brought within any vehicle exterior detection area around the doors, the items listed to the right turn on.

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
      Door Lock/Unlock-linked When any door is unlocked, the lights that operate turn on.

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
      When all doors are locked, the lights that operate turn off.
      Door Open/Close-linked When any door is opened, the lights that operate turn on.

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
      When all doors are closed, the lights that operate turn off.
      Power Switch-linked When the power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off, the lights that operate turn on.

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
      Delay

      When approximately 15 seconds elapse after any of the following conditions is met, the lights that operate turn off:


      • An actuation area-linked function is used.

      • The door unlock-linked function is active.

      • Any door is opened and then all doors are closed when the power switch is off.

      • The power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off when all doors are closed.

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*

      When approximately 40 seconds elapse after any of the following conditions is met, the lights that operate turn off:


      • Any door is opened and then all doors are closed when the power switch is off.

      • The power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off when all doors are closed.

      		 Power Switch Illumination

      • *: w/ Footwell Light

  2. BATTERY SAVING CONTROL SYSTEM


    1. The battery saving function has the following control functions:

      Control Condition Lights that Operate
      Basic operation

      When 20 minutes elapse after the power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off and the lights that operate remain on, the lights are turned off.

      Tech Tips

      Battery saving control is canceled when any of the following operations is performed before the lights are turned off by battery saving control. After the operation is performed, the lights remain on for another 20 minutes and then the lights are turned off.


      • The power switch is turned on (ACC) or on (IG).

      • Any door is opened and closed.

      • The luggage compartment door is opened and closed.

      • Any door is unlocked.

      • The key enters any exterior actuation area.

      • Power Switch Illumination

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly
      Interior light auto cut operation

      When 20 minutes elapse after the power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off and the lights that operate remain on, the lights are turned off.

      Tech Tips

      Battery saving control is canceled when any of the following operations is performed before the lights are turned off by battery saving control. After the operation is performed, the lights remain on for another 20 minutes and then the lights are turned off.


      • The power switch is turned on (ACC) or on (IG).

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • Vanity Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Luggage Compartment Light Assembly

      • Courtesy Light Assembly
