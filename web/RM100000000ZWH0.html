OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR GLASS REMOVAL

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips


  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  • The following procedure is for the LH side.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE OUTER MIRROR


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape to the areas shown in the illustration.

    2. Push the upper part of the mirror surface and tilt it.

    3. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 2 claws on the lower part of the outer mirror as shown in the illustration.

      Note

      Do not push the outer mirror with excessive force. Doing so may cause the actuator to come off or break the mirror surface.

    4. Push the lower part of the mirror surface and tilt it.

    5. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 2 claws on the upper part of the outer mirror as shown in the illustration.

      Note

      Do not push the outer mirror with excessive force. Doing so may cause the actuator to come off or break the mirror surface.

    6. w/o Blind Spot Monitor System:


      1. Disconnect the connector to remove the outer mirror.

    7. w/ Blind Spot Monitor System:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Push in this Direction

        Disengage the claw as shown in the illustration.

      3. Disengage the clamp.

      4. Disconnect the connector to remove the outer mirror.
