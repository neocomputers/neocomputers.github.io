ROAD SIGN ASSIST SYSTEM SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


  1. ROAD SIGN ASSIST SYSTEM DESCRIPTION


    1. The road sign assist system uses the forward recognition camera to recognize traffic signs and provides information to the driver via the multi-information display.

  2. ROAD SIGN ASSIST SYSTEM CONTROL


    1. Control Pattern 1:

      *a Multi-information Display *b Traffic Sign
      *c Distance - -

      As soon as the vehicle passes a traffic sign recognized by the forward recognition camera, the sign is displayed on the multi-information display.

      If another traffic sign is not recognized after a certain distance, the sign displayed on the multi-information display turns off.

    2. Control Pattern 2:

      *a Power Switch Off *b Power Switch On (IG)
      *c Multi-information Display - -

      If the power switch is turned off while a traffic sign is displayed on the multi-information display, the same sign is displayed on the multi-information display when the power switch is next turned on.

    3. Control Pattern 3:

      *a Multi-information Display *b Traffic Sign

      When the vehicle changes onto another road after turning right or left, the sign displayed on the multi-information display for the road that the vehicle was being driven on turns off.
