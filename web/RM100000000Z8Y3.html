FRONT PASSENGER AIRBAG ASSEMBLY INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE


    1. *a Airbag Connector
      *b Airbag Connector Lock

      Connect the 2 airbag connectors of the No. 2 instrument panel wire to the instrument panel passenger airbag assembly.

      Note


      • When connecting any airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.

      • Be sure to only connect the connectors to each corresponding color.

    2. Push in the 2 airbag connector locks to install the 2 airbag connectors to the instrument panel passenger airbag assembly.

    3. Engage the clamp.

  2. INSTALL INSTRUMENT PANEL PASSENGER AIRBAG ASSEMBLY


    1. Engage the 3 hooks.

    2. Push in the instrument panel passenger airbag assembly to engage the 3 hooks.

    3. Install the instrument panel passenger airbag assembly with the 2 screws.

  3. INSTALL LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  4. CONNECT NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE


    1. Check that the power switch is off.

    2. Check that the cable is disconnected from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal.

      CAUTION:

      Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    3. Connect the connector.

      Note

      When connecting any airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.
