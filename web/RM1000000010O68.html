REAR BRAKE DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE REAR DISC BRAKE PISTON


    1. Using SST, remove the rear disc brake piston by turning it counterclockwise.

      SST
      09719-12010   ( 09719-01030 )

      Note


      • Do not damage the rear disc brake piston.

      • Do not allow any brake fluid to spatter.

  2. REMOVE CYLINDER BOOT


    1. Remove the cylinder boot from the rear disc brake cylinder.

  3. REMOVE PISTON SEAL


    1. *a Protective Tape

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, remove the piston seal from the rear disc brake cylinder.

      Note

      Do not damage the inner surface or piston seal groove of the rear disc brake cylinder.

  4. REMOVE REAR DISC BRAKE BLEEDER PLUG CAP

  5. REMOVE REAR DISC BRAKE BLEEDER PLUG
