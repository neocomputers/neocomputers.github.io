  2. INSTALL REAR DOOR UPPER OUTSIDE STRIPE


    1. Refer to the illustration to position a new rear door upper outside stripe.

      *A LH Side *B RH Side
      *a Point A *b Point B
      *c Edge of Curved Surface *d Straight
