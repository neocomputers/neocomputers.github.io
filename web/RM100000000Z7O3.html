LUMBAR SWITCH(for Manual Seat) INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT LUMBAR POWER SEAT SWITCH (DRIVER SEAT)


    1. *A for LHD
      *B for RHD
      *a Front Lumbar Power Seat (Release)
      *b Front Lumbar Power Seat (Hold)
      *c

      Component without harness connected

      (Front Lumbar Power Seat Switch (Driver Seat))

      Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      5 - 2 Front lumbar power seat (Hold) pressed Below 1 Ω
      3 - 4
      5 - 4 Off
      3 - 4
      3 - 2 Front lumbar power seat (Release) pressed
      5 - 4

      If the result is not as specified, replace the front lumbar power seat switch (driver seat).
