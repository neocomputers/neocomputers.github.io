  7. REMOVE HIGH PRESSURE MAGNETIC VALVE


    1. Remove the bolt and high pressure magnetic valve with connector tube.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the valve to connector tube.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

    3. Using a 5 mm hexagon socket wrench, remove the hexagon bolt and high pressure magnetic valve.

    4. Remove the O-ring from the valve to connector tube.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.
