AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B14B6

DTC Code DTC Name
B14B6 Lost Communication with Heat Pump ECU LIN

DESCRIPTION

A signal indicating the heat pump operation cycle status is sent from the heat pump ECU assembly to the air conditioning amplifier assembly and a signal indicating the vehicle condition is sent from the air conditioning amplifier assembly to the heat pump ECU assembly via LIN communication in order to optimally control the system.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Memory
B14B6 Lost Communication with Heat Pump ECU LIN Lost Communication with heat pump ECU assembly

  • Air conditioning amplifier assembly

  • Harness or connector

  • Heat pump ECU assembly

Memorized

(10 sec. or more)*

  • *: The air conditioning amplifier assembly stores this DTC if the malfunction has occurred for the period of time indicated in the brackets.
