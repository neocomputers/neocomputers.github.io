STEERING PAD SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT STEERING PAD SWITCH ASSEMBLY (Type A)


    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Steering Pad Switch Assembly)

      		 *b Seek+
      *c Seek- *d Volume+
      *e Volume- *f MODE
      *g Voice *h Off Hook
      *i On Hook *j Up
      *k Down *l Left
      *m Right *n Enter
      *o Back *p TRIP
      *q Steering Wheel Heater - -
      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      A-11 - A-8 No switch pushed 95 to 105 kΩ
      Seek+ switch pushed Below 2.5 Ω
      Seek- switch pushed 313 to 345 Ω
      Volume+ switch pushed 950 to 1050 Ω
      Volume- switch pushed 2955 to 3265 Ω
      A-10 - A-8 No switch pushed 95 to 105 kΩ
      MODE switch pushed Below 2.5 Ω
      On hook switch pushed 313 to 345 Ω
      Off hook switch pushed 950 to 1050 Ω
      Voice switch pushed 2955 to 3265 Ω
      A-3 - A-8 No switch pushed 95 to 105 kΩ
      Left switch pushed Below 2.5 Ω
      Up switch pushed 313 to 345 Ω
      Down switch pushed 950 to 1050 Ω
      Right switch pushed 2955 to 3265 Ω
      A-2 - A-8 No switch pushed 95 to 105 kΩ
      Enter switch pushed Below 2.5 Ω
      TRIP switch pushed 313 to 345 Ω
      Back switch pushed 950 to 1050 Ω
      A-6 - A-12 No switch pushed 1 MΩ or higher
      Steering wheel heater switch pushed Below 2.5 Ω
      A-1 - B-1 Always Below 2.5 Ω

      Tech Tips

      If the result is not as specified, replace the steering pad switch assembly.

    2. Check the illumination.

      *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Steering Pad Switch Assembly)

      		 - -

      1. Connect a positive (+) lead from the auxiliary battery to terminal 5 and a negative (-) lead to terminal 9 of the steering pad switch assembly connector.

      2. Check that the steering pad switch assembly illumination illuminates.

        OK
        The steering pad switch assembly illumination illuminates.

        Tech Tips

        If the result is not as specified, replace the steering pad switch assembly.
