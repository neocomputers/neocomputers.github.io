FORWARD RECOGNITION CAMERA SYSTEM DIAGNOSIS SYSTEM


  1. DIAGNOSIS FUNCTION


    1. If a malfunction is detected in the forward recognition camera, the forward recognition camera stores a DTC without directly informing the driver via indicator lights, and then notifies the systems that use the forward recognition camera.

      Those systems then notify the driver.
