REAR TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT REAR TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH

    *a

    Component without harness connected

    (Rear Turn Signal Light Assembly LH)

    1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the rear turn signal light assembly LH and check that the light illuminates.

      OK
      Condition Specified Condition

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

      		 Turn signal light illuminates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear turn signal light assembly LH.

  2. INSPECT REAR TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

    *a

    Component without harness connected

    (Rear Turn Signal Light Assembly RH)

    1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the rear turn signal light assembly RH and check that the light illuminates.

      OK
      Condition Specified Condition

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

      		 Turn signal light illuminates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear turn signal light assembly RH.

  3. INSPECT REAR FOG LIGHT LED (w/ Rear Fog Light)

    *a

    Component without harness connected

    (Rear Fog Light LED)

    1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the rear fog light LED and check that the light illuminates.

      OK
      Condition Specified Condition

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

      		 Fog light illuminates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear fog light LED.
