  • Steering Angle Setting


    1. Check that the steering wheel is centered (approximately +/- 5 degrees or less) and then select "STEERING CENTER MEMORIZE".

      Tech Tips

      When performing removal and installation, or replacement of the rear television camera assembly, it is not necessary to perform the steering angle setting.

    2. After the centered steering position is memorized, turn the steering wheel to the left full lock position and then to the right full lock position.

      Tech Tips

      It is also possible to turn the steering wheel to the right full lock position first.

    3. Select "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" to store the steering angle adjustment value and change the screen to the "Back Camera Position Setting" screen.

      Tech Tips


      • "Next" cannot be selected until the steering center and maximum steering wheel angle have been stored.

      • When "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" is selected, a beep will sound to confirm that the steering angle adjustment values have been stored.

      • The adjustment values will not be stored unless "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" is selected after turning the steering wheel from lock to lock.

      • When "Back" is selected, the screen changes to the "Car Signal Check" screen without storing any values.

      • If the Steering Angle Settings have not been adjusted, the adjustment values may not be stored when "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" is selected.

      • If the steering sensor is malfunctioning, the adjustment values may not be stored when "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" is selected, even if no DTCs are detected.

      • If the adjustment values are not stored when "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" is selected after performing the steering angle setting procedure, replace the steering sensor.

        Click here

    Back Camera Position Setting

    Tech Tips


    • When the back door is open, the "Back door is open. Do not use the rear view monitor when the back door is not completely closed." message will be displayed and it will not be possible to perform back camera position setting.

    • If the "Back door is open. Do not use the rear view monitor when the back door is not completely closed." message is displayed even when the back door is closed, perform inspections according to Problem Symptoms Table (When adjusting the camera optical axis, "Back door is open. Do not use the rear view monitor when the back door is not completely closed." is displayed even after the back door has been closed).

      Click here


    1. Perform vertical and horizontal position adjustment.


      • Move the circle (A) left, right, up and down by selecting the buttons (C), (D), (E) and (F) so that the left end of Target Bar for Back Camera Position Setting is positioned within the center of the circle (A) (center of the inner red circle).

    2. Perform roll angle adjustment.


      • Rotate the bar (B) by selecting the buttons (G) or (H) so that the bar (B) becomes parallel to Target Bar for Back Camera Position Setting.

    3. Select "Next" on the "Back Camera Position Setting" screen.

  • Back Verify Mode


    1. Check that (A) and the target adjustment bar are overlapping.


      • If the lines are not aligned, perform the "STEERING CENTER MEMORIZE" and "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" operations.

      • If (A) and the target adjustment bar are not aligned even if the tires are aligned straight ahead, perform the back camera position setting operation.

    2. Select "OK" to return to the "Function Check/Setting I" screen and complete the adjustment.

      Tech Tips


      • The update is not completed until "OK" is selected.

      • When "OK" is selected, a beep will sound to confirm that the adjustment values have been stored.

      • The adjustment values are not stored until the beep has sounded.

  • Finish diagnostic mode.


    • for Navigation Receiver Type: Click here

    • for Radio and Display Type: Click here

  • Confirm steering angle adjustment.

    Tech Tips

    If the steering angle has been adjusted, confirm the steering angle adjustment on the parking assist monitor screen after finishing diagnostic mode.


    1. Check that the estimated course lines on the parking assist screen move until the steering wheel is fully turned to either the left or right.

      Tech Tips

      If the estimated course line stops moving before the steering wheel is fully turned to either the left or right, the steering angle adjustment values have not been stored correctly. In this case, perform "STEERING CENTER MEMORIZE" and "MAX STEERING ANGLE MEMORIZE" again.
