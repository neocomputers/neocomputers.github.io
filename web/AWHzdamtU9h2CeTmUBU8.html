AIR CONDITIONING PRESSURE SENSOR INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL AIR CONDITIONER PRESSURE SENSOR


    1. Remove the vinyl tape from the discharge pipe sub-assembly.

    2. Sufficiently apply compressor oil to a new air conditioner pressure sensor and the fitting surface of the air conditioner pressure sensor.

      Compressor Oil
      ND-OIL 11 or equivalent
    3. *a Turn
      *b Hold

      Using a 27 mm deep socket wrench, install the air conditioner pressure sensor as shown in the illustration.

      Torque:
      10.8 N*m  { 110 kgf*cm, 8 ft.*lbf }

    4. Connect the connector.
