WATER PUMP WITH MOTOR REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. DRAIN COOLANT (for Inverter)

    Click here

  3. REMOVE INVERTER WATER PUMP ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the inverter water pump assembly connector.

      Note

      To prevent coolant from entering the inverter water pump assembly connector, perform the following procedure.

      Apply insulating tape to the connectors of the wire harness and inverter water pump assembly.

    2. Remove the 2 bolts.

    3. *1 Outlet No. 1 Hybrid Water Pump Hose
      *2 Inlet Hybrid Water Pump Hose

      Slide the 2 clips and disconnect the outlet No. 1 hybrid water pump hose and inlet hybrid water pump hose to remove the inverter water pump assembly.

      Note


      • Put pieces of cloth into the pipes and disconnected hoses or cover the pipes and hoses with plastic bags to prevent foreign matter from entering the inverter water pump assembly and inverter cooling system.

      • Do not apply excessive force to the hoses.
