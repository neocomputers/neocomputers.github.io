PROCEDURE


  1. PRECAUTION

    CAUTION:

    Be sure to read Precaution thoroughly before servicing.

    Click here

    Note

    After turning the power switch off, waiting time may be required before disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal. Therefore, make sure to read the disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal notices before proceeding with work.

    Click here

  2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE AUXILIARY BATTERY TERMINAL

    CAUTION:

    Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    Note

    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected.

    Click here

  3. REMOVE FRONT DOOR INSIDE HANDLE BEZEL PLUG


    1. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 3 claws to remove the front door inside handle bezel plug.

  4. REMOVE FRONT ARMREST ASSEMBLY


    1. Place Hand Here
      Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 3 clips and 7 claws as shown in the illustration.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the guide to remove the front armrest assembly as shown in the illustration.

  5. REMOVE MULTIPLEX NETWORK MASTER SWITCH ASSEMBLY WITH FRONT DOOR UPPER ARMREST BASE PANEL (for Driver Side)


    1. Place Hand Here
      Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 clips and 4 claws as shown in the illustration.

    2. Disconnect the connector to remove the multiplex network master switch assembly with front door upper armrest base panel.

  6. REMOVE POWER WINDOW REGULATOR SWITCH ASSEMBLY WITH FRONT DOOR UPPER ARMREST BASE PANEL (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. Place Hand Here
      Remove in this Direction

    2. Disconnect the connector to remove the power window regulator switch assembly with front door upper armrest base panel.

  8. REMOVE FRONT DOOR TRIM BOARD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape to the front door panel as shown in the illustration.

    2. Remove the 3 screws.

    3. Disengage the 2 clips.

    4. Using a clip remover, disengage the 10 clips.

    5. While pulling out the front door trim board sub-assembly, insert a hand between the front door trim board sub-assembly and front door panel up to the area where the claws are engaged.

    6. Place Hand Here

      While pulling down the part of the front door inner glass weatherstrip shown in the illustration, pull out the front door trim board sub-assembly to disengage the 2 claws.

    7. Using a clip remover, disengage the clip.

    8. Engage the 2 claws.

    9. Pull out the front door trim board sub-assembly to disengage the guide from the front door panel.

    10. Lift the front door trim board sub-assembly to separate the front door trim board sub-assembly with the front door inner glass weatherstrip from the front door panel.

      Note

      To prevent damage to the guide, lift the front door trim board sub-assembly with the guide disengaged.

    11. *1 Front Door Inside Locking Cable Assembly
      *2 Front Door Lock Remote Control Cable Assembly
      Remove in this Direction

      Disconnect the front door lock remote control cable assembly and front door inside locking cable assembly to remove the front door trim board sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.

  10. REMOVE FRONT DOOR INNER GLASS WEATHERSTRIP


    1. Remove in this Direction

  18. REMOVE FRONT DOOR GLASS SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the hole plug.

    2. for Driver Side:


      1. Connect the multiplex network master switch assembly.

    3. for Front Passenger Side:


      1. Connect the power window regulator switch assembly.

    4. Connect the cable to the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal.

    5. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    6. Move the front door glass sub-assembly so that the door glass bolts can be seen.

    7. Turn the power switch off.

    8. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal.

    9. for Driver Side:


      1. Disconnect the multiplex network master switch assembly.

    10. for Front Passenger Side:


      1. Disconnect the power window regulator switch assembly.

    11. Remove the 2 bolts.

      Note

      After the bolts are removed, do not allow the front door glass sub-assembly to fall.

    12. Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

      Remove the front door glass sub-assembly as indicated by the arrows, in the order shown in the illustration.

      Note

      Do not damage the front door glass sub-assembly.

  19. REMOVE FRONT DOOR WINDOW REGULATOR ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the front door No. 2 service hole cover.

    2. *a Temporary Bolt

      Disconnect the connector.

    3. Loosen the temporary bolt.

      Note

      Do not remove the temporary bolt. If the temporary bolt is removed, the front door window regulator assembly may fall and cause damage.

    4. Remove the 5 bolts.

    5. Remove the front door window regulator assembly.

    6. Remove the temporary bolt from the front door window regulator assembly.

  20. REMOVE FRONT DOOR GLASS RUN


    1. Remove the front door glass run.
