VEHICLE PROXIMITY NOTIFICATION SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. DTC CHECK


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Vehicle Proximity Notification System / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Vehicle Proximity Notification System > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  2. DTC CLEAR


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Vehicle Proximity Notification System / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Vehicle Proximity Notification System > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
