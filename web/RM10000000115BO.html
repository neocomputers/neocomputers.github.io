FRAME WIRE INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL HV BATTERY CHARGER WIRE (w/o Solar Charging System)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Engage the 3 clamps to install the HV battery charger wire.

    2. Connect the 2 electric vehicle charger assembly connectors and slide the green-colored lock as shown in the illustration to lock it securely.

      Note

      Make sure that the connectors are connected securely.

    3. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Engage the clamp.

    4. Connect the inlet AC charger cable connector and slide the green-colored lock as shown in the illustration to lock it securely.

      Note

      Make sure that the connector is connected securely.

    5. Engage the 2 clamps to connect the HV battery charger wire.

    6. Connect the HV battery junction block assembly connector.

      Note

      Make sure that the connector is connected securely.

    7. *a Earth Terminal

      Engage the clamp.

    8. Install the nut (B).

      Torque:
      8.0 N*m  { 82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf }

    9. Install the nut (A) to connect the earth terminal.

      Torque:
      8.0 N*m  { 82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf }

  2. INSTALL HV BATTERY CHARGER WIRE (w/ Solar Charging System)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Engage the 3 clamps to install the HV battery charger wire.

    2. Connect the 2 electric vehicle charger assembly connectors and slide the green-colored lock as shown in the illustration to lock it securely.

      Note

      Make sure that the connectors are connected securely.

    3. *a Green-colored Lock
      Connect in this direction.

      Connect the 2 solar energy control unit connectors as shown in the illustration and check that the green-colored lock of each connector is securely locked.

      Note

      Make sure that the connectors are connected securely.

    4. Engage the 2 clamps.

    5. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Engage the clamp.

    6. Connect the inlet AC charger cable connector and slide the green-colored lock as shown in the illustration to lock it securely.

      Note

      Make sure that the connector is connected securely.

    7. Engage the 2 clamps to connect the HV battery charger wire.

    8. Connect the HV battery junction block assembly connector.

      Note

      Make sure that the connector is connected securely.

    9. *a Earth Terminal

      Engage the clamp.

    10. Install the nut (B).

      Torque:
      8.0 N*m  { 82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf }

    11. Install the nut (A) to connect the earth terminal.

      Torque:
      8.0 N*m  { 82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf }

  3. INSTALL HV FLOOR UNDER WIRE

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. *A for LHD
      *B for RHD

      Engage the clamp (B) to install the HV floor under wire.

    2. Install a new clamp (A).

    3. Insert the HV floor under wire into the floor panel hole and engage the grommet.

    4. Install 3 new clamps in the order shown in the illustration.

      *a Front of Vehicle *b RH Side

    5. Install the 2 nuts.

      Torque:
      8.5 N*m  { 87 kgf*cm, 75 in.*lbf }

    6. Engage the 4 clamps.

    7. Engage the 2 claws to close the wire harness clamp.

    8. *a Earth Terminal

      Install the nut to connect the earth terminal.

      Torque:
      8.0 N*m  { 82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf }

    9. Connect the 2 HV battery junction block assembly connectors.

      Note

      Make sure that the connectors are connected securely.

  4. CONNECT HV BATTERY CHARGER WIRE

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. Connect the HV battery junction block assembly connector.

      Note

      Make sure that the connector is connected securely.

  5. CONNECT NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY


    1. Connect the No. 2 parking brake cable assembly with the 2 bolts.

      Torque:
      6.0 N*m  { 61 kgf*cm, 53 in.*lbf }

  38. CONNECT ENGINE WIRE (w/ Canister Pump Module)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Do not allow any foreign matter or water to enter the inverter with converter assembly.


    1. Connect the inverter with converter assembly connector and move the lock lever as shown in the illustration.

      Note


      • To prevent damage due to static electricity, do not touch the terminals of the disconnected connectors.

      • Do not damage the terminals, connector housing or inverter with converter assembly when connecting the connectors.

      • Do not touch the waterproof seal or terminals of the connector.

  39. CONNECT ENGINE WIRE (w/o Canister Pump Module)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Do not allow any foreign matter or water to enter the inverter with converter assembly.


    1. Engage the 2 clamps and connect the engine wire.

    2. Install the bolt.

      Torque:
      8.0 N*m  { 82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf }

    3. Connect the 2 inverter with converter assembly connectors and move each lock lever as shown in the illustration.

      Note


      • To prevent damage due to static electricity, do not touch the terminals of the disconnected connectors.

      • Do not damage the terminals, connector housing or inverter with converter assembly when connecting the connectors.

      • Do not touch the waterproof seal or terminals of the connector.
