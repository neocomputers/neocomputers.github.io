ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING INSTALLATION

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips


  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  • The following procedure is for the LH side.

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING CLIP (w/o Solar Charging System)

    Note

    When installing new roof drip side finish moulding clips, remove any double-sided tape remaining where the roof drip side finish moulding clips will be installed on the vehicle body and clean the vehicle body with a non-residue solvent.


    1. *a Adhesive
      *b 2 to 3 mm Bead of Adhesive

      Apply a 2 to 3 mm (0.0787 to 0.118 in.) bead of adhesive (3M DP-105 or equivalent) to each new roof drip side finish moulding clip.

      Tech Tips

      Adhesive strength (tensile strength): 13.7 MPa (140.0 kgf/cm2, 1987 psi) or more (when the temperature is 23°C (73°F).)

    2. Install the roof drip side finish moulding clips to the positions on the roof panel shown in the illustration. Determine the locations and firmly press and install the roof drip side finish moulding clips.

      Standard Measurement
      Area Measurement Area Measurement
      A 3.0 to 5.0 mm (0.118 to 0.197 in.) B 1.5 to 2.5 mm (0.0591 to 0.0984 in.)
      C 3.0 to 5.0 mm (0.118 to 0.197 in.) D 1.4 to 2.4 mm (0.0551 to 0.0945 in.)

    3. Install the roof drip side finish moulding when 40 minutes or more have elapsed since the roof drip side finish moulding clips were installed.

      Tech Tips


      • Initial hardening time: 40 minutes

      • Complete hardening time: 24 hours

  2. INSTALL CENTER ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING (w/o Solar Charging System)


    1. Engage the 2 clips to install the center roof drip side finish moulding as shown in the illustration.

      Standard Measurement
      Area Measurement Area Measurement
      A 3.0 mm (0.118 in.) B 3.9 mm (0.154 in.)

  3. INSTALL ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING CLIP (w/ Solar Charging System)

    Note

    When installing new roof drip side finish moulding clips, remove any double-sided tape remaining where the roof drip side finish moulding clips will be installed on the vehicle body and clean the vehicle body with a non-residue solvent.


    1. *a Adhesive
      *b 2 to 3 mm Bead of Adhesive

      Apply a 2 to 3 mm (0.0787 to 0.118 in.) bead of adhesive (3M DP-105 or equivalent) to each new roof drip side finish moulding clip.

      Tech Tips

      Adhesive strength (tensile strength): 13.7 MPa (140.0 kgf/cm2, 1987 psi) or more (when the temperature is 23°C (73°F).)

    2. Install the roof drip side finish moulding clips to the positions on the roof panel shown in the illustration. Determine the locations and firmly press and install the roof drip side finish moulding clips.

      Standard Measurement
      Area Measurement
      A 3.0 to 5.0 mm (0.118 to 0.197 in.)
      B 1.4 to 2.4 mm (0.0551 to 0.0945 in.)
      C 3.0 to 5.0 mm (0.118 to 0.197 in.)
      D 1.4 to 2.4 mm (0.0551 to 0.0945 in.)

    3. Install the roof drip side finish moulding when 40 minutes or more have elapsed since the roof drip side finish moulding clips were installed.

      Tech Tips


      • Initial hardening time: 40 minutes

      • Complete hardening time: 24 hours

  4. INSTALL CENTER ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING (w/ Solar Charging System)


    1. Engage the 2 clips to install the center roof drip side finish moulding as shown in the illustration.

      Standard Measurement
      Area Measurement
      A 5.1 mm (0.201 in.)
      B 3.9 mm (0.154 in.)
