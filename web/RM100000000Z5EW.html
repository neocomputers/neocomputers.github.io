WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK FOR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Rain and/or Humidity Sensor / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Rain and/or Humidity Sensor > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Rain and/or Humidity Sensor / Clear DTCs.


      Body Electrical > Rain and/or Humidity Sensor > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
