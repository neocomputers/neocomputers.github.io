ROOM LIGHT BULB REPLACEMENT

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 ROOM LIGHT BULB


    1. *a Protective Tape
      Front

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 4 claws to remove the No. 1 room light lens.

    2. Front
      Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

      Pull back the tab and remove the No. 1 room light bulb as indicated by the arrows, in the order shown in the illustration.

  2. INSTALL NO. 1 ROOM LIGHT BULB


    1. Front
      Install in this Direction (1)
      Install in this Direction (2)

      Pull back the tab and install the No. 1 room light bulb as indicated by the arrows, in the order shown in the illustration.

    2. Front

      Engage the 4 claws to install the No. 1 room light lens.
