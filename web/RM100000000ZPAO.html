UPPER INSTRUMENT PANEL DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE METER MIRROR SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Headup Display)

    Click here

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL PIN


    1. Remove the 4 screws <A> and 4 No. 1 instrument panel pins.

  3. REMOVE DEFROSTER NOZZLE ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 3 screws <A>.

    2. Disengage the 9 guides to remove the defroster nozzle assembly.

  4. REMOVE AUTOMATIC LIGHT CONTROL SENSOR

    Click here

  5. REMOVE INDICATOR LIGHT ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  6. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL SPACER


    1. Remove the 2 screws <A> and No. 1 instrument panel spacer.

  7. REMOVE NAVIGATION ANTENNA ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET (w/ Navigation Antenna)

    Click here

  8. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Navigation Antenna)

    Click here

  9. REMOVE SIDE DEFROSTER NOZZLE ASSEMBLY LH


    1. Remove the 2 screws <A>.

    2. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the side defroster nozzle assembly LH.

  10. REMOVE SIDE DEFROSTER NOZZLE LH


    1. Disengage the 3 claws to remove the side defroster nozzle LH.

  11. REMOVE SIDE DEFROSTER NOZZLE ASSEMBLY RH


    1. Remove the 2 screws <A>.

    2. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the side defroster nozzle assembly RH.

  12. REMOVE SIDE DEFROSTER NOZZLE RH


    1. Disengage the 3 claws to remove the side defroster nozzle RH.

  13. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE


    1. Disengage the 10 clamps to remove the No. 2 instrument panel wire.

  14. REMOVE SPARE SWITCH HOLE COVER (for RHD)


    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the spare switch hole cover.

  15. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL CUSHION


    1. Remove the instrument panel cushion.
