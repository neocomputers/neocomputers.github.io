AUTOMATIC HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVEL CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. AUTOMATIC HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVEL CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


    1. Stop the vehicle on a level surface and keep the vehicle height unchanged.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the light control switch to the head position.

    4. Slowly change the vehicle posture and then hold it in that position for 3 seconds or more and check that the headlight beam level changes.
