LIGHTING SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. INSPECT ILLUMINATED ENTRY SYSTEM OPERATION

    Note

    Perform this inspection with the customize parameters at the initial settings.

    Tech Tips

    Perform this inspection with interior light door switch in the map light assembly turned DOOR position.

    Control Condition Lights that Operate
    Actuation Area-linked With the power switch off and all of the doors closed and locked, enter an exterior detection area while carrying an electrical key transmitter and check that the lights that operate turn on for approximately 15 seconds and then turn off.

    • Map Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
    Door Lock/Unlock-linked With the power switch off and all of the doors closed and locked, unlock any door using an unlock operation (key-linked, wireless or entry unlock operation) and check that the lights that operate turn on for 15 seconds and then turn off.

    • Map Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
    Door Open/Close-linked With the power switch off and all of the doors closed, open any door and check that the lights that operate turn on. While the lights that operate are on, close all of the doors and check that the lights that operate turn off after approximately 15 seconds.

    • Map Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
    With the power switch off and all of the doors closed, open any door and check that the light that operates turns on. While the light that operates is on, close all of the doors and check that the light that operates turns off after approximately 40 seconds. Power Switch Illumination
    With the power switch on, open any door and check that the lights that operate turn on. While the lights that operate are on, close all of the doors and check that the lights that operate turn off.

    • Map Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
    Power Switch-linked Turn the power switch from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off and check that the lights that operate turn on for approximately 15 seconds and then turn off.

    • Map Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

    • No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly (Footwell Light)*
    Turn the power switch from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off and check that the light that operates turns on for approximately 40 seconds and then turns off. Power Switch Illumination

    • *: w/ Footwell Light

  2. INSPECT BATTERY SAVING CONTROL OPERATION


    1. The operations and conditions of this control are described below.

      Control Condition Lights that Operate
      Basic operation

      When 20 minutes elapse after the power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off and the lights that operate remain on, the lights are turned off.

      Tech Tips

      Battery saving control is canceled when any of the following operations is performed before the lights are turned off by battery saving control. After the operation is performed, the lights remain on for another 20 minutes and then the lights are turned off.


      • The power switch is turned on (ACC) or on (IG).

      • Any door is opened and closed.

      • The luggage compartment door is opened and closed.

      • Any door is unlocked.

      • The key enters any exterior actuation area.

      • Power Switch Illumination

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly
      Interior light auto cut operation

      When 20 minutes elapse after the power switch is turned from on (ACC) or on (IG) to off and the lights that operate remain on, the lights are turned off.

      Tech Tips

      Battery saving control is canceled when any of the following operations is performed before the lights are turned off by battery saving control. After the operation is performed, the lights remain on for another 20 minutes and then the lights are turned off.


      • The power switch is turned on (ACC) or on (IG).

      • Map Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Room Light Assembly

      • Vanity Light Assembly

      • No. 1 Luggage Compartment Light Assembly

      • Courtesy Light Assembly
