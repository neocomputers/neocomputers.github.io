PRE-CRASH SAFETY SYSTEM ECU Power Source Circuit

DESCRIPTION

This circuit supplies power to the driving support ECU assembly when the power switch is on (IG).

WIRING DIAGRAM

*1 from IG1-NO. 1 Relay
*2 ECU-IG1 NO. 4
*3 Driving Support ECU Assembly

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

Inspect the fuses for circuits related to this system before performing the following inspection procedure.

PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK DRIVING SUPPORT ECU ASSEMBLY (B VOLTAGE)


    1. *a

      Front view of wire harness connector

      (to Driving Support ECU Assembly)

      Disconnect the I41 driving support ECU assembly connector.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Voltage
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
