AUDIO AND VISUAL SYSTEM Speaker Circuit

DESCRIPTION


  • If there is a short in a speaker circuit, the radio and display receiver assembly detects it and stops output to the speakers.

    Thus sound cannot be heard from the speakers even if there is no malfunction in the radio and display receiver assembly or speakers.
