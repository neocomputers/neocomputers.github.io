  3. REMOVE FLOW SHUTTING VALVE (WATER VALVE)


    1. Disconnect the 3 connectors.

    2. Disengage the clamp to disconnect the wire harness from the water outlet.

    3. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 3 radiator hose.

    4. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 9 water by-pass hose.

    5. Remove the 2 nuts.

    6. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 10 water by-pass hose and remove the water outlet with the flow shutting valve (water valve).

    7. Remove the water outlet gasket from the cylinder head sub-assembly.

    8. Secure the water outlet in a vise between aluminum plates.

      Note

      Do not damage the water outlet.

    9. Remove the 2 bolts and flow shutting valve (water valve).

    10. Remove the gasket from the flow shutting valve (water valve).
