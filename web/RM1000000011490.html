PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE REAR BUMPER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE BLIND SPOT MONITOR SENSOR LH


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove the 3 nuts and blind spot monitor sensor LH.

      Note

      Replace the blind spot monitor sensor if it has been dropped or subjected to a severe impact.

  3. REMOVE BLIND SPOT MONITOR SENSOR RH


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove the 3 nuts and blind spot monitor sensor RH.

      Note

      Replace the blind spot monitor sensor if it has been dropped or subjected to a severe impact.

  4. REMOVE BLIND SPOT MONITOR BRACKET LH


    1. Disengage the clamp.

    2. Remove the 3 nuts and blind spot monitor bracket LH.

  5. REMOVE BLIND SPOT MONITOR BRACKET RH


    1. Disengage the clamp.

    2. Remove the 3 nuts and blind spot monitor bracket RH.
