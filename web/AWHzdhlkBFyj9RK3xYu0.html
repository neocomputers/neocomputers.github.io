  9. REMOVE REAR BUMPER ENERGY ABSORBER


    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Disengage the 3 clamps to remove the rear bumper energy absorber.

  10. REMOVE NO. 3 LUGGAGE ROOM WIRE


    1. Disengage the 6 clamps and remove the No. 3 luggage room wire.

  11. REMOVE REAR BUMPER CENTER PAD


    1. Remove the 3 rear bumper center pads.

  12. REMOVE REAR BUMPER PLATE LH


    1. *a Hook
      Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws as shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the hook to remove the rear bumper plate LH.

    Tech Tips

    Use the same procedure as for the LH side.

  14. REMOVE REAR BUMPER CENTER GUARD


    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Disengage the 14 claws to remove the rear bumper center guard.

    1. Remove the No. 1 rear bumper pad.

    1. Remove in this Direction

    2. Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

    3. Disengage the guide as shown in the illustration to remove the rear bumper side retainer LH.
