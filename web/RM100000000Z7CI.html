PRE-CRASH SAFETY SYSTEM TERMINALS OF ECU


  1. CHECK DRIVING SUPPORT ECU ASSEMBLY


    1. Measure the voltage and resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Terminal No. (Symbol) Wiring Color Terminal Description Condition Specified Condition
      I41-7 (B) - I41-28 (GND)

      P - W-B *1

      B - W-B *2

      		 Power source Power switch on (IG) 11 to 14 V
      Power switch off Below 1 V
      I41-3 (BZ) - I41-28 (GND) P - W-B Skid control buzzer output Power switch on (IG), buzzer not sounding 11 to 14 V
      Power switch on (IG), buzzer sounding Below 1 V
      I41-28 (GND) - Body ground W-B - Body ground Ground Always Below 1 Ω

      *1: for LHD

      *2: for RHD
