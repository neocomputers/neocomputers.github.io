SIMPLE INTELLIGENT PARKING ASSIST SYSTEM UTILITY


  1. TIRE CIRCUMFERENCE SETTING


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar / Utility.


      Body Electrical > IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar > Utility
      Tester Display
      Tire Circumference Setting

    5. According to the GTS, select the tire circumference setting.

      Tire Circumference Setting Tire Circumference Compensation Compensation
      Short 3 Small 3 -15 mm (-0.591 in)
      Short 2 Small 2 -10 mm (-0.394 in)
      Short 1 Small 1 -5 mm (-0.197 in)
      Standard Standard 0 mm (0.00 in)
      Long 1 Large 1 5 mm (0.197 in)
      Long 2 Large 2 10 mm (0.394 in)
      Long 3 Large 3 15 mm (0.591 in)

    6. Disconnect the GTS from the DLC3.
