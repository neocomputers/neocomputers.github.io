PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE REAR BUMPER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE REAR SIDE ULTRASONIC SENSOR

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the rear side ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.

  3. REMOVE REAR CORNER ULTRASONIC SENSOR

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the rear corner ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.

  4. REMOVE REAR CENTER ULTRASONIC SENSOR

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the rear center ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.

  5. REMOVE REAR SIDE ULTRASONIC SENSOR RETAINER

    Tech Tips


    • Perform this procedure only when replacement of the rear side ultrasonic sensor retainer is necessary.

    • When removing the rear side ultrasonic sensor retainer, heat the rear bumper assembly and rear side ultrasonic sensor retainer using a heat light.


    1. Heat the rear bumper assembly and rear side ultrasonic sensor retainer using a heat light at the specified temperature for 3 to 5 minutes.

      Heating Temperature
      Item Temperature
      Rear Bumper Assembly 40 to 60°C (104 to 140°F)
      Rear Side Ultrasonic Sensor Retainer

      CAUTION:


      • Do not touch the heat light and heated parts.

      • Touching the heat light may result in burns.

      • Touching heated parts for a long time may result in burns.

      *a Heated Part
      *b Heat Light

    2. Remove the 2 rear side ultrasonic sensor retainers.

      *A for RH Side *B for LH Side
