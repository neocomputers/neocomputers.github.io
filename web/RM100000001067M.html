PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE MASS AIR FLOW METER SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the mass air flow meter sub-assembly connector.

    2. Remove the 2 screws and mass air flow meter sub-assembly from the air cleaner cap sub-assembly.

      Note

      If the mass air flow meter sub-assembly has been struck or dropped, replace it.
