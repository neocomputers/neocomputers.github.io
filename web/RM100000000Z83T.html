RADIO RECEIVER REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL END RH (for LHD)

    Click here

  2. REMOVE CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  3. REMOVE RADIO RECEIVER ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET (for 7 Inch Display)


    1. Remove the 4 bolts.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Pull the radio receiver assembly with bracket toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 guides.

    3. Disconnect each connector and remove the radio receiver assembly with bracket.

  4. REMOVE RADIO AND DISPLAY RECEIVER ASSEMBLY (for 8 Inch Display)


    1. Remove the 4 bolts.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Pull the radio and display receiver assembly toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the 2 guides.

    3. Disconnect each connector and remove the radio and display receiver assembly.

  5. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIO BRACKET (for 7 Inch Display)


    1. Remove the 3 screws and No. 1 radio bracket.

  6. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIO BRACKET (for 7 Inch Display)


    1. Remove the 3 screws and No. 2 radio bracket.

  7. REMOVE RADIO AND DISPLAY RECEIVER ASSEMBLY (for 7 Inch Display)
