BACK DOOR LOCK INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT BACK DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY


    1. Check the operation of the door lock motor.


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Back Door Lock Assembly)
        *b Close (Lock)
        *c Open (Unlock)

        Move the door lock to the close (lock) position.

      2. Apply auxiliary battery voltage and check the operation of the door lock motor.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Result

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

        		 Back door lock motor open (unlock) operation

        If the result is not as specified, replace the back door lock assembly.

    2. Check the operation of the back door courtesy switch.


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        2 - 3 Closed (Locked) 10 kΩ or higher
        2 - 3 Open (Unlocked) Below 10 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the back door lock assembly.
