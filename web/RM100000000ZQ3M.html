PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT VEHICLE APPROACHING SPEAKER ASSEMBLY


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Vehicle Approaching Speaker Assembly)

      Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 (+) - 2 (-) Always 6.4 to 9.6 Ω

      Tech Tips

      Make sure that there are no problems such as looseness or play with the installation of the vehicle approaching speaker assembly. If the result is not as specified, replace the vehicle approaching speaker assembly.
