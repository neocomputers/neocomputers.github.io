  17. REMOVE FRONT DOOR LOCK WITH MOTOR ASSEMBLY


    1. *1 Front Door Lock Open Rod
      Remove in this Direction

      Disconnect the connector.

    2. Using a T30 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the 3 screws.

    3. Slide the front door lock with motor assembly downward to disconnect the front door lock open rod and remove the front door lock with motor assembly and cables as a unit.

    4. When reusing the front door lock with motor assembly:


      1. Remove the door lock wiring harness seal from the front door lock with motor assembly.
