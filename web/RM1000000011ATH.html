HEADUP DISPLAY DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 COMBINATION METER MIRROR CASE


    1. Remove the screw.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 6 claws and 2 guides to remove the No. 1 combination meter mirror case as shown in the illustration.

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 COMBINATION METER MIRROR PLATE


    1. Remove the 5 screws and No. 1 combination meter mirror plate.

  3. REMOVE COMBINATION METER MIRROR ECU


    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    2. Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

      Disengage the 2 claws and raise the holder in the direction indicated by the arrow (1) shown in the illustration.

      Note

      Make sure not to rotate the holder 90° or more, or it may be damaged.

    3. Disconnect the connector in the direction indicated by the arrow (2) shown in the illustration.

    4. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the combination meter mirror ECU.

  4. REMOVE COMBINATION DISPLAY


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the combination display as shown in the illustration.

  5. REMOVE COMBINATION METER WIRE


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the 2 clamps to remove the combination meter wire.

  6. REMOVE COMBINATION METER MIRROR


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the mirror clip as shown in the illustration.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 guides to remove the combination meter mirror as shown in the illustration.

  7. REMOVE NO. 2 COMBINATION METER MIRROR


    1. Remove the 3 screws.

    2. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the No. 2 combination meter mirror.
