TIRE PRESSURE WARNING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:C2176/76

DTC Code DTC Name
C2176/76 Receiver Error

DESCRIPTION

Tire pressure warning valve and transmitter signals are transmitted to the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver in the vehicle as radio waves.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Note
C2176/76 Receiver Error Malfunction in the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver internal circuit Tire pressure warning ECU and receiver -

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note


  • When replacing the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver, read the transmitter IDs stored in the old ECU using the GTS and write them down before removal.

  • It is necessary to perform initialization after registration Click here of the transmitter IDs into the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver if the ECU has been replaced.
