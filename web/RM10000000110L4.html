ELECTRONICALLY CONTROLLED BRAKE SYSTEM TRC does not Operate

DESCRIPTION

When ABS, TRC or VSC is operating, the skid control ECU (brake booster with master cylinder assembly) blinks the slip indicator light to inform the driver that slippage occurred.

When in VSC off mode, or TRC and VSC are disabled, the multi-information display in the combination meter assembly displays "Traction Control Turned Off" and the VSC OFF indicator light illuminates to inform the driver.

When a communication malfunction with the hybrid vehicle control ECU is detected, TRC and VSC are disabled. Also when the system is malfunctioning, TRC and VSC are disabled and the indicator lights of related systems illuminate.

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

After replacing the skid control ECU (brake booster with master cylinder assembly), perform linear solenoid valve offset learning, ABS holding solenoid valve learning, yaw rate and acceleration sensor zero point calibration and system information memorization after performing "Reset Memory".

      The VSC OFF switch (combination switch assembly) had been operated to turn off TRC when TRC did not operate.

      When the multi-information display in the combination meter assembly displays "Traction Control Turned Off", TRC does not operate.

  5. PERFORM OPERATION CHECK DRIVING


    1. Operate the VSC OFF switch (combination switch assembly) and check that the system is not in TRC off mode or VSC off mode.

    2. Check that no DTCs are output and that each warning light is not illuminated.

    3. Start off the vehicle on a low friction surface and check that TRC operates when attempting to make the drive wheels slip.

      OK
      TRC operates when slip occurs when starting off.

      Note


      • Even when the system is normal, VSC may not operate when the vehicle speed is low or TRC may not operate due to a malfunction. Make sure that only the drive wheels slip during the procedure.

      • When confirming that the slip indicator light blinks, do not slip the drive wheels longer than necessary. Whether TRC operated or not can be checked by reading the Freeze Frame Data when the system operated.

      • Pay attention to your surroundings when performing the procedure and depress the accelerator pedal with caution.
