RADIO ANTENNA CORD INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL NO. 4 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. When reusing the No. 4 antenna cord sub-assembly:


      1. Install in this Direction

        Engage the claw as shown in the illustration to temporarily install a new No. 1 wire harness protector.

        Tech Tips

        Use the same procedure for the No. 2 wire harness protector.

      2. Install in this Direction

        Install the No. 1 wire harness protector to the grommet as shown in the illustration.

        Tech Tips

        Use the same procedure for the No. 2 wire harness protector.

      3. for Back Door Side:


        1. Connect the rubber protrusion of the grommet to the No. 2 wire harness protector with tape.

        2. Connect the antenna cord to the No. 2 wire harness protector with tape.

    2. Engage the 8 claws to connect the 2 grommets.

    3. w/o Glass Breakage Sensor:


      1. Engage the clamp.

      2. Connect the connector.

      3. Engage the 2 clamps.

      4. Connect the connector to install the No. 4 antenna cord sub-assembly.

    4. w/ Glass Breakage Sensor:


      1. Engage the 4 clamps.

      2. Connect the 2 connectors.

      3. Engage the 2 clamps.

      4. Connect the 2 connectors to install the No. 4 antenna cord sub-assembly.

  2. INSTALL BACK DOOR UPPER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  3. INSTALL NO. 3 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Engage the 6 clamps.

    2. Engage the claw.

    3. Connect the connector to install the No. 3 antenna cord sub-assembly.

  4. INSTALL NO. 5 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna)


    1. Engage the clamp.

    2. Connect the connector to install the No. 5 antenna cord sub-assembly.

  5. INSTALL NO. 2 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Tech Tips

    Butyl tape and adhesive tape are not available as supply parts. If these pieces of tape still have enough adhesion to secure the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the roof headlining assembly, reuse them. If the adhesive tape and/or the butyl tape is no longer sticky, apply new tape following the procedure below.


    1. Apply new butyl tape.

      w/o Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:

      *a Marking - -
      Butyl tape Front

      w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:

      *a Marking - -
      Butyl tape Front

      1. Remove the old butyl tape from the roof headlining assembly.

      2. Prepare an appropriate amount of new butyl tape.

        Tech Tips

        Be careful not to touch the adhesive surface.

      3. Apply the butyl tape to the roof headlining assembly while aligning the tape with the markings on the roof headlining assembly.

      4. Peel off the release paper from the butyl tape.

    2. w/o Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:


      1. Align the marking tape (A) on the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the protrusion on the front of the roof headlining assembly and wrap tape around the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly and protrusion of the roof headlining assembly.

        *a Marking Tape (A) *b Marking Tape (B)
        *c Adhesive Tape (A) *d Adhesive Tape (B)
        *e Adhesive Tape (C) *f Protrusion
        *g Notch *h Marking
        Adjustment Area Front

      2. Install the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the roof headlining assembly from the marking tape (A) to the adjustment area.

        Note


        • Make sure that there are no gaps between the roof headlining assembly and No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly, and that the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is not twisted.

        • Make sure the No. 2 antenna cord assembly is securely installed. If any part of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is loose, it will cause an abnormal noise.

      3. Apply the adhesive tape (A) as shown in the illustration.

      4. Engage the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the notch of the roof headlining assembly.

      5. Align the marking tape (B) on the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the marking on the roof headlining assembly and secure the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the adhesive tape (B) as shown in the illustration.

      6. Install the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the roof headlining assembly from the marking tape (B) to the adjustment area.

        Note


        • Make sure that there are no gaps between the roof headlining assembly and No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly, and that the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is not twisted.

        • Make sure the No. 2 antenna cord assembly is securely installed. If any part of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is loose, it will cause an abnormal noise.

        Tech Tips

        Secure the extra length of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly in the adjustment area.

      7. Apply the adhesive tape (C) as shown in the illustration to secure the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly.

    3. w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:


      1. Align the marking tape (A) on the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the protrusion on the front of the roof headlining assembly and wrap tape around the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly and protrusion of the roof headlining assembly.

        *a Marking Tape (A) *b Marking Tape (B)
        *c Marking Tape (C) *d Adhesive Tape (A)
        *e Adhesive Tape (B) *f Adhesive Tape (C)
        *g Adhesive Tape (D) *h Protrusion
        *i Notch *j Marking
        Adhesive Tape Front
        Adjustment Area - -

      2. Install the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the roof headlining assembly from the marking tape (A) to each adjustment area.

        Note


        • Make sure that there are no gaps between the roof headlining assembly and No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly, and that the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is not twisted.

        • Make sure the No. 2 antenna cord assembly is securely installed. If any part of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is loose, it will cause an abnormal noise.

      3. Apply the adhesive tape (A) as shown in the illustration.

      4. Engage the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the notch of the roof headlining assembly.

      5. Align the marking tape (B) on the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the marking on the roof headlining assembly and secure the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the adhesive tape (B) as shown in the illustration.

      6. Install the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the roof headlining assembly from the marking tape (B) to the adjustment area.

        Note


        • Make sure that there are no gaps between the roof headlining assembly and No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly, and that the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is not twisted.

        • Make sure the No. 2 antenna cord assembly is securely installed. If any part of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is loose, it will cause an abnormal noise.

        Tech Tips

        Secure the extra length of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly in the adjustment area.

      7. Align the marking tape (C) on the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the marking on the roof headlining assembly and secure the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly with the adhesive tape (C) as shown in the illustration.

      8. Install the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly to the roof headlining assembly from the marking tape (C) to the adjustment area.

        Note


        • Make sure that there are no gaps between the roof headlining assembly and No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly, and that the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is not twisted.

        • Make sure the No. 2 antenna cord assembly is securely installed. If any part of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly is loose, it will cause an abnormal noise.

        Tech Tips

        Secure the extra length of the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly in the adjustment area.

      9. Apply the adhesive tape (D) as shown in the illustration to secure the No. 2 antenna cord sub-assembly.

      10. Engage the clamp.
