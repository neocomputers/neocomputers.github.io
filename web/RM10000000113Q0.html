ELECTRONIC SHIFT LEVER SYSTEM VEHICLE CONTROL HISTORY


  1. CHECK VEHICLE CONTROL HISTORY (HYBRID CONTROL SYSTEM)


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Hybrid Control / Utility / Vehicle Control History.


      Powertrain > Hybrid Control > Utility
      Tester Display
      Vehicle Control History
      FFD Group Code Tester Display Measurement Item Diagnostic Note
      - X0500 Shift P Operation during Running History of P position switch (transmission shift main switch) being operated while driving -
      - X0501 Shift Operation when Auxiliary Battery Voltage Low History of attempts to change shift state when auxiliary battery voltage was low -
      - X0502 Consecutive Shift Change between Shift P and Other than Shift P in Short Times History of attempts to change shift state to and from park (P) within a short period of time -
      - X0503 Shift Operation during Ready Indicator Blinking History of attempts to change shift state with READY indicator light blinking -
      - X0504 Shift R/D/B(S) Operation during Ready OFF History of attempts to change shift state to reverse (R), drive (D) or brake (B) with power switch on (IG) -
      - X0505 Shift Operation during Accelerator & Brake Depress History of attempts to change shift state from park (P) with READY indicator light illuminated and accelerator and brake pedals depressed -
      - X0506 Shift B(S) Operation from Shift Position R History of attempts to change shift state from reverse (R) to brake (B) -
      - X0507 Shift D Operation during Backward Movement History of attempts to change shift state to drive (D) while backing up -
      - X0508 Shift R Operation during Forward Movement History of attempts to change shift state to reverse (R) while driving forward -
      - X0509 Shift R/D Operation during Ready OFF History of shift state being changed to neutral (N) due to shift lever being held in R or D with power switch on (IG) -
      - X050A Quick Shift Operation to Shift Position N during Running History of shift state being changed to neutral (N) while driving, without shift lever being held in N for a short time -
      - X050B Shift N Change by Busy Shift History of shift state being changed to neutral (N) due to shift lever being repeatedly moved to R, D and/or N within a short period of time -
      - X050C Shift N Operation on The Way Back to Home Position after Shift D/R Operation History of shift state being changed to neutral (N) when shift lever was returning from R or D -
      - X050D Shift R Operation on The Way Back to Home Position after Shift D Operation History of shift state being changed to reverse (R) when shift lever was returning from D -
      - X050E Shift D Operation on The Way Back to Home Position after Shift R Operation History of shift state being changed to drive (D) when shift lever was returning from R -
      - X050F Shift P Auto Change by Cruise Control History of shift state being changed to park (P) automatically by brake hold prevention function of dynamic radar cruise control system (w/ full-speed range following function) -
      - X0510 Shift Operation without Depressing Brake from Shift Position P History of attempts to change shift state from park (P) with brake pedal not depressed -
      - X0511 Shift N Operation at Short Times during Low Speed/Stopping History of attempts to change shift state to neutral (N) by not holding shift lever in N for a long enough time while stopped or driving at a low speed -
      - X0512 Shift B(S) Operation from Shift Position P/N History of attempts to change shift state from park (P) or neutral (N) to brake (B) -
      - X0513 Shift N Operation during Running History of shift state being changed to neutral (N) by holding shift lever in N while driving -
      - X0514 Shift Operation to Except P Position during Hybrid Battery Charging/Power Feeding History of attempts to change shift state from park (P) to any other position when charging from external power source or supplying power to external power source -
      - X0515 Shift P Operation before Vehicle Stop History of shift state not being changed to park (P) when P position switch (transmission shift main switch) was operated before vehicle was stopped -
      - X0516 Shift P Operation during Other than Shift P Operation History of P position switch (transmission shift main switch) and shift lever being operated simultaneously -
      - X0517 Auto Change to Shift Position P Cancel History of P position automatic change function not operating -
      - X0518 Voltage Low for Shift Control System History of shift control system not operating due to drop in power source voltage -

  2. VEHICLE CONTROL HISTORY FREEZE FRAME DATA (HYBRID CONTROL)


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Hybrid Control / Utility / Vehicle Control History.


      Powertrain > Hybrid Control > Utility
      Tester Display
      Vehicle Control History

    5. Select a vehicle control history item to access the applicable freeze frame data.

    6. Check the freeze frame Data recorded with the Vehicle Control History.

      Click here

  3. CLEAR VEHICLE CONTROL HISTORY (HYBRID CONTROL SYSTEM)


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Hybrid Control / Utility / Vehicle Control History (Clear).

      Note

      By performing this procedure, all stored Vehicle Control History items will be cleared.
