CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR


    1. Disconnect the No. 1 fuel vapor feed hose from the fuel vapor feed pipe.

    2. Disconnect the camshaft position sensor connector.

    3. Remove the bolt and camshaft position sensor from the cylinder head cover sub-assembly.

      Note

      If a camshaft position sensor has been struck or dropped, replace it.
