    1. Disengage the claw and guide to remove the front bumper pad LH.

    Tech Tips

    2. Remove in this Direction

  11. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER EXTENSION MOUNTING BRACKET


    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Double-sided Tape
      Remove in this Direction

      Separate the 2 double-sided tapes to disengage the 2 guides to remove the front bumper extension mounting bracket as shown in the illustration.

    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the 2 front fender liner retainers.

      Tech Tips

    Click here

  14. REMOVE FRONT TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

    Tech Tips

  15. REMOVE NO. 3 ENGINE ROOM WIRE (w/ Intuitive Parking Assist System)


    1. Disengage the 7 clamps and remove the No. 3 engine room wire.

  16. REMOVE FOG LIGHT COVER LH


    1. Disengage the 5 claws to remove the fog light cover LH.

  17. REMOVE FOG LIGHT COVER RH

    Tech Tips

    Use the same procedure as for the LH side.

  18. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER HOLE COVER LH


    1. *a Hook
      Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws as shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the hook to remove the front bumper hole cover LH.

  19. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER HOLE COVER RH

    Tech Tips

    Use the same procedure as for the LH side.

  20. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER PAD


    1. Double-sided Tape

      Separate the double-sided tape to remove the front bumper pad.

      Tech Tips

      Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  21. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER EXTENSION LH


    1. Remove the 6 screws.

    2. Remove the clip.

    3. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 3 claws and 6 guides as shown in the illustration to remove the front bumper extension LH.

  22. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER EXTENSION RH

    Tech Tips

    Use the same procedure as for the LH side.

  23. REMOVE HOOD TO FRONT END PANEL SEAL


    1. Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

      Disengage the 2 guides as shown in the illustration.

    2. Separate the double-sided tape and disengage the guide to remove the hood to front end panel seal.

      Double-sided Tape - -

  24. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER CENTER BAR


    1. Remove the 8 clips.

    2. Remove the 2 outside moulding retainers.

    3. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 guides to remove the front bumper center bar as shown in the illustration.

  25. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE EMBLEM ASSEMBLY


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape around the 2 spring nuts as shown in the illustration.

    2. *a Spring Nut

      Using a screwdriver, pull out the 2 spring nuts as shown in the illustration.

    3. *a Cut

      Using a plier nipper (side cutters), cut the 2 spring nuts at the positions shown in the illustration and remove them.

    4. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and 2 guides as shown in the illustration to remove the radiator grille emblem assembly.

  26. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER ENERGY ABSORBER


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 4 guides as shown in the illustration to remove the front bumper energy absorber.

  27. REMOVE LOWER FRONT BUMPER ABSORBER


    1. Remove the 3 clips and lower front bumper absorber.
