PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE HEADLIGHT LIGHT CONTROL ECU SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Note

    Make sure to replace the headlight gasket with a new one. Failure to do so may cause water ingress.


    1. Remove the 3 screws.

    2. Separate the connector cover from the headlight light control ECU sub-assembly.

    3. Disconnect the 2 connectors to remove the headlight light control ECU sub-assembly.

      Note

      If the headlight light control ECU sub-assembly has been struck or dropped, replace it with a new one.

    Note
