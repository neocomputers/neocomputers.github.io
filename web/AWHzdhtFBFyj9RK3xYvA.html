NAME PLATE REMOVAL

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips

When removing the name plates, heat the vehicle body, back door panel and name plates using a heat light.

Heating Temperature
Item Temperature
Vehicle Body 40 to 60°C (104 to 140°F)
Name Plate 20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)

CAUTION:


  • Do not touch the heat light and heated parts, touching the heat light may result in burns.

  • Touching heated parts for a long time may result in burns.

*a Heated Part
*b Heat Light

Note

Do not heat the vehicle body, back door panel or name plate excessively.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 BACK DOOR NAME PLATE (for Type A)


    1. *a Wooden Block
      *b Piano Wire
      Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape around the No. 1 back door name plate as shown in the illustration.

    2. Insert a piano wire between the back door panel and No. 1 back door name plate.

    3. Tie objects that can serve as handles (for example, wooden blocks) to both wire ends.

    4. Pull the piano wire and cut the double-sided tape that holds the No. 1 back door name plate to the back door panel to remove the No. 1 back door name plate.

      Note

      Be careful not to damage the back door panel.

  2. REMOVE NO. 2 BACK DOOR NAME PLATE (for Type A)

  4. REMOVE BACK DOOR ORNAMENT SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape around the back door ornament sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.

    2. Using a moulding remover, separate the double-sided tape and disengage the 2 pins to remove the back door ornament sub-assembly.

      Note

      Be careful not to damage the back door panel.

  5. REMOVE SIDE PANEL EMBLEM

    4. Pull the piano wire and cut the double-sided tape that holds the side panel emblem to the vehicle body to remove the side panel emblem.
