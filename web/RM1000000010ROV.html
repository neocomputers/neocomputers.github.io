LIGHTING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B243D, B243E

DTC Code DTC Name
B243D Left Low Beam Fan Malfunction
B243E Right Low Beam Fan Malfunction

DESCRIPTION

A DTC is stored when a malfunction of the headlight fan occurs and the low beam headlight is dim. The No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH stores DTC B243D and B243E.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Note
B243D Left Low Beam Fan Malfunction Malfunction of headlight fan occurs and low beam headlight LH is dim

  • Headlight unit assembly LH

  • Headlight unit LH

  • No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH

 -
B243E Right Low Beam Fan Malfunction Malfunction of headlight fan occurs and low beam headlight RH is dim

  • Headlight unit assembly RH

  • Headlight unit RH

  • No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly RH

 -

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

If the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH has been replaced, it is necessary to synchronize the vehicle information and initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.

  1. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / HL AutoLeveling*1 or AHS*2 / Trouble Codes.


      • *1: w/o Adaptive High Beam System

      • *2: w/ Adaptive High Beam System

    5. Clear the DTCs.
