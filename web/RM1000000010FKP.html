STOP LIGHT SWITCH INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL STOP LIGHT SWITCH ASSEMBLY


    1. Insert the stop light switch assembly until the threaded sleeve hits the pedal.

      Note

      When inserting the stop light switch assembly, support the pedal from behind so that the pedal is not pushed in.

    2. Turn the stop light switch assembly 1/4 clockwise to install it.

      Torque:
      1.5 N*m  { 15 kgf*cm, 13 in.*lbf }
      or less

      Note

      When inserting the stop light switch assembly, support the pedal from behind so that the pedal is not pushed in.

    3. Connect the connector.

    4. Check the protrusion of the plunger.

      Protrusion of the Plunger
      Area Measurement
      a 0.6 to 2.6 mm (0.0236 to 0.102 in.)

    5. If the protrusion is not as specified, recheck the stop light switch assembly installation and perform brake pedal adjustment if necessary.
