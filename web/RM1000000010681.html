PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR (w/ Canister Pump Module)


    1. Drain the engine coolant (for Engine).

      Click here

    2. Remove the No. 1 engine cover sub-assembly.

      Click here

    3. Disconnect the No. 2 engine coolant temperature sensor connector.

    4. Remove the clip and No. 2 engine coolant temperature sensor from the radiator pipe.

      Note

      If the No. 2 engine coolant temperature sensor has been struck or dropped, replace it.

  2. REMOVE ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR


    1. Remove the EGR valve assembly.

      Click here

    2. Disconnect the engine coolant temperature sensor connector.

    3. *1 Gasket

      Using SST, remove the engine coolant temperature sensor from the water outlet.

      SST
      09817-33191

      Note

      If the engine coolant temperature sensor has been struck or dropped, replace it.

    4. Remove the gasket from the engine coolant temperature sensor.

  3. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR (w/o Canister Pump Module)


    1. Drain the engine coolant (for Engine).

      Click here

    2. Remove the inverter with converter assembly.

      Click here

    3. Disconnect the No. 2 engine coolant temperature sensor connector.

    4. Remove the clip and No. 2 engine coolant temperature sensor from the No. 2 water by-pass pipe.

      Note

      If the No. 2 engine coolant temperature sensor has been struck or dropped, replace it.
