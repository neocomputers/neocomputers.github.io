CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK FOR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Central Gateway / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Central Gateway > Trouble Codes

    5. Read the DTCs.

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Central Gateway / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Central Gateway > Trouble Codes

    5. Clear the DTCs.


      Body Electrical > Central Gateway > Clear DTCs
