BLOWER UNIT INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL BLOWER ASSEMBLY


    1. for LHD:


      1. Engage the 3 guides and 2 claws.

      2. Install the blower assembly to the air conditioning radiator assembly with the 2 screws.

      3. Engage the 4 guides.

      4. Connect the connector.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Engage the 2 guides and 2 claws.

      2. Install the blower assembly to the air conditioning radiator assembly with the 2 screws.

      3. Engage the 2 guides.

      4. Connect the connector.
