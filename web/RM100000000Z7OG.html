SEAT HEATER SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT SEAT HEATER SWITCH (for LH)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Seat Heater Switch (for LH))

      Check the seat heater switch (for LH).


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        1 - 6 HI switch on Below 1 Ω
        2 - 3
        1 - 2 LO switch on Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the seat heater switch (for LH).

    2. Check that the LEDs illuminate.


      1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the seat heater switch (for LH) and check that each LED illuminates.

        OK
        Auxiliary battery Connection Condition Specified Condition

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 6

        		 HI switch on Comes on

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 6

        		 LO switch on Comes on

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 4

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 5

        		 Always Comes on

        If the result is not as specified, replace the seat heater switch (for LH).

  2. INSPECT SEAT HEATER SWITCH (for RH)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Seat Heater Switch (for RH))

      Check the seat heater switch (for RH).


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        2 - 10 HI switch on Below 1 Ω
        3 - 4
        2 - 3 LO switch on Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the seat heater switch (for RH).

    2. Check that the LEDs illuminate.


      1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the seat heater switch (for RH) and check that each LED illuminates.

        OK
        Auxiliary battery Connection Condition Specified Condition

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 3

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 10

        		 HI switch on Comes on

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 3

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 10

        		 LO switch on Comes on

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 6

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 8

        		 Always Comes on

        If the result is not as specified, replace the seat heater switch (for RH).
