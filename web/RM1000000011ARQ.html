METER / GAUGE SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK FOR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Combination Meter / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Combination Meter > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Combination Meter / Clear DTCs.


      Body Electrical > Combination Meter > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
