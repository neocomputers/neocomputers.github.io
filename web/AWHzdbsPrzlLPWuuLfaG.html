ROOF HEADLINING DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 ROOF SILENCER PAD


    1. Remove the No. 1 roof silencer pad.

  2. REMOVE NO. 2 ROOF SILENCER PAD


    1. Remove the No. 2 roof silencer pad.

  3. REMOVE NO. 2 ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  4. REMOVE VANITY LIGHT ASSEMBLY

    Tech Tips

    Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.


    1. *1 Bulb Holder
      *a Claw (A)
      *b Claw (B)

      Disengage the 2 claws (A) and separate the bulb holder from the vanity light assembly as shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the claw (B) to remove the vanity light assembly.

  5. REMOVE THEFT WARNING ULTRASONIC SENSOR (w/ Intrusion Sensor)

    Click here

  6. REMOVE NO. 1 ROOF WIRE


    1. Remove the adhesive tape from the roof headlining assembly.

      *A w/o Toyota Safety Sense *B w/ Toyota Safety Sense
      *C for LHD with Rain Sensor *D for RHD with Rain Sensor
      *E w/ Intrusion Sensor - -
      Adhesive Tape - -

    2. Turn the 2 visor connectors counterclockwise approximately 90° and remove them from the roof headlining assembly.

      *a 90° - -
      Rotation Direction - -

    3. Disengage the 4 clamps and remove the No. 1 roof wire from the roof headlining assembly.

  7. REMOVE FRONT SIDE RAIL SPACER LH (w/ Roof Headlining Pad)


    1. Remove the front side rail spacer LH.

  8. REMOVE FRONT SIDE RAIL SPACER RH (w/ Roof Headlining Pad)


    1. Remove the front side rail spacer RH.

  9. REMOVE NO. 2 ROOF HEADLINING SET PLATE LH (w/ Roof Headlining Pad)


    1. Remove the No. 2 roof headlining set plate LH.

  10. REMOVE NO. 2 ROOF HEADLINING SET PLATE RH (w/ Roof Headlining Pad)


    1. Remove the No. 2 roof headlining set plate RH.

  11. REMOVE NO. 2 ROOF HEADLINING PAD (w/ Roof Headlining Pad)


    1. Remove the No. 2 roof headlining pad.

  12. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING PAD (w/ Roof Headlining Pad)


    1. Remove the roof headlining pad.
