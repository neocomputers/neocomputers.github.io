  10. DISCONNECT ENGINE WIRE (w/o Canister Pump Module)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Do not allow any foreign matter or water to enter the inverter with converter assembly.


    1. Move each lock lever as shown in the illustration and disconnect the 2 inverter with converter assembly connectors.

      Note


      • Do not damage the terminals, connector housing or inverter with converter assembly during disconnection.

      • Cover the hole where the cable was connected with tape (non-residue type) or equivalent to prevent entry of foreign matter.

      • Insulate the disconnected terminals with insulating tape.

      • Do not touch the waterproof seal or terminals of the connector.

    2. Remove the bolt.

    3. Disengage the 2 clamps and disconnect the engine wire.

  49. REMOVE HV FLOOR UNDER WIRE

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Insulate each disconnected high-voltage connector with insulating tape. Wrap the connector from the wire harness side to the end of the connector.


    1. *a Earth Terminal

      Disconnect the 2 HV battery junction block assembly connectors.

    2. Remove the nut and disconnect the earth terminal.

    3. Disengage the 2 claws and open the wire harness clamp.

    4. Disengage the 4 clamps.

    5. Remove the 2 nuts.

    6. Remove the 3 clamps.

      Note

      If the clamps are removed forcibly, the stud bolts may be damaged.

    7. Disengage the grommet and pull out the HV floor under wire from the floor panel hole.

    8. *A for LHD
      *B for RHD

      Remove the clamp (A).

    9. Disengage the clamp (B) to remove the HV floor under wire.

  50. REMOVE HV BATTERY CHARGER WIRE (w/o Solar Charging System)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Insulate the disconnected connectors with insulating tape.


    1. *a Earth Terminal

      Remove the nut (A) and disconnect the earth terminal.

    2. Remove the nut (B).

    3. Disengage the clamp.

    4. Disconnect the HV battery junction block assembly connector.

    5. Disengage the 2 clamps to disconnect the HV battery charger wire.

    6. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Using a screwdriver, slide the green-colored lock of the connector as shown in the illustration to release it and disconnect the inlet AC charger cable connector.

    7. Disengage the clamp.

    8. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Using a screwdriver, slide the green-colored lock of the connector as shown in the illustration to release it and disconnect the 2 electric vehicle charger assembly connectors.

    9. Disengage the 3 clamps to remove the HV battery charger wire.

  51. REMOVE HV BATTERY CHARGER WIRE (w/ Solar Charging System)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.

    Note

    Insulate the disconnected connectors with insulating tape.


    1. *a Earth Terminal

      Remove the nut (A) and disconnect the earth terminal.

    2. Remove the nut (B).

    3. Disengage the clamp.

    4. Disconnect the HV battery junction block assembly connector.

    5. Disengage the 2 clamps to disconnect the HV battery charger wire.

    6. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Using a screwdriver, slide the green-colored lock of the connector as shown in the illustration to release it and disconnect the inlet AC charger cable connector.

    7. Disengage the clamp.

    8. Disengage the 2 clamps.

    9. *a Screwdriver Insertion Position
      Release in this direction.

      Insert the screwdriver at the position shown in the illustration to release the green-colored lock of each connector and disconnect each solar energy control unit connector.

    10. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Using a screwdriver, slide the green-colored lock of the connector as shown in the illustration to release it and disconnect the 2 electric vehicle charger assembly connectors.

    11. Disengage the 3 clamps to remove the HV battery charger wire.
