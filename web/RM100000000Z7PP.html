RELAY ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT DEF RELAY


    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      3 - 4 Auxiliary battery voltage applied to terminals 1 and 2 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 4 Auxiliary battery voltage not applied between terminals Below 1 Ω
      3 - 5 Auxiliary battery voltage applied to terminals 1 and 2 Below 1 Ω
      3 - 5 Auxiliary battery voltage not applied between terminals 10 kΩ or higher
      4 - 5 Always 10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the DEF relay.
