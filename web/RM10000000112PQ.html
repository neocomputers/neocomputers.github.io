FUEL PUMP(w/ Canister Pump Module) INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL FUEL SUCTION TUBE WITH PUMP AND GAUGE ASSEMBLY


    1. Install a new fuel suction tube set gasket to the fuel tank assembly.

    2. Set the fuel suction tube with pump and gauge assembly to the fuel tank assembly.

      Note

      Be careful not to bend the arm of the fuel sender gauge assembly.

    3. *a Protrusion
      Notch

      Align the protrusions of the fuel suction tube with pump and gauge assembly with the notches in the fuel tank assembly.

  2. INSTALL FUEL PUMP GAUGE RETAINER


    1. Install the fuel pump gauge retainer.


      1. While pressing down on the fuel suction tube with pump and gauge assembly, temporarily install the fuel pump gauge retainer.

      2. *a Insertion Point
        *b SST (Plate)
        *c SST (Claw)
        *d SST (Bolt)

        Temporarily install SST (plate) and SST (claw) to the fuel pump gauge retainer.

        SST
        09808-14031   ( 09808-01030, 09808-01050 )
        09808-01110

        Tech Tips

        Securely insert the ends of SST (claw) into the insertion points in the fuel pump gauge retainer.

      3. While firmly pressing SST (claw) into the insertion points in the fuel pump gauge retainer, tighten SST (bolt).

      4. *a Triangle Mark (Fuel Pump Gauge Retainer)
        *b Triangle Mark (Fuel Tank Assembly)
        *c SST (Handle)
        *d SST (Plate)
        *e SST (Claw)
        *f SST (Bolt)
        *g Extension Bar (270 mm)
        Front Side

        Install SST (handle) and an extension bar (270 mm) to SST (plate).

        SST
        09808-14031   ( 09808-01010, 09808-01020 )

      5. Using SST, rotate the fuel pump gauge retainer so that the triangle mark on the fuel pump gauge retainer is aligned with the triangle mark on the fuel tank assembly to install the fuel suction tube with pump and gauge assembly to the fuel tank assembly.

        Note


        • Do not use any tools other than specified as this may result in damage to the fuel pump gauge retainer or fuel tank assembly.

        • Do not press down on SST excessively as this may make the fuel pump gauge retainer hard to rotate, and may damage components.

        • Make sure to rotate SST (handle) horizontally. If it is rotated at an angle, SST may come off.

        • Do not spin SST too fast or use an impact wrench as this may result in damage to components.

        • If SST comes off of the fuel pump gauge retainer, loosen SST (bolt) and reinstall SST.

        • Make sure that the fuel suction tube set gasket does not come off.

    2. Engage the claw to install the No. 1 fuel tube clamp.

  3. CONNECT FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Push the fuel tube joint onto the plug of the fuel suction plate sub-assembly, then install the tube joint clip.

      *1 Fuel Suction Plate Sub-assembly *2 Tube Joint Clip
      *3 Fuel Tank Main Tube Sub-assembly - -
      *a Fuel Tube Joint *b O-ring
      *c Correct *d Incorrect
      *e Collar - -
      Insert Insert

      Note


      • Check that there are no scratches or foreign matter around the connecting parts of the fuel tube joint and plug before performing this work.

      • Check that the fuel tube joint is securely inserted to the end.

      • Check that the tube joint clip is on the collar of the fuel tube joint.

      • After installing the tube joint clip, check that the fuel tank main tube sub-assembly is securely connected by pulling on it.

  4. INSTALL FUEL TANK PRESSURE SENSOR (VAPOR PRESSURE SENSOR)

    Click here

  5. INSTALL REAR FLOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER


    1. Remove any remaining butyl tape from the rear floor service hole cover and vehicle body.

    2. Clean the installation surfaces of the rear floor service hole cover and vehicle body.

    3. Connect the fuel pump connector and fuel tank pressure sensor (vapor pressure sensor) connector.

    4. *a Rear Floor Service Hole Cover (Back Side)
      *b Butyl Tape Adhesion Area

      Install the rear floor service hole cover with new butyl tape.

      Note

      Securely install the rear floor service hole cover.

  6. INSTALL CENTER REAR SEATBACK HINGE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Install the center rear seatback hinge sub-assembly to the vehicle body with the 2 bolts.

      Torque:
      36.8 N*m  { 375 kgf*cm, 27 ft.*lbf }

    2. Engage the clamp to connect the wire harness to the center rear seatback hinge sub-assembly.

    3. Connect the rear seat inner belt assembly LH connector.
