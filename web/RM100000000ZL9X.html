AIR CONDITIONING PRESSURE SENSOR ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT AIR CONDITIONER PRESSURE SENSOR


    1. Check the wire harness.


      1. Disconnect the A22 air conditioner pressure sensor connector.

      2. Disconnect the I60 air conditioning amplifier assembly connector.

      3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        A22-1 (-) - I60-2 (SG-2) Always Below 1 Ω
        A22-2 (PR) - I60-24 (PRE) Always Below 1 Ω
        A22-3 (+) - I60-6 (S5-3) Always Below 1 Ω
        A22-1 (-) or I60-2 (SG-2) - Body ground Always 10 kΩ or higher
        A22-2 (PR) or I60-24 (PRE) - Body ground Always 10 kΩ or higher
        A22-3 (+) or I60-6 (S5-3) - Body ground Always 10 kΩ or higher

        If the resistance is not as specified, repair the wire harness.

      4. Reconnect the I60 air conditioning amplifier assembly connector.

      5. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      6. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Voltage
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        A22-3 (+) - Body ground Power switch on (IG) 4.75 to 5.25 V

        If the voltage is not as specified, repair the wire harness or replace air conditioning amplifier assembly.

    2. Check the air conditioner pressure sensor.


      1. Connect the connector to the air conditioner pressure sensor.

      2. Install a manifold gauge set.

      3. Turn the A/C switch on.

      4. *a

        Component with harness connected

        (Air Conditioning Amplifier Assembly)

        Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Tech Tips

        Check from the rear of the connector while it is connected to the air conditioning amplifier assembly.

        Standard Voltage
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        I60-24 (PRE) - I60-2 (SG-2)

        Refrigerant pressure: Normal pressure (less than 3025 kPa (30.8 kgf/cm2, 439 psi) and more than 176 kPa (1.8 kgf/cm2, 26 psi))

        		 0.74 to 4.61 V

        If the voltage is not as specified, replace the air conditioner pressure sensor.
