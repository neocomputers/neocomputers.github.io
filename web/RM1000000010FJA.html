BACK DOOR COURTESY SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT BACK DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY (BACK DOOR COURTESY LIGHT SWITCH)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Back Door Lock Assembly (Back Door Courtesy Light Switch))
      *b Close (Lock)
      *c Open (Unlock)

      Check the operation of the back door courtesy light switch.


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        2 - 3 Closed (Locked) 10 kΩ or higher
        2 - 3 Open (Unlocked) Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the back door lock assembly (back door courtesy light switch).
