WATER PUMP WITH MOTOR INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL INVERTER WATER PUMP ASSEMBLY


    1. *1 Outlet No. 1 Hybrid Water Pump Hose
      *2 Inlet Hybrid Water Pump Hose
      *a Alignment Mark (Blue)
      *b Alignment Mark (White)
      *c Rib
      *d 2 to 11 mm (0.0787 to 0.433 in.)
      *e Up
      *f Front of Vehicle
      *g RH Side

      Connect the outlet No. 1 hybrid water pump hose and inlet hybrid water pump hose to the inverter water pump assembly and slide the 2 clips to secure them.

      Note

      To prevent foreign matter from entering the inverter water pump assembly and inverter cooling system, do not remove the pieces of cloth from the pipes and disconnected hoses until installation.

      Tech Tips


      • Make sure to align the alignment marks of the hoses with the ribs of the inverter water pump assembly.

      • Make sure that the clips are positioned as shown in the illustration.

    2. Temporarily install the inverter water pump assembly to the fan shroud with the 2 bolts.

    3. Fully tighten the 2 bolts.

      Torque:
      7.0 N*m  { 71 kgf*cm, 62 in.*lbf }

      Note

      As overtightening the bolts may damage the fan shroud, make sure to use a torque wrench.

    4. Connect the inverter water pump assembly connector.

      Note

      If a dustproof cap is installed to the inverter water pump assembly connector, do not remove it until the connector is to be connected.
