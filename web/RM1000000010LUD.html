TOYOTA PARKING ASSIST-SENSOR SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the TOYOTA parking assist-sensor system on.

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar / Trouble Codes.

    6. Check for DTCs.

      Click here


      Body Electrical > IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar > Trouble Codes

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the TOYOTA parking assist-sensor system on.

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar / Trouble Codes.

    6. Clear the DTCs.


      Body Electrical > IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar > Clear DTCs
