CAUTION:

The fuel tank assembly is very heavy. Be sure to follow the procedure described in the repair manual, or the fuel tank assembly may fall off the engine lifter.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE FUEL SUCTION TUBE WITH PUMP AND GAUGE ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. DRAIN FUEL

  3. REMOVE TAIL EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  4. REMOVE NO. 1 FLOOR UNDER COVER ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 3 bolts.

    2. Disengage the clip to remove the No. 1 floor under cover assembly.

  5. REMOVE REAR FLOOR SIDE MEMBER COVER LH


    1. Remove the 3 bolts and clip (A).

    2. Disengage the clip (B) to remove the rear floor side member cover LH.

  6. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TANK PROTECTOR


    1. Remove the 4 clips and No. 1 fuel tank protector from the fuel tank assembly.

  7. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK VENT HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the fuel tank vent hose sub-assembly from the fuel tank solenoid main valve assembly.

      Click here

  8. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK TO FILLER PIPE HOSE


    1. Disconnect the fuel tank to filler pipe hose from the fuel tank assembly.

      Click here

  9. SEPARATE NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and separate the No. 3 parking brake cable assembly.

  10. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the fuel tank main tube sub-assembly from the fuel pipe.

      Click here

  11. REMOVE FUEL TANK ASSEMBLY

    CAUTION:

    The fuel tank assembly is very heavy. Be sure to follow the procedure described in the repair manual, or the fuel tank assembly may fall off the engine lifter.


    1. Support the fuel tank assembly using an engine lifter.

      Tech Tips

      Using height adjustment attachments and plate lift attachments, keep the fuel tank assembly horizontal.

    2. Remove the 4 bolts.

    3. Lower the engine lifter to remove the fuel tank assembly.

      Note


      • Be careful not to drop the fuel tank assembly.

      • When removing the fuel tank assembly, tilt it slightly to prevent it from interfering with the surrounding parts.

  12. REMOVE FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 3 clamps to remove the fuel tank main tube sub-assembly from the fuel tank assembly.

  13. REMOVE FUEL TANK VENT HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 2 clamps to remove the fuel tank vent hose sub-assembly from the fuel tank assembly.

  14. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TANK PROTECTOR SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 3 clips and No. 1 fuel tank protector sub-assembly from the fuel tank assembly.
