CONDENSER ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT COOLER CONDENSER ASSEMBLY


    1. If the cooler condenser assembly fins are dirty, clean them with water and dry them with compressed air.

      Note

      Do not damage the cooler condenser assembly fins.

    2. If any cooler condenser assembly fins are bent, straighten them using a screwdriver or pliers.

  2. INSPECT FOR REFRIGERANT LEAK


    1. Using a halogen leak detector, check the pipe joints for refrigerant leaks.

    2. If a refrigerant leak is detected in a joint, check the torque of the bolt of the joint.
