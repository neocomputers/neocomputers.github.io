FUEL LID OPENER MOTOR ASSEMBLY INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FUEL LID LOCK WITH MOTOR ASSEMBLY


    1. Check the operation of the fuel lid lock with motor assembly (motor operation).


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Fuel Lid Lock with Motor Assembly)
        *b Shaft Stroke
        *c Shaft not pushed
        *d Shaft Pushed
        *e Lever

        Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the fuel lid lock with motor assembly connector, and measure the shaft stroke.

        Standard
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Specified Condition

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

        16.80 mm

        (0.661 in.)

        If the result is not as specified, replace the fuel lid lock with motor assembly.

    2. Check the operation of the fuel lid lock with motor assembly (fuel lid courtesy switch). (w/ Canister Pump Module)


      1. Measure the resistance according the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        2 - 3

        Shaft not pushed (ON)

        (Shaft stroke is below 0.73 mm (0.029 in.).)

        		 Below 1 Ω
        2 - 3

        Shaft pushed (OFF)

        (Shaft stroke is more than 0.73 mm (0.029 in.).)

        		 10 kΩ or higher

        If the result is not as specified, replace the fuel lid lock with motor assembly.
