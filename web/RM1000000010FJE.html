PROCEDURE

  2. REMOVE FENDER APRON MUDGUARD SEAL SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the clip.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the clip to remove the fender apron mudguard seal sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.

  3. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape around the headlight assembly as shown in the illustration.

    2. *a Connector Lock Lever
      Disconnect in this Direction

      Disengage the claw, pull down the connector lock lever as shown in the illustration and disconnect the connector.

    3. *a Bolt
      *b Screw

      Remove the bolt and 3 screws.

    4. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the guide to remove the headlight assembly as shown in the illustration.
