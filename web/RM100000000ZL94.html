  10. REMOVE SUCTION HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and suction hose sub-assembly.

    2. Remove the 2 O-rings from the suction hose sub-assembly.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering them.

  11. REMOVE INLET NO. 1 AIR CLEANER


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and inlet No. 1 air cleaner.

  12. DISCONNECT DISCHARGE HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the discharge hose sub-assembly from the compressor with motor assembly.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the discharge hose sub-assembly.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering them.

  13. DISCONNECT NO. 1 DISCHARGE HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the No. 1 discharge hose sub-assembly from the compressor with motor assembly.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the No. 1 discharge hose sub-assembly.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering them.
