CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips


  • Inspect the fuses for circuits related to this system before performing the following procedure.

  • The steering wheel heater unit is built into the steering wheel assembly which cannot be disassembled. Therefore, when the steering wheel heater unit has a malfunction, replace the steering wheel assembly.
