LIGHTING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B243B, B243C

DTC Code DTC Name
B243B LED Wiring for High Beam LH Circuit
B243C LED Wiring for High Beam RH Circuit

DESCRIPTION

If a headlight unit high beam output abnormal signal from the headlight unit is detected by the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH, DTC B243B and B243C are stored.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Note
B243B LED Wiring for High Beam LH Circuit Wiring for high beam LH circuit has a malfunction

  • Headlight unit assembly LH

  • Headlight unit LH

  • No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH

 w/ Adaptive High Beam System
B243C LED Wiring for High Beam RH Circuit Wiring for high beam RH circuit has a malfunction

  • Headlight unit assembly RH

  • Headlight unit RH

  • No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly RH

 w/ Adaptive High Beam System

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

If the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH has been replaced, it is necessary to synchronize the vehicle information and initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.
