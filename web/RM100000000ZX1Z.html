AIR CLEANER FILTER ELEMENT REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. SEPARATE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Separate the 2 clamp hooks from the air cleaner cap sub-assembly.

    2. *a Portion A

      Separate the air cleaner cap sub-assembly from the air cleaner case sub-assembly.

      Note

      Make sure that the portion A of the wire harness does not contact with the No. 1 engine cover sub-assembly when lifting up the air cleaner cap sub-assembly.

  2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER FILTER ELEMENT SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly from the air cleaner case sub-assembly.

      Note

      Do not allow foreign matter to enter the air cleaner cap sub-assembly and air cleaner case sub-assembly.

  3. INSPECT AIR CLEANER FILTER ELEMENT SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Tech Tips

    This procedure is only performed when the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly will be reused.


    1. Check whether the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly is dirty or clogged.

    2. *a Air Cleaner Filter Element Sub-assembly Intake Downstream Side (Air Cleaner Cap Sub-assembly Side)

      If the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly is dirty or has foreign matter adhered to it, clean the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly with compressed air.

      Note

      When cleaning the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly, blow the compressed air from the intake downstream side (from the air cleaner cap sub-assembly side).

      Tech Tips

      If the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly is still extremely dirty after cleaning it, replace the air cleaner filter element sub-assembly with a new one.
