OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY RH


    1. Check the operation of the mirror surface.

      Note

      If the mirror surface is fully turned to the right, left, upward or downward position, the motor slips and produces a clicking noise. This is not a malfunction.


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Outer Rear View Mirror Assembly RH)

        Disconnect the outer rear view mirror assembly RH connector.

      2. Apply auxiliary battery voltage and check the operation of the mirror surface.

        OK
        Auxiliary battery Connection Specified Condition

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 6 (MV)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 5 (M+)

        		 Turns upward

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 5 (M+)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 6 (MV)

        		 Turns downward

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 4 (MH)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 5 (M+)

        		 Turns left

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 5 (M+)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 4 (MH)

        		 Turns right

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly RH.

    2. Check the operation of the mirror heater.


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Outer Rear View Mirror Assembly RH)

        Disconnect the outer rear view mirror assembly RH connector.

      2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        2 (+) - 8 (-) 25°C (77°F) 3.57 to 4.83 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly RH.

      3. Connect a cable from the positive (+) auxiliary battery terminal to terminal 2 and the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal to terminal 8, then check that the mirror becomes warm.

        Tech Tips

        It takes a short time for the mirror to become warm.

        OK
        Mirror becomes warm.

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly RH.

    3. Check the operation of the retractable mirror.

      Note


      • Disconnect and reconnect the auxiliary battery between each mirror position check.

      • The mirror position cannot be changed manually when the auxiliary battery is connected. To change the mirror position manually, the auxiliary battery must be disconnected first.

      • If the motor is kept energized, even after each mirror position check, it may lead to a motor malfunction. Make sure to disconnect the auxiliary battery immediately after performing each mirror position check.


      1. *a (A) Forward position
        *b (B)
        *c (C) Driving position
        *d (D)
        *e (E) Retracted position
        *f

        Component without harness connected

        ((Outer Rear View Mirror Assembly RH))

        Disconnect the outer rear view mirror assembly RH connector.

      2. For each position: Disconnect the auxiliary battery, set the mirror position by hand, connect the auxiliary battery, and check the retractable mirror movement.

        OK
        Auxiliary battery Connection Condition Specified Condition

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        		 Forward position (A) Moves from (A) to (E)

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        		 Forward position (A) Does not move

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        		 Position between forward position (A) and driving position (C) Moves from (B) to (E)

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        		 Position between forward position (A) and driving position (C) Moves from (B) to (A)

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        		 Driving position (C) Moves from (C) to (E)

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        		 Driving position (C) Does not move

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        		 Position between driving position (C) and retracted position (E) Moves from (D) to (E)

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        		 Position between driving position (C) and retracted position (E) Moves from (D) to (C)

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        		 Retracted position (E) Does not move

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 9 (MF)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 3 (MR)

        		 Retracted position (E) Moves from (E) to (C)

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly RH.
