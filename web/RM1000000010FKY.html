PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 HEADLIGHT ECU SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Note

    Make sure to replace the headlight gasket with a new one. Failure to do so may cause water ingress.


    1. Remove the 3 screws.

    2. Separate the connector cover from the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly.

    3. Disconnect the 2 connectors to remove the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly.

      Note

      If the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly has been struck or dropped, replace it with a new one.

  3. REMOVE HEADLIGHT GASKET

    Note

    Make sure to replace the headlight gasket with a new one. Failure to do so may cause water ingress.


    1. Remove the headlight gasket.
