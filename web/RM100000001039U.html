  5. REMOVE RADIATOR SUPPORT OPENING COVER

  6. REMOVE HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. *a Protective Tape

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, remove the hood lock nut cap.

    3. *1 Hood Lock Bolt

      Remove the 2 bolts and hood lock bolt.

    4. Disconnect the hood lock control cable assembly to remove the hood lock assembly.

  7. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

  8. DISCONNECT HOOD LOCK CONTROL LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  9. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the hood lock control cable assembly to remove the hood lock control lever sub-assembly.

  10. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY (for LHD)


    1. Using a screwdriver, disengage the 4 clamps and grommet as shown in the illustration.

      *1 Hood Lock Control Cable Assembly - -
      *a Clamp *b Grommet

      Tech Tips

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Pull the hood lock control cable assembly from the engine compartment to remove it.
