Tech Tips

The oil pump with motor assembly cannot be activated only with the OIL PMP relay on. It is activated after receiving the command signals (OPM1) from the hybrid vehicle control ECU.

CONFIRMATION DRIVING PATTERN

Tech Tips

After repair has been completed, clear the DTC and then check that the vehicle has returned to normal by performing the following All Readiness check procedure.

Click here


  1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

  2. Turn the power switch on (IG) and turn the GTS on.

  3. Clear the DTCs (even if no DTCs are stored, perform the clear DTC procedure).

  4. Turn the power switch on (IG) and turn the GTS on.

  5. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Hybrid Control / Active Test / T/M Cooler Oil Pump.

  6. According to the display on the GTS, perform the Active Test and set the duty to 90 %.

    Tech Tips

    The Active Test cannot be performed if the duty ratio is set to 50 % or lower or 95 % or higher, or if the ambient temperature is -10 °C (14 °F) or lower.

  7. According to the display on the GTS, read the Data List and check that the value of "Motor/Generator Cooling Oil Pump Motor Revolution" is between 500 rpm and 7000 rpm.
