FUEL TANK CAP(w/ Canister Pump Module) INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FUEL TANK CAP ASSEMBLY


    1. *a Gasket

      Visually check that the fuel tank cap assembly and gasket are not deformed or damaged.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the fuel tank cap assembly.

    2. *a ID Mark

      Confirm that ID mark Z is printed on the fuel tank cap assembly.

      CAUTION:

      Make sure to use a fuel tank cap assembly that has the same ID mark, or a malfunction may occur in the fuel system.
