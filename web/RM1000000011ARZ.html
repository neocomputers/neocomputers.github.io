METER / GAUGE SYSTEM Operating Light Control Rheostat does not Change Light Brightness

DESCRIPTION

The combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate) receives signals from this circuit to adjust the illumination of the combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate). The combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate) sets the illumination level based on the operation of the light control rheostat up or down switch in the combination switch assembly.

WIRING DIAGRAM

*1 Light Control Rheostat Up Switch
*2 Light Control Rheostat Down Switch
*3 Combination Switch Assembly
*4 Combination Meter Assembly (Meter Circuit Plate)

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

When replacing the combination meter assembly, always replace it with a new one. If a combination meter assembly which was installed to another vehicle is used, the information stored in it will not match the information from the vehicle and a DTC may be stored.

Tech Tips


  • The meter illumination level can be adjusted by pressing the light control rheostat switch.

  • The meter illumination dims when the taillights are turned on.

  • Setting the meter illumination level to maximum brightness cancels the above dimming of the meter illumination.
