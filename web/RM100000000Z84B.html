ROOF ANTENNA REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE ROOF ANTENNA ASSEMBLY WITH COVER


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove the bolt.

    3. Remove in this Direction

      Pull the washer and holder as shown in the illustration and remove the roof antenna assembly with cover.

  3. REMOVE ROOF ANTENNA ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

      Pull the roof antenna assembly in the direction indicated by the arrow (1) shown in the illustration to disengage the 5 claws and 2 guides.

    2. Pull the roof antenna assembly in the direction indicated by the arrow (2) shown in the illustration to disengage the guide and remove the roof antenna assembly.

    3. When reusing the roof antenna assembly:


      1. Remove the seal.
