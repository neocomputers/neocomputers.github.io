FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY(for Manual Seat) DISASSEMBLY

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

CAUTION:

Wear protective gloves. Sharp areas on the parts may injure your hands.

Tech Tips


  • Use the same procedure for RHD and LHD vehicles.

  • The following procedure is for LHD vehicles.

  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  • The following procedure is for the LH side.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE SEAT SLIDE POSITION SENSOR PROTECTOR (for Driver Side)


    1. w/ Airbag Cut Off Switch:

      Click here

  2. REMOVE SEAT POSITION AIRBAG SENSOR (for Driver Side)


    1. w/ Airbag Cut Off Switch:

      Click here

  3. REMOVE OCCUPANT DETECTION ECU (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. w/ Occupant Classification System:

      Click here

  4. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY (for Driver Side)

    Click here

  5. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY (for Front Passenger Side)

    Click here

  6. DISCONNECT SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK COVER


    1. Disconnect the rubber band of the separate type front seatback cover.

  7. REMOVE VERTICAL ADJUSTING HANDLE (for Driver Side)


    1. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this direction

      Operate the vertical adjusting handle and using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claw and remove the vertical adjusting handle as shown in the illustration.

  8. REMOVE RECLINING HINGE COVER


    1. *a Protective Tape

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claw.

    2. Disengage the guide to remove the reclining hinge cover.

  9. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER RELEASE HANDLE


    1. Remove the reclining adjuster release handle.

  10. REMOVE SEAT ADJUSTER COVER CAP (for Driver Side)


    1. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this direction (1)
      Remove in this direction (2)

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 claws as shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the seat adjuster cover cap as shown in the illustration.

  11. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD (for Driver Side)


    1. Disengage the 2 claws.

    2. Disengage the claw and 2 guides.

    3. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this direction

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the 2 claws as shown in the illustration.

    4. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the guide as shown in the illustration.

    5. Disengage the 2 clamps.

    6. Disconnect the front lumbar power seat switch connector to remove the front seat cushion shield.

  12. REMOVE FRONT LUMBAR POWER SEAT SWITCH (for Driver Side)

    Click here

  13. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER CUSHION SHIELD (for Driver Side)


    1. Disengage the 2 claws.

    2. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and 2 guides to remove the front seat inner cushion shield as shown in the illustration.

  14. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. Disengage the 2 claws.

    2. Disengage the claw and 2 guides.

    3. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this direction

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claw as shown in the illustration.

    4. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the guide to remove the front seat cushion shield as shown in the illustration.

  15. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER CUSHION SHIELD (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. Disengage the 3 claws.

    2. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and 2 guides to remove the front seat inner cushion shield as shown in the illustration.

  16. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 2 hooks.

    2. for Driver Side:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Disengage the clamp.

      3. Disconnect the connector.

      4. Disengage the clamp.

    3. for Front Passenger Side:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Disengage the clamp.

    4. Disengage the claw and 4 clamps to separate the front seat airbag assembly wire harness.

      Tech Tips

      Disengage the 4 clamps first.

    5. Remove the 4 bolts.

    6. Disengage the 4 guides to remove the separate type front seatback assembly from the separate type front seat cushion assembly.

  17. REMOVE FRONT SEAT HEADREST SUPPORT


    1. Disengage the 3 hooks.

    2. Disengage the clamp.

    3. Open the 2 fasteners.

    4. w/ Occupant Classification System:


      1. Remove in this direction

        Disengage the hook as shown in the illustration.

      2. *1 Seatback Cover Hook
        *a Pin

        Pull up the pin of the seatback cover hook as shown in the illustration and remove the seatback cover hook.

      3. *1 Seat Cover Hanger
        *a Bracket
        Remove in this direction

        Slide the bracket as shown in the illustration to disengage the hook and disconnect the bracket from the seat cover hanger.

    5. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the 2 front seat headrest supports.

  18. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK COVER WITH PAD


    1. Remove the separate type front seatback cover with pad.

  19. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK COVER


    1. Using a screwdriver, disengage the 17 claws and remove the separate type front seatback cover from the separate type front seatback pad as shown in the illustration.

      *a Screwdriver - -

    Click here

  21. REMOVE FRONT SEAT AIRBAG ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  23. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK SPRING SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the 2 front seatback edge protectors from the front seatback frame sub-assembly.

    2. Remove in this direction

      Remove the front seatback spring sub-assembly RH from the front seatback frame sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.

  24. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK EDGE PROTECTOR (for Driver Side)


    1. Remove the 2 front seatback edge protectors from the lumbar support adjuster assembly LH.

  25. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK EDGE PROTECTOR (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. Remove the 2 front seatback edge protectors from the front seatback spring sub-assembly RH.

  26. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK HOOK


    1. Remove the 2 front seatback hooks from the front seatback frame sub-assembly.

  27. REMOVE FRONT SEAT NO. 2 WIRE (for Driver Side)


    1. Disengage the clamp to remove the front seat No. 2 wire.

  28. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER WITH PAD


    1. for Driver Side:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

      2. Disengage the clamp.

    2. for Front Passenger Side:


      1. Disengage the claw and 4 clamps.

        Tech Tips

        Disengage the 4 clamps first.

    3. Disengage the 2 hooks.

    4. Disengage the 10 hooks and remove the separate type front seat cushion cover with pad.

  29. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER


    1. Using a screwdriver, disengage the 13 claws and remove the separate type front seat cushion cover from the separate type front seat cushion pad as shown in the illustration.

      *a Screwdriver - -

  32. REMOVE LH SEAT RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER LH (for Driver Side)


    1. Disengage the claw.

    2. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the LH seat reclining adjuster inside cover LH.

  33. REMOVE LH SEAT RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER RH (for Driver Side)


    1. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the LH seat reclining adjuster inside cover RH.

  34. REMOVE RH SEAT RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER LH (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. w/ Occupant Classification System:


      1. Disengage the clamp.

    2. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the RH seat reclining adjuster inside cover LH.

  35. REMOVE RH SEAT RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER RH (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the RH seat reclining adjuster inside cover RH.

  36. REMOVE FRONT SEAT LOWER CUSHION SHIELD LH (for Driver Side)


    1. *a Hook
      *b Guide

      Disengage the hook.

    2. Disengage the claw and guide to remove the front seat lower cushion shield LH.

  37. REMOVE FRONT SEAT LOWER CUSHION SHIELD RH (for Driver Side)


    1. *a Hook
      *b Guide

      Disengage the hook.

    2. Disengage the claw and guide to remove the front seat lower cushion shield RH.

  38. REMOVE FRONT SEAT WIRE LH (for Driver Side)


    1. Disconnect each connector and disengage each clamp to remove the front seat wire LH.

  39. REMOVE FRONT SEAT NO. 2 WIRE LH (for Driver Side)


    1. w/ Airbag Cut Off Switch:


      1. Disengage the clamp.

      2. Disengage the claw to remove the front seat No. 2 wire LH.

  40. REMOVE FRONT SEAT WIRE RH (for Front Passenger Side)


    1. w/ Occupant Classification System:


      1. Disconnect each connector and disengage each clamp to remove the front seat wire RH.

  41. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION WIRE HARNESS HOLDER


    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Disengage the claw to remove the front seat cushion wire harness holder.

  42. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SPRING SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 6 hooks to remove the front seat cushion spring sub-assembly from the front seat adjuster assembly.

  43. REMOVE SEAT TRACK ADJUSTING HANDLE


    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the seat track adjusting handle from the front seat adjuster assembly.

  44. REMOVE FRONT SEAT VERTICAL ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY (for Driver Side)


    1. Remove the front seat vertical adjuster assembly.


      1. Remove the 3 nuts and vertical adjuster plate.

      2. Remove the vertical adjuster spacer.

      3. Remove the front vertical adjuster brake sub-assembly.

  45. REMOVE VERTICAL ADJUSTER BRACKET COVER (for Driver Side)


    1. Disengage the claw to remove the vertical adjuster bracket cover from the front vertical adjuster brake sub-assembly.
