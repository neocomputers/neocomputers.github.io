      OK

      Tech Tips

      If the vehicle is turned on a turntable with the power switch on (ACC) or on (IG), the system may store the angular velocity. As a result, the vehicle position cursor may deviate. In this case, the vehicle position cursor will return to normal by turning the power switch off and back to ON again with the vehicle completely stopped.
