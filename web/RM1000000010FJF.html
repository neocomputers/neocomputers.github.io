  5. REMOVE HEADLIGHT LENS

    Note


    • Make sure to wear clean rubber gloves when performing this procedure.

    • Do not allow dirt or foreign matter to get on the headlight lens.

    • If there are any fingerprints or foreign matter on the back of the headlight lens, wipe them off with a soft cloth.


    1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.

    2. Screw
      "TORX" screw

      Remove the 9 screws.

    3. Using a T20H "TORX" driver, remove the "TORX" screw.

    4. Using a hair dryer, heat the backside of the headlight assembly.

      Note

      If the headlight assembly is heated unevenly, it will deform or melt.

    5. Disengage the 7 claws.

    6. Remove the headlight lens from the housing.

      Tech Tips

      If the headlight lens cannot be removed even after heating, using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, lift the headlight lens. Be careful not to damage the headlight lens and housing.

  6. REMOVE HEADLIGHT LENS GASKET


    1. Remove the headlight lens gasket from the housing.

      Note

      Make sure to replace the headlight lens gasket with a new one. Failure to do so may cause water ingress.

  7. REMOVE HEADLIGHT UNIT (w/o Adaptive High Beam System)

    Note


    • Make sure to wear clean rubber gloves when performing this procedure.

    • Do not allow dirt or foreign matter to get on the headlight unit or other components.

    • If there are any fingerprints or foreign matter on the headlight unit or other components, wipe them off with a soft cloth.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the pivot collar.

    3. Turn in this Direction

      While holding the headlight unit by hand, turn the aiming screws as shown in the illustration until the aiming screws are disconnected from the headlight unit.

      Tech Tips

      Make sure to remember the number of aiming screw rotations.

    4. Remove the headlight unit from the housing.

  8. REMOVE HEADLIGHT UNIT (w/ Adaptive High Beam System)

    Note


    • Make sure to wear clean rubber gloves when performing this procedure.

    • Do not allow dirt or foreign matter to get on the headlight unit or other components.

    • If there are any fingerprints or foreign matter on the headlight unit or other components, wipe them off with a soft cloth.


    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    2. Disengage the clamp.

    3. Remove the 2 screws.

    4. Disengage the guide.

    5. Turn in this Direction

      While holding the headlight unit by hand, turn the aiming screws as shown in the illustration until the aiming screws are disconnected from the headlight unit.

      Tech Tips

      Make sure to remember the number of aiming screw rotations.

    6. Remove the headlight unit from the housing.
