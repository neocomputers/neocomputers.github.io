ELECTRONICALLY CONTROLLED BRAKE SYSTEM CHECK FOR INTERMITTENT PROBLEMS


  1. CHECK FOR INTERMITTENT PROBLEMS

    Tech Tips

    A momentary interruption (open circuit) in the connectors and/or wire harnesses between the sensors and ECUs can be detected using the Data List function of the GTS.


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Follow the directions on the GTS to display the Data List and select areas where momentary interruptions should be monitored.

      Tech Tips


      • A momentary interruption (open circuit) cannot be detected for 3 seconds after the power switch is turned on (IG) (initial check).

      • If Error remains displayed for one of the items on the GTS, check for continuity between the ECU and the item for which Error is displayed.

      • If a momentary interruption occurs and Error is displayed on the GTS, Error will remain displayed on the GTS for 1 second after the circuit returns to normal.

    6. While observing the screen, gently jiggle the connectors or wire harnesses between the ECUs and sensors, or between ECUs.

      Tech Tips

      If the display changes, this indicates that there has been a momentary interruption (open circuit) in the connector and/or wire harness. In this case, repair or replace the connectors and/or wire harnesses as one of them is faulty.
