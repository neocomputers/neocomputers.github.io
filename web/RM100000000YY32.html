SPEED LIMITER SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. CHECK SPEED LIMITER INDICATOR AND MULTI-INFORMATION DISPLAY


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    2. *1 Speed Limiter Switch

      Check that the speed limiter indicator turns on when the speed limiter is turned on using the speed limiter switch and that the speed limiter indicator turns off when the speed limiter is turned off using the speed limiter switch.

    3. Check that the status of the speed limiter operation appears on the main display or multi-information display.
