BRAKE BOOSTER PUMP(for LHD) INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT BRAKE BOOSTER PUMP ASSEMBLY


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Brake Booster Pump Assembly)

      Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      2 (BM1) - 4 (GND1) Always Below 10 Ω
      1 (BM2) - 3 (GND2) Always Below 10 Ω
      2 (BM1) - 1 (BM2) Always Below 1 Ω
      4 (GND1) - 3 (GND2) Always Below 1 Ω
