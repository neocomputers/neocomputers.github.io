REAR TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY DISASSEMBLY

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips


  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  • The following procedure is for the LH side.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE BACK-UP LIGHT BULB


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Turn the back-up light socket with the back-up light bulb as shown in the illustration to remove them as a unit.

    2. Remove the back-up light bulb from the back-up light socket.

  2. REMOVE BACK-UP LIGHT SOCKET

  3. REMOVE REAR FOG LIGHT LED (w/ Rear Fog Light)


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Using a T20 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the screw.

    2. Turn the rear fog light LED as shown in the illustration to remove it.

  4. REMOVE REAR FOG LIGHT GASKET (w/ Rear Fog Light)


    1. Remove the rear fog light gasket.

      Note

      After the rear fog light gasket is removed, be sure to replace it with a new one. Failure to do so may cause water ingress.

  5. REMOVE REAR TURN SIGNAL LIGHT LENS AND BODY
