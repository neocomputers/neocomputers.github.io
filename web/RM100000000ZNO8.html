ACCUMULATOR DISASSEMBLY

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

As a pipe may deform or its joint may be damaged if force is applied to the pipe, do not carry the accumulator and accessory assembly by a pipe or apply excessive force to a pipe while performing work.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 3 RECEIVER TANK BRACKET


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 3 receiver tank bracket.

  2. REMOVE NO. 2 RECEIVER TANK BRACKET


    1. Disengage the 2 clamps.

    2. Remove the 4 bolts.

    3. Remove in this Direction

      Remove the air conditioning receiver tank with bracket as shown in the illustration.

    4. Remove the O-ring from the No. 1 connector tube.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

    5. Remove the 4 bolts, No. 2 receiver tank bracket and 2 receiver holders.

    6. Remove the 2 bushes.

    7. Remove the 2 No. 1 cooler cushions.

    8. Remove the 2 No. 2 cooler cushions.

  3. REMOVE NO. 1 COOLER REFRIGERANT SUCTION PIPE


    1. Remove the bolt and No. 1 cooler refrigerant suction pipe.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the No. 1 cooler refrigerant suction pipe.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

  4. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING RECEIVER TANK


    1. Remove the bolt and air conditioning receiver tank.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the No. 1 connector tube.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

  5. REMOVE NO. 6 COOLER BRACKET


    1. Remove the bolt and No. 6 cooler bracket.

  6. REMOVE NO. 1 RECEIVER TANK BRACKET


    1. Loosen the bolt.

    2. Disengage the clamp.

    3. Remove the 6 bolts and No. 1 receiver tank bracket with No. 5 cooler bracket.

    4. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 1 receiver tank bracket from the No. 5 cooler bracket.

    5. Remove the 2 bushes.

    6. Remove the 2 No. 1 cooler cushions.

    7. Remove the 2 No. 2 cooler cushions.

  7. REMOVE COOLER REFRIGERANT LIQUID PIPE A


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and cooler refrigerant liquid pipe A.

    2. Remove the 2 O-rings from the cooler refrigerant liquid pipe A.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

  8. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CONDITIONING ACCESSORY ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  9. REMOVE NO. 3 CONNECTOR TUBE


    1. Remove the bolt and No. 3 connector tube.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the No. 3 connector tube.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

  10. REMOVE LOW PRESSURE MAGNETIC VALVE

    Click here

  11. REMOVE HIGH PRESSURE MAGNETIC VALVE

    Click here

  12. REMOVE VALVE TO CONNECTOR TUBE


    1. Remove the bolt and valve to connector tube.

    2. Remove the O-ring from the No. 5 connector tube.

      Note

      Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.
