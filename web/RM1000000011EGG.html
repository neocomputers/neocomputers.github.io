PARKING ASSIST MAIN SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT S-IPA SWITCH (COMBINATION SWITCH ASSEMBLY)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (S-IPA Switch (Combination Switch Assembly))

      Make sure that there is no looseness in the locking part and the connecting part of the connector.

    2. Disconnect the S-IPA switch (combination switch assembly) connector.

    3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      5 (IG) - 7 (E) Switch pushed Below 50 Ω
      Switch not pushed 10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the S-IPA switch (combination switch assembly).

    4. Check that the switch illumination illuminates.


      1. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (S-IPA Switch (Combination Switch Assembly))

        Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the S-IPA switch (combination switch assembly) and check that the switch illumination illuminates.

        OK
        Connection Specified Condition

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 10 (ILL+)

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1 (ILL-)

        		 Illuminates

        If the result is not as specified, replace the S-IPA switch (combination switch assembly).
