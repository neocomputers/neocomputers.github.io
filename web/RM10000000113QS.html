SHIFT CONTROL ACTUATOR REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE SHIFT CONTROL ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the shift control actuator assembly connector.

    2. Remove the 3 shift control actuator bolts and shift control actuator assembly from the hybrid vehicle transaxle assembly.

      Tech Tips

      As the position of the shift control actuator assembly is detected automatically, initialization is not necessary after disconnecting and reconnecting the cable to the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal.
