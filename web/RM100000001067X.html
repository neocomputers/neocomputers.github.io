  1. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM HEAD CAP (for LHD, RHD Type A)

    Click here

  2. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM AND BLADE ASSEMBLY LH (for LHD, RHD Type A)

    Click here

  3. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM AND BLADE ASSEMBLY RH (for LHD, RHD Type A)

    Click here

  4. REMOVE COWL WATER EXTRACT SHIELD LH (for LHD, RHD Type A)

    Click here

  5. REMOVE COWL WATER EXTRACT SHIELD RH (for LHD, RHD Type A)

    Click here

  6. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (for LHD, RHD Type A)

    Click here

  7. SEPARATE CENTER NO. 1 COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER (for RHD Type B)


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape to the area shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the 3 claws and separate the hood to cowl top seal from the cowl top ventilator louver.

    3. Disengage the 2 claws and 8 guides and separate the center No. 1 cowl top ventilator louver.

  8. REMOVE IGNITION COIL ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the 4 ignition coil assembly connectors.

    2. Remove the 4 bolts and 4 ignition coil assemblies from the cylinder head cover sub-assembly.

      Note

      If an ignition coil assembly has been struck or dropped, replace it.

      Tech Tips

      Arrange the removed parts in the correct order.
