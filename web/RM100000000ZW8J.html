FUEL VAPOR CONTAINMENT VALVE(w/ Canister Pump Module) INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FUEL VAPOR CONTAINMENT VALVE (FUEL TANK SOLENOID MAIN VALVE ASSEMBLY)


    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 20°C (68°F) 25 to 30 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the fuel vapor containment valve (fuel tank solenoid main valve assembly).
