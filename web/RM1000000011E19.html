WIRELESS CHARGING SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. WIRELESS CHARGING SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


    1. *a Mobile Wireless Charger Switch
      *b Indicator Light (Green/Amber)
      *c Charging Area

      Turn the power switch on (READY, IG or ACC).

      Tech Tips

      If the power switch is turned off when the wireless charging system is on, the wireless charging system will automatically turn on the next time the power switch is turned on (READY, IG or ACC).

    2. Turn the wireless charging system on and check that the indicator light (green) illuminates.

    3. Place a rechargeable device on the charging area and check that the indicator light (amber) illuminates, indicating charging is in progress.

      Tech Tips

      For details about Qi-compatible rechargeable devices

      Click here

    4. Check that the indicator light (green) illuminates when charging is complete.

      Tech Tips

      Some mobile devices, cases or accessories may not cause the indicator (green) to illuminate when charging is complete, even though they are Qi-compatible. Check the mobile device to confirm the charge status.

    5. Turn the wireless charging system off and check that the indicator light turns off.
