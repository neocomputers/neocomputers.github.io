DYNAMIC RADAR CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:C1A52

DTC Code DTC Name
C1A52 Driving Support ECU Communication Abnormal

DESCRIPTION

DTC C1A52 is stored when the hybrid vehicle control ECU cannot recognize the driving support ECU assembly.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1A52 Driving Support ECU Communication Abnormal Approximately 17 seconds after the power switch is turned on (IG), the hybrid vehicle control ECU cannot recognize the driving support ECU assembly for approximately 1.3 seconds or more.

  • Hybrid vehicle control ECU

  • Driving support ECU assembly

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note


  • When replacing the driving support ECU assembly, always replace it with a new one. If a driving support ECU assembly which was installed to another vehicle is used, the information stored in the driving support ECU assembly will not match the information from the vehicle. As a result, a DTC may be stored.

  • Before replacing thehybrid vehicle control ECU, refer to Service Bulletin.
