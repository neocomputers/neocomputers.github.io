CHARGE INLET ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT CABLE EV CHARGER LOCK ASSEMBLY (for Type A)


    1. Charging connector lock operation inspection


      1. *a Locked
        *b Unlocked
        *c Push Lifter
        *d Charging Connector Lock
        *e Lock Pin

        Press the push lifter and check that the lock pin of the charging connector lock operates.

        CAUTION:

        Do not place your hand or fingers under the lock pin while performing this inspection as they may be pinched when the lock pin operates.

        Tech Tips


        • Make sure to perform this inspection with all of the doors locked and while carrying the electrical key transmitter sub-assembly (electric key) within the charging port lid unlock detection area.

        • Confirm that the auxiliary battery is normal before performing this inspection.
