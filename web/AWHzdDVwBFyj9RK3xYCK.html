FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER DISPOSAL

PROCEDURE


  1. DISPOSE OF FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY

    CAUTION:


    • Always use a cloth to prevent shards of metal flying about due to the release of pressurized gas.

    • Always wear safety glasses.

    Tech Tips

    The gas is colorless, odorless and non-poisonous.


    1. except Rough Road Area Specification Vehicles


      1. Place the front shock absorber horizontally with the piston rod extended, and using a hacksaw, make a hole between (A) and (B), and between (C) and (D) shown in the illustration to discharge the gas inside.

    2. for Rough Road Area Specification Vehicles


      1. Place the front shock absorber horizontally with the piston rod extended, and using a hacksaw, make a hole between (A) and (B) shown in the illustration to discharge the gas inside.
