PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY LH


    1. Remove the nut and front stabilizer link assembly LH from the front shock absorber assembly.

      Note

      Do not damage the boot of the ball joint.

      Tech Tips

      If the ball joint turns together with the nut, use a 6 mm hexagon socket wrench to hold the stud bolt.

  3. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY RH

    Tech Tips

    Perform the same procedure as for the LH side.

  4. REMOVE STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  5. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 STABILIZER BRACKET LH


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and front stabilizer bracket LH from the front suspension crossmember sub-assembly.

  6. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 STABILIZER BRACKET RH

    Tech Tips

    Perform the same procedure as for the LH side.

  7. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER BAR


    1. Remove the front stabilizer bar with 2 front stabilizer bar bushings from the front suspension crossmember sub-assembly.

  8. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER BAR BUSHING LH


    1. Remove the front stabilizer bar bushing LH from the front stabilizer bar.

  9. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER BAR BUSHING RH

    Tech Tips

    Perform the same procedure as for the LH side.
