SEAT BELT WARNING SYSTEM(w/o Occupant Classification System) Front Passenger Side Seat Belt Warning Light does not Operate

DESCRIPTION

The occupant detection sensor (separate type front seat cushion pad (front passenger seat)) detects if the passenger seat is occupied and sends signals to the combination meter assembly. The front seat inner belt assembly (front passenger seat) detects the passenger seat belt condition and sends signals to the combination meter assembly. After receiving the signals from the occupant detection sensor (separate type front seat cushion pad (front passenger seat)) and front seat inner belt assembly (front passenger seat), the seat belt warning light on the combination meter assembly will blink or go off.

WIRING DIAGRAM

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips

The seat belt warning light on the combination meter assembly is used for both the driver seat and front passenger seat.Check that the operation of the seat belt warning light is normal first.

Click here

If the seat belt warning light does not operate for both the driver seat and front passenger seat, replace the meter circuit plate.
