  13. REMOVE FRONT PILLAR GARNISH LH


    1. Protective Tape

      Apply protective tape around the front pillar garnish LH as shown in the illustration.

    2. *1 Front Pillar Garnish Clip
      Place Hand Here
      Remove in this Direction

      Pull the upper part of the front pillar garnish LH toward the inside of the cabin to disengage the clip and the front pillar garnish LH from the base of the front pillar garnish clip.

      Tech Tips

      Let the front pillar garnish LH hang from the front pillar garnish clip.

    3. *1 Front Pillar Garnish Clip
      Remove in this Direction

      While pushing the tabs of the front pillar garnish clip as shown in the illustration, disengage it.

      Note


      • The front pillar garnish clip is reusable if it is not damaged.

      • If the front pillar garnish clip is damaged, replace it with a new one.

      Tech Tips

      When the front pillar garnish clip cannot be disengaged easily:


      1. Move in this Direction

        While pushing the tab of the front pillar garnish clip shown in the illustration with your finger, move the front pillar garnish clip as shown in the illustration.

      2. Pull in this Direction

        While pulling the front pillar garnish clip as shown in the illustration, push the tab of the front pillar garnish clip shown in the illustration with a screwdriver and disengage the front pillar garnish clip.

    4. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 guides to remove the front pillar garnish LH as shown in the illustration.

    5. *1 Curtain Shield Airbag Assembly LH
      *a Protective Cover
      *b Adhesive Tape

      Protect the curtain shield airbag assembly LH.


      1. Cover the curtain shield airbag assembly LH with a piece of cloth or nylon and secure the edges of the cover with tape as shown in the illustration.

        Note

        Cover the curtain shield airbag assembly LH with a protective cover as soon as the front pillar garnish LH is removed.

  68. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY


    1. for Windshield Glass Side:


      1. Disconnect each connector.

    2. for Front Pillar LH Side:


      1. Remove the protective cover.

      2. Using a clip remover, disengage the 4 clamps.

      3. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

      4. Install the protective cover.

    3. for Front Pillar RH Side:


      1. Remove the protective cover.

      2. *A w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna

        Using a clip remover, disengage the 3 clamps.

      3. Disconnect each connector.

      4. Install the protective cover.

    4. for Rear Pillar RH Side:


      1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    5. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips.

      *A w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna - -

    6. w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna:


      1. Disconnect the connector.

    7. Remove in this Direction

      Remove the roof headlining assembly from the vehicle through the back door as shown in the illustration.

      Note

      Do not damage the roof headlining assembly or vehicle interior.
