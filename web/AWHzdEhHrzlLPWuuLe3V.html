TIRE REPAIR SEAL DISPOSAL

PROCEDURE


  1. DISPOSE OF TIRE REPAIR SEAL

    Tech Tips

    Confirm the expiration date on the side of the repair seal bottle.


    1. Dispose of the retrieved seal by consigning disposal to a waste disposal firm.

      Note


      • The valve and valve core cannot be reused.

      • Do not reuse items such as cloths that were used to wipe off seal or gloves soiled with the seal. Dispose of such items as burnable waste.

    2. Dispose of the empty repair seal bottle as plastic waste.
