PARKING ASSIST MONITOR SYSTEM INITIALIZATION


  1. INITIALIZE PARKING ASSIST MONITOR SYSTEM


    1. If estimated course lines are not displayed on the image of the area behind the vehicle, perform the following procedure to correct the steering angle neutral point.


      1. Fully turn the steering wheel to the left and right on level ground.

        Note

        Memorization of the steering angle neutral point must be carried out with the power switch on (READY). Apply the parking brake, depress the brake pedal, check that park (P) is selected, and ensure that the vehicle is not moving.

        Tech Tips

        If estimated course lines are not displayed on the image of the area behind the vehicle with the steering wheel fully turned to the left or right, perform "Steering Angle Setting in Calibration".
