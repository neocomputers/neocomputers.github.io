  28. DISCONNECT AC CHARGER INLET CABLE (w/o CABLE EV CHARGER LOCK ASSEMBLY)


    1. Disengage the 3 clamps.

    2. Bolt
      Nut

      Remove the bolt.

    3. Remove the 4 nuts and disconnect the AC charger inlet cable.

  29. DISCONNECT AC CHARGER INLET CABLE WITH CABLE EV CHARGER LOCK ASSEMBLY (w/ CABLE EV CHARGER LOCK ASSEMBLY)


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the 3 clamps.

    3. Bolt
      Nut

      Remove the bolt.

    4. Remove the 4 nuts and disconnect the AC charger inlet cable with cable EV charger lock assembly.

  30. REMOVE FUEL FILLER OPENING LID LOCK BRACKET


    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    2. Disengage the 4 clamps.

    3. Remove the 4 bolts.

    4. Disengage the guide.

    5. Disengage the fuel tank drain plug shield to remove the fuel filler opening lid lock bracket.
