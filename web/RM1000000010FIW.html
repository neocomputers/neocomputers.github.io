FOOTWELL LIGHT INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT NO. 1 INTERIOR ILLUMINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH (FOOTWELL LIGHT LH)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly LH (Footwell Light LH))

      Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the connector and check that the footwell light LH comes on.

      OK
      Measurement Condition Condition Specified Condition

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 2 (B)

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 1 (L)

      		 Always Footwell light LH comes on

      If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 1 interior illumination light assembly LH (footwell light LH).

  2. INSPECT NO. 1 INTERIOR ILLUMINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH (FOOTWELL LIGHT RH)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (No. 1 Interior Illumination Light Assembly RH (Footwell Light RH))

      Apply auxiliary battery voltage to the connector and check that the footwell light RH comes on.

      OK
      Measurement Condition Condition Specified Condition

      Auxiliary battery positive (+) → 2 (B)

      Auxiliary battery negative (-) → 1 (L)

      		 Always Footwell light RH comes on

      If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 1 interior illumination light assembly RH (footwell light RH).
