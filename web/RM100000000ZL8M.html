AIR CONDITIONING UNIT DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. PRECAUTION

    Note

    Make sure to perform initialization after replacing the air conditioning radiator damper servo sub-assembly. If initialization is not performed, the air conditioner unit assembly will not perform properly as the air conditioning amplifier assembly will not be able to recognize the position of the air conditioning radiator damper sub-assembly.

  2. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR DUCT (for LHD)


    1. Remove the screw.

    2. Disengage the 3 claws to remove the No. 2 air duct.

  3. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR DUCT (for RHD)


    1. Remove the screw.

    2. Disengage the 3 claws to remove the No. 1 air duct.

  4. REMOVE ASPIRATOR


    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the aspirator.

  5. REMOVE BLOWER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  6. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY


    1. for LHD:


      1. Remove in this Direction

        Disconnect the connector.

      2. Remove the screw.

      3. Disengage the guide to remove the air conditioning amplifier assembly as shown in the illustration.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Remove in this Direction

        Disconnect the connector.

      2. Remove the screw.

      3. Disengage the guide to remove the air conditioning amplifier assembly as shown in the illustration.

  7. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING HARNESS ASSEMBLY


    1. for LHD:


      1. Disconnect each connector.

      2. Disengage each clamp to remove the air conditioning harness assembly.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Disconnect each connector.

      2. Disengage each clamp to remove the air conditioning harness assembly.

  8. REMOVE DRAIN COOLER HOSE


    1. Remove the drain cooler hose.

  9. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CONDITIONING RADIATOR DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 2 screws and No. 1 air conditioning radiator damper servo sub-assembly.

  10. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CONDITIONING RADIATOR DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 3 screws and No. 2 air conditioning radiator damper servo sub-assembly.

  11. REMOVE NO. 3 AIR CONDITIONING RADIATOR DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 2 screws and No. 3 air conditioning radiator damper servo sub-assembly.

  12. REMOVE HEATER GROMMET


    1. Remove the heater grommet.

  13. REMOVE HEATER CLAMP


    1. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the heater clamp.

  14. REMOVE HEATER RADIATOR UNIT SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Remove the heater radiator unit sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.

      Note

      Prepare a drain pan or cloth in case the coolant leaks.

  15. REMOVE GROMMET


    1. Remove the grommet.

  16. REMOVE NO. 1 DISCHARGE TUBE


    1. for LHD:


      1. *a Clamp
        *b Guide
        Bolt
        Nut
        Remove in this Direction

        Disengage each clamp and each guide.

      2. Remove the bolt, nut and No. 1 discharge tube with thermistor as shown in the illustration.

      3. Remove the 2 O-rings from the No. 1 discharge tube.

        Note

        Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

    2. for RHD:


      1. *a Clamp
        *b Guide
        Bolt
        Nut
        Remove in this Direction

        Disengage each clamp and each guide.

      2. Remove the bolt, nut and No. 1 discharge tube with thermistor as shown in the illustration.

      3. Remove the 2 O-rings from the No. 1 discharge tube.

        Note

        Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent entry of moisture and foreign matter.

  17. REMOVE COOLER PACKING

    Click here

  18. REMOVE INTERNAL CONDENSER TEMPERATURE SENSOR

    Click here

  19. REMOVE COOLER PACKING

    Click here

  20. REMOVE DISCHARGE TEMPERATURE SENSOR

    Click here

  21. REMOVE DRAIN HOSE


    1. Remove the drain hose.

  22. REMOVE COOLER PIPE GROMMET


    1. Remove the cooler pipe grommet.

  23. REMOVE NO. 1 COOLER EVAPORATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Using a 4 mm hexagon socket wrench, remove the 2 hexagon bolts and connector tube.

    2. Remove the 2 O-rings from the No. 1 cooler evaporator sub-assembly.

    3. Remove the 2 screws.

    4. Disengage the 10 claws to remove the upper heater case with No. 1 cooler evaporator sub-assembly from the lower heater case.

    5. Disengage the clamp.

    6. Remove the No. 1 cooler evaporator sub-assembly with evaporator temperature sensor from the upper heater case.

  24. REMOVE EVAPORATOR TEMPERATURE SENSOR

    Click here

  25. REMOVE NO. 2 CONDENSER ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Remove the No. 2 condenser assembly as shown in the illustration.
