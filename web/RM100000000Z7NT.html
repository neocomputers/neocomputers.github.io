FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY(for Power Seat) DISASSEMBLY

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

CAUTION:

Wear protective gloves. Sharp areas on the parts may injure your hands.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH

    Click here

  2. DISCONNECT SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK COVER


    1. Disconnect the rubber band of the separate type front seatback cover.

  3. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER NO. 1 CUSHION SHIELD LH


    1. Remove the clip.

    2. Disengage the guide as shown in the illustration.

    3. Disengage the 2 claws as shown in the illustration.

    4. Disengage the 3 guides to remove the front seat inner No. 1 cushion shield LH.

  4. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH


    1. Disengage the 2 claws.

    2. Disengage the claw and 2 guides.

    3. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this direction

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claw as shown in the illustration.

    4. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the guide as shown in the illustration.

    5. Disconnect the front power seat switch LH connector to remove the front seat cushion shield LH.

  5. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER CUSHION SHIELD LH


    1. Disengage the 2 claws.

    2. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and 2 guides to remove the front seat inner cushion shield LH as shown in the illustration.

  6. REMOVE SLIDE AND VERTICAL POWER SEAT SWITCH KNOB


    1. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 claws to remove the slide and vertical power seat switch knob.

  7. REMOVE RECLINING POWER SEAT SWITCH KNOB


    1. Using a moulding remover, disengage the 4 claws to remove the reclining power seat switch knob.

  8. REMOVE FRONT SEAT SIDE NO. 1 BRACKET LH


    1. Remove the 2 screws and front seat side No. 1 bracket LH from the front seat cushion shield LH.

  9. REMOVE FRONT POWER SEAT SWITCH LH

    Click here

  10. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 2 hooks.

    2. Disconnect the connector.

    3. Disengage the clamp.

    4. Disconnect the connector.

    5. Disengage the clamp.

    6. Disengage the claw and 4 clamps to separate the front seat airbag assembly LH wire harness.

      Tech Tips

      Disengage the 4 clamps first.

    7. Remove the 4 bolts.

    8. Disengage the 4 guides to remove the separate type front seatback assembly from the separate type front seat cushion assembly.

  11. REMOVE FRONT SEAT HEADREST SUPPORT


    1. Disengage the 3 hooks.

    2. Disengage the clamp.

    3. Open the 2 fasteners.

    4. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the hook as shown in the illustration.

    5. *1 Seatback Cover Hook
      *a Pin

      Pull up the pin of the seatback cover hook as shown in the illustration and remove the seatback cover hook.

    6. *1 Seat Cover Hanger
      *a Bracket
      Remove in this direction

      Slide the bracket as shown in the illustration to disengage the hook and disconnect the bracket from the seat cover hanger.

    7. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the 2 front seat headrest supports.

  12. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK COVER WITH PAD


    1. Remove the separate type front seatback cover with pad.

  13. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEATBACK COVER


    1. Using a screwdriver, disengage the 17 claws and remove the separate type front seatback cover from the separate type front seatback pad as shown in the illustration.

      *a Screwdriver - -

  14. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK HEATER ASSEMBLY LH

    Click here

  15. REMOVE FRONT SEAT AIRBAG ASSEMBLY LH

    Click here

  16. REMOVE LUMBAR SUPPORT ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY LH

    Click here

  17. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK EDGE PROTECTOR


    1. Remove the 2 front seatback edge protectors from the lumbar support adjuster assembly LH.

  18. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK HOOK


    1. Remove the 2 front seatback hooks from the front seatback frame sub-assembly LH.

  19. REMOVE FRONT SEAT NO. 2 WIRE LH


    1. Disconnect each connector and disengage each clamp to remove the front seat No. 2 wire LH.

  20. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER WITH PAD


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the clamp.

    3. Disengage the 10 hooks and remove the separate type front seat cushion cover with pad.

  21. REMOVE SEPARATE TYPE FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER


    1. Using a screwdriver, disengage the 13 claws and remove the separate type front seat cushion cover from the separate type front seat cushion pad as shown in the illustration.

      *a Screwdriver - -

  22. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION HEATER ASSEMBLY LH

    Click here

  23. REMOVE LH SEAT RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER LH


    1. Disengage the claw.

    2. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the LH seat reclining adjuster inside cover LH.

  24. REMOVE LH SEAT RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER RH


    1. Disengage the 4 claws to remove the LH seat reclining adjuster inside cover RH.

  25. REMOVE FRONT SEAT LOWER CUSHION SHIELD LH


    1. *a Hook
      *b Guide

      Disengage the hook.

    2. Disengage the claw and guide to remove the front seat lower cushion shield LH.

  26. REMOVE FRONT SEAT LOWER CUSHION SHIELD RH


    1. *a Hook
      *b Guide

      Disengage the hook.

    2. Disengage the claw and guide to remove the front seat lower cushion shield RH.

  27. REMOVE FRONT SEAT WIRE LH


    1. Disconnect each connector and disengage each clamp to remove the front seat wire LH.

  28. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SPRING SUB-ASSEMBLY LH


    1. Disengage the 6 hooks to remove the front seat cushion spring sub-assembly LH from the front seat adjuster assembly LH.

  29. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION WIRE HARNESS HOLDER LH


    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the 2 claws.

    3. Disengage the guide to remove the front seat cushion wire harness holder LH as shown in the illustration.

  30. REMOVE FRONT SEAT LOWER NO. 1 CUSHION SHIELD LH


    1. Disengage the claw.

    2. Remove in this direction

      Disengage the guide to remove the front seat lower No. 1 cushion shield LH as shown in the illustration.

  31. REMOVE POWER SEAT LIFTER MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH


    1. Remove the power seat lifter motor assembly LH.


      1. Remove the 3 nuts and front seat adjuster plate LH.

      2. Remove the vertical adjuster spacer.

      3. Remove the front seat vertical housing sub-assembly LH.

  32. REMOVE POWER SEAT TILT MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH


    1. Remove the 3 bolts and power seat tilt motor assembly LH.
