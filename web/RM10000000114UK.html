FRONT CRANKSHAFT OIL SEAL REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL RH

    Click here

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  3. REMOVE REAR ENGINE UNDER COVER RH

    Click here

  4. REMOVE REAR SIDE RAIL REINFORCEMENT SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

    Click here

  5. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT PULLEY


    1. *a Hold
      *b Turn

      Using SST to hold the crankshaft pulley, loosen the crankshaft pulley set bolt. Further loosen the crankshaft pulley set bolt until 2 or 3 threads remain screwed into the crankshaft.

      SST
      09213-58014   ( 91551-80840 )
      09330-00021
    2. *a Hold
      *b Turn

      Using SST and the crankshaft pulley set bolt, remove the crankshaft pulley and crankshaft pulley set bolt.

      SST
      09950-50013   ( 09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05021 )

      Tech Tips

      Apply lubricant to the threads and end of SST.

  6. REMOVE TIMING CHAIN COVER OIL SEAL


    1. *a Cut Position
      *b Protective Tape

      Using a knife, cut through the lip of the timing chain cover oil seal.

    2. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, pry out the timing chain cover oil seal.

      Note

      After removal, check the crankshaft for damage. If it is damaged, smooth the surface with 400-grit sandpaper.
