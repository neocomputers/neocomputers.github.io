FRONT LOWER BALL JOINT ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT LOWER BALL JOINT ASSEMBLY


    1. Check for looseness.


      1. Lift up the vehicle.

      2. Move the front lower No. 1 suspension arm sub-assembly up and down by hand with a force of 294 N (30 kgf) or more to check that there is no looseness at the front lower ball joint assembly.

        Tech Tips

        If the front lower ball joint assembly is loose, replace it with a new one.
