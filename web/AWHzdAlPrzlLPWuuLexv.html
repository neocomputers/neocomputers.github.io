FORWARD RECOGNITION CAMERA SYSTEM UTILITY

Note


  • If the forward recognition camera has been replaced with a new one or the windshield glass has been removed and installed, it is necessary to perform Forward Recognition Camera Learning. If the system is turned on without performing Forward Recognition Camera Learning, DTC C1AA9 will be stored.

    Click here

  • When replacing the windshield glass of a vehicle equipped with a forward recognition camera, make sure to use a Toyota genuine part. If a non-Toyota genuine part is used, the forward recognition camera may not be able to be installed due to a missing bracket or the forward recognition camera system may not operate properly due to a difference in the transmissivity or black ceramic border.


  1. Recognition Camera/Target Position Memory

    Tech Tips

    Recognition Camera/Target Position Memory is used to enter required information into the forward recognition camera.


    1. Perform Recognition Camera Axis Adjust according to the display on the GTS.

      Click here


      Chassis > Front Recognition Camera > Utility
      Tester Display
      Recognition Camera/Target Position Memory

  2. Tech Tips

    Recognition Camera Axis Adjust is used to calibrate the internal data of the forward recognition camera to enhance its recognition accuracy.

    Recognition Camera Axis Adjust


    1. Perform Recognition Camera Axis Adjust according to the display on the GTS.

      Click here


      Chassis > Front Recognition Camera > Utility
      Tester Display
      Recognition Camera Axis Adjust

  3. Tech Tips

    History Clear is used to clear the history of Data List items Blockage, Low Temperature, or High Temperature.

    History Clear


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / front recognition camera / Utility / History Clear.

    5. Perform History Clear according to the display on the GTS.


      Chassis > Front Recognition Camera > Utility
      Tester Display
      History Clear
