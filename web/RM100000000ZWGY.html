OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR GLASS INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT OUTER MIRROR RH


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Outer Mirror RH)

      Check the outer mirror heater operation.


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        1 - 2 25°C (77°F) 3.57 to 4.83 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer mirror RH.

    2. Check the operation of the outer rear view mirror indicator. (w/ Blind Spot Monitor System)

      Note

      Do not apply a voltage of more than 6 V.


      1. Connect 4 new 1.5 V dry-cell batteries in series.

    3. Check the operation of the outer rear view mirror indicator. (w/ Blind Spot Monitor System)

      Note

      Do not apply a voltage of more than 6 V.


      1. Connect 4 new 1.5 V dry-cell batteries in series.

      2. *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Outer Mirror RH)

        Apply 6 V battery voltage to the terminals of the connector, and check that the outer rear view mirror indicator comes on.

        OK
        Tester Connection Specified Condition

        6 V battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

        6 V battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Outer rear view mirror indicator comes on

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer mirror RH.

  2. INSPECT OUTER MIRROR LH


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Outer Mirror LH)

      Check the outer mirror heater operation. (w/ Mirror Heater)


      1. Measure the resistance according the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        1 - 2 25°C (77°F) 3.57 to 4.83 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer mirror LH.

    2. Check the operation of the outer rear view mirror indicator. (w/ Blind Spot Monitor System)

      Note

      Do not apply a voltage of more than 6 V.


      1. Connect 4 new 1.5 V dry-cell batteries in series.

      2. (Outer Mirror LH)
        *a

        Component without harness connected

        (Outer Mirror LH)

        Apply 6 V battery voltage to the terminals of the connector, and check that the outer rear view mirror indicator comes on.

        OK
        Tester Connection Specified Condition

        6 V battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

        6 V battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Outer rear view mirror indicator comes on

        If the result is not as specified, replace the outer mirror LH.
