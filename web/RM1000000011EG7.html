PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE FRONT SIDE ULTRASONIC SENSOR

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front side ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.

  3. REMOVE ULTRASONIC SENSOR CLIP

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the clamp.

    2. Disengage the 4 claws and remove the ultrasonic sensor clip as shown in the illustration.

  4. REMOVE FRONT CORNER ULTRASONIC SENSOR

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front corner ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.

  5. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER PAD LH

    Click here

  6. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER PAD RH

    Tech Tips

    Use the same procedure as for the LH side.

  7. REMOVE FRONT CENTER ULTRASONIC SENSOR

    Tech Tips

    The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws and remove the front center ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.

  8. REMOVE FRONT SIDE ULTRASONIC SENSOR RETAINER

    Tech Tips


    • Perform this procedure only when replacement of the front side ultrasonic sensor retainer is necessary.

    • When removing the front side ultrasonic sensor retainer, heat the front bumper assembly and front side ultrasonic sensor retainer using a heat light.

    • The illustration is for the LH side. The orientation for the RH side is the opposite of the LH side.


    1. Heat the front bumper assembly and front side ultrasonic sensor retainer using a heat light at the specified temperature for 3 to 5 minutes.

      Heating Temperature
      Item Temperature
      Front Bumper Assembly 40 to 60°C (104 to 140°F)
      Front Side Ultrasonic Sensor Retainer

      CAUTION:


      • Do not touch the heat light and heated parts.

      • Touching the heat light may result in burns.

      • Touching heated parts for a long time may result in burns.

      *a Heated Part
      *b Heat Light

    2. Remove the front side ultrasonic sensor retainer.
