  49. REMOVE INLET AC CHARGER CABLE (w/o CABLE EV CHARGER LOCK ASSEMBLY)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. Disconnect the drain hose.

    2. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Disconnect the inlet AC charger cable connector (A).

    3. Using a screwdriver, slide the green-colored lock of the connector as shown in the illustration to release it and disconnect the inlet AC charger cable connector (B).

    4. Disengage the 4 clamps.

    5. *a Earth Terminal
      Nut
      Bolt

      Remove the bolt and disconnect the earth terminal.

    6. Remove the 4 nuts and inlet AC charger cable.

  50. REMOVE INLET AC CHARGER CABLE (w/ CABLE EV CHARGER LOCK ASSEMBLY)

    CAUTION:

    Wear insulated gloves.


    1. Disconnect the drain hose.

    2. *a Green-colored Lock
      Slide

      Disconnect the inlet AC charger cable connector (A).

    3. Using a screwdriver, slide the green-colored lock of the connector as shown in the illustration to release it and disconnect the inlet AC charger cable connector (B).

    4. Disengage the 4 clamps.

    5. Disconnect the cable EV charger lock assembly connector.

    6. *a Earth Terminal
      Nut
      Bolt

      Remove the bolt and disconnect the earth terminal.

    7. Remove the 4 nuts and inlet AC charger cable.
