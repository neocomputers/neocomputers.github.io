AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B14A7

DTC Code DTC Name
B14A7 Refrigerant Outlet Sensor Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The discharge temperature sensor is installed to the inlet pipe of the No. 2 condenser assembly and detects the refrigerant temperature for heat pump air-conditioning control. The resistance of the discharge temperature sensor changes depending on the refrigerant temperature of the No. 2 condenser assembly inlet. When the temperature becomes low, the resistance of the thermistor increases. When the temperature becomes high, the resistance decreases.

The heat pump ECU assembly applies a voltage (5 V) to the discharge temperature sensor and reads voltage changes due to changes in the resistance of the discharge temperature sensor.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Memory
B14A7 Refrigerant Outlet Sensor Circuit Open or short in discharge temperature sensor circuit

  • Discharge temperature sensor

  • Harness or connector

  • Heat pump ECU assembly
