SFI SYSTEM(w/o Canister Pump Module) PRECAUTION


  1. WHEN USING GTS

    CAUTION:

    Observe the following items for safety reasons:


    • Before using the GTS, read the instruction manual.

    • Prevent the GTS cable from being caught on the pedals, shift lever or steering wheel when driving with the GTS connected to the vehicle.

    • When driving the vehicle for testing purposes using the GTS, 2 persons are required. One to drive the vehicle, and another to operate the GTS.
