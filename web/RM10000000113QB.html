OIL PUMP REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE OIL PUMP WITH MOTOR ASSEMBLY


    1. *A for Rough Road Area Specification Vehicles
      Bolt
      Clip

      Remove the 3 bolts and clip.

    2. for Rough Road Area Specification Vehicles:


      1. Remove the clip.

    3. Turn back the front fender liner LH.

    4. Disconnect the oil pump with motor assembly connector.

    5. Remove the bolt and nut and separate the oil pump with motor assembly from the vehicle body.

    6. *1 Inlet Oil Cooler Hose
      *2 Outlet Oil Cooler Hose

      Slide the 2 clips and disconnect the inlet oil cooler hose and outlet oil cooler hose to remove the oil pump with motor assembly.

      Note

      Do not drop the oil pump with motor assembly.
