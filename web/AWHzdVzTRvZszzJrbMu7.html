SEAT POSITION SENSOR ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

CAUTION:

Be sure to correctly follow the removal and installation procedures for the seat position airbag sensor.

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT SEAT POSITION AIRBAG SENSOR (for Vehicle not Involved in Collision)


    1. Perform a diagnostic system check.

      Click here

  2. INSPECT SEAT POSITION AIRBAG SENSOR (for Vehicle Involved in Collision)


    1. Perform a diagnostic system check.

      Click here

    2. Visually check for defects with the seat position airbag sensor removed from the vehicle.

      Tech Tips

      The defects are as follows:


      • Cracks in the sensor housing

      • Dents in the sensor housing

      • Chips in the sensor housing

      • Cracks or other damage to the connector

      OK
      No defects are found.

      If any defects are found, replace the seat position airbag sensor with a new one.
