LIGHTING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B242C, B242D

DTC Code DTC Name
B242C Right Headlight ECU Malfunction
B242D Left Headlight ECU Malfunction

DESCRIPTION

These DTCs are stored if the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH or RH detects an internal malfunction. The No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH stores DTC B242C and B242D.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Note
B242C Right Headlight ECU Malfunction An internal malfunction occurs in the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly RH No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly RH -
B242D Left Headlight ECU Malfunction An internal malfunction occurs in the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH -

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

If the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH has been replaced, it is necessary to synchronize the vehicle information and initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.
