PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK DTC OUTPUT (HEALTH CHECK)


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Health Check.

    5. Check DTCs.

      Result
      Result Proceed to
      No DTCs are output. A
      DTCs are output. B

    6. Turn the power switch off.


    B

    A

  2. SIMULATION TEST (USING MULTI-INFORMATION DISPLAY)


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    2. Display the timer charging setting screen.


      1. Push the timer charging switch (fuel control switch) to display the timer charging setting screen on the multi-information display.

      2. Using the steering pad switches, enter the following menus on the multi-information display: Vehicle Settings / Charge settings / Charge Schedule.

      3. Change or set the charge schedule on the setting screen.

        Result
        Result Proceed to
        Charge schedule can be changed/set on multi-information display A
        Timer charging switch (fuel control switch) does not operate B
        Steering pad switches do not operate C
        Charge schedule cannot be changed/set on multi-information display D

    3. Turn the power switch off.
