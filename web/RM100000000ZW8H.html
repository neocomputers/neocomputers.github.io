  5. DISCONNECT NO. 8 WATER BY-PASS HOSE


    1. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 8 water by-pass hose from the No. 3 water by-pass pipe.

  6. DISCONNECT NO. 9 WATER BY-PASS HOSE


    1. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 9 water by-pass hose from the No. 3 water by-pass pipe.

  7. REMOVE PURGE VALVE (PURGE VSV)


    1. *1 No. 1 Fuel Vapor Feed Hose
      *2 No. 2 Fuel Vapor Feed Hose

      Disconnect the purge valve (purge VSV) connector.

    2. Disconnect the No. 1 fuel vapor feed hose from the purge valve (purge VSV).

    3. Disconnect the No. 2 fuel vapor feed hose from the purge valve (purge VSV).

    4. Remove the bolt and purge valve (purge VSV) with No. 3 water by-pass pipe from the camshaft housing sub-assembly.

    5. Remove the screw and purge valve (purge VSV) from the No. 3 water by-pass pipe.
