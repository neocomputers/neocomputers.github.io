SHIFT LEVER INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL SHIFT LOCK CONTROL UNIT ASSEMBLY


    1. Connect the shift lock control unit assembly connector.

    2. Install the shift lock control unit assembly to the No. 1 instrument panel brace sub-assembly and No. 2 instrument panel brace sub-assembly with the 3 nuts.

      Torque:
      12 N*m  { 122 kgf*cm, 9 ft.*lbf }

    3. Engage the clamp.

    Click here

  15. INSPECT SHIFT LEVER


    1. Turn the power switch on (READY).

    2. Check that all shift positions can be selected by moving the shift lever.

      Tech Tips

      After the shift lock control unit assembly is replaced with a new one, perform the above operation. If this operation is not performed, moving the shift lever may not initially select shift positions.
