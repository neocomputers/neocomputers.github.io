TIRE REPAIR SEAL RECOVERY

PROCEDURE


  1. RECOVERY OF TIRE REPAIR SEAL

    Note


    • Try not to spill the tire repair seal in the tire while performing service.

    • Wipe off any spilt seal with a cloth or equivalent.


    1. Using a tire changer, remove the tire from the wheel by following the same procedure as for a regular tire change.

      Note

      Do not raise the tire rapidly as the seal may spill out.

    2. Retrieve the tire repair seal from the removed tire.

      Tech Tips

      Using a cloth or equivalent, wipe off any seal spilt on the tire, wheel, tire changer or floor.
