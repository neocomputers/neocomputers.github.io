  1. REMOVE REAR ENGINE UNDER COVER LH

    Click here

  2. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT (for Engine)

    Click here

  3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  4. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the mass air flow meter sub-assembly connector.

    2. Disengage the wire harness clamp.

    3. *a Clamp
      *b Guide

      Disengage the 2 clamps.

    4. Disengage the 2 guides and remove the air cleaner cap sub-assembly from the air cleaner case sub-assembly.

    5. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the air cleaner cap sub-assembly from the air cleaner hose assembly.

  5. REMOVE AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY


    1. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 2 ventilation hose from the cylinder head cover sub-assembly.

    2. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the air cleaner hose assembly from the throttle body assembly.

  6. REMOVE THROTTLE BODY ASSEMBLY


    1. *A w/ Stud Bolt
      *B w/o Stud Bolt
      *1 Water By-pass Hose
      *2 No. 2 Water By-pass Hose

      Disconnect the throttle body assembly connector.

    2. Slide the 2 clips and disconnect the water by-pass hose and No. 2 water by-pass hose from the throttle body assembly.

    3. *A w/ Stud Bolt
      *B w/o Stud Bolt

      w/ Stud Bolt:


      1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and throttle body assembly from the intake manifold.

        Note

        If the throttle body assembly has been struck or dropped, replace it.

    4. w/o Stud Bolt:


      1. Remove the 4 bolts and throttle body assembly from the intake manifold.

        Note

        If the throttle body assembly has been struck or dropped, replace it.

  7. REMOVE THROTTLE BODY GASKET


    1. *A w/ Stud Bolt
      *B w/o Stud Bolt

      Remove the throttle body gasket from the intake manifold.
