AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B1381

DTC Code DTC Name
B1381 Internal Condenser Refrigerant Temperature Sensor Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The internal condenser temperature sensor is installed to the outlet pipe of the No. 2 condenser assembly and detects the refrigerant temperature for heat pump air-conditioning control. The resistance of the internal condenser temperature sensor changes depending on the refrigerant temperature of the No. 2 condenser assembly outlet. When the temperature becomes low, the resistance of the thermistor increases. When the temperature becomes high, the resistance decreases.

The heat pump ECU assembly applies a voltage (5 V) to the internal condenser temperature sensor and reads voltage changes due to changes in the resistance of the internal condenser temperature sensor.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Memory
B1381 Internal Condenser Refrigerant Temperature Sensor Circuit Open or short in internal condenser temperature sensor circuit

  • Internal condenser temperature sensor

  • Harness or connector

  • Heat pump ECU assembly

Memorized

(4 sec. or more)*

  • *: The heat pump ECU assembly stores this DTC if the malfunction has occurred for the period of time indicated in the brackets.
