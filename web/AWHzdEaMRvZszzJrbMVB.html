TIRE PRESSURE WARNING VALVE DISPOSAL

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips

The tire pressure warning valve and transmitter is powered by a lithium battery. When disposing of the tire pressure warning valve and transmitter, remove the battery and dispose of it properly.

PROCEDURE


  1. DISPOSE OF TIRE PRESSURE WARNING VALVE AND TRANSMITTER


    1. Urethane

      Remove the urethane that protects the lithium battery and the circuit board.

    2. *1 Terminal
      *2 Lithium Battery

      Cut the 2 terminals to remove the lithium battery from the tire pressure warning valve and transmitter.
