METER / GAUGE SYSTEM Entire Combination Meter does not Operate

DESCRIPTION

This circuit is the power source circuit for the combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate). This circuit provides two types of power sources; one is a constant power source, and the other is an IG power source.

WIRING DIAGRAM

*1 from IG2 NO. 2 Relay
*2 from Auxiliary Battery
*3 ECU-DCC NO. 2
*4 Combination Meter Assembly (Meter Circuit Plate)

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note


  • When replacing the combination meter assembly, always replace it with a new one. If a combination meter assembly which was installed to another vehicle is used, the information stored in it will not match the information from the vehicle and a DTC may be stored.

  • Inspect the fuses of circuits related to this system before performing the following procedure.

PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (POWER SOURCE CIRCUIT)
