DYNAMIC RADAR CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:C1A01

DTC Code DTC Name
C1A01 Driving Support ECU

DESCRIPTION

This DTC indicates an internal malfunction of the driving support ECU assembly.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1A01 Driving Support ECU When the power switch is on (IG) and the dynamic radar cruise control system is operating, the driving support ECU assembly detects an internal malfunction for approximately 0.2 seconds. Driving support ECU assembly

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

When replacing the driving support ECU assembly, always replace it with a new one. If a driving support ECU assembly which was installed to another vehicle is used, the information stored in the driving support ECU assembly will not match the information from the vehicle. As a result, a DTC may be stored.

PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK FOR DTC (RADAR CRUISE 2)


    1. Clear the DTCs.

      Click here


      Powertrain > Radar Cruise2 > Clear DTCs

    2. Make sure that the DTC detection conditions are met.

      Tech Tips

      If the detection conditions are not met, the system cannot detect the malfunction.


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      2. Turn the dynamic radar cruise control system on using the cruise control main switch (ON/OFF button).

      3. Wait for 1 second or more.

    3. Check for DTCs.

      Click here


      Powertrain > Radar Cruise2 > Trouble Codes
