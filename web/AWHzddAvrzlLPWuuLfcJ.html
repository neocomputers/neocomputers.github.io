HOOD DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE HOOD INSULATOR


    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 6 hood insulator clips.

    2. Disengage the 2 guides to remove the hood insulator.

  2. REMOVE WASHER NOZZLE SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  3. DISCONNECT WASHER HOSE ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 5 clamps.

    2. Disengage the grommet and disconnect the washer hose assembly.

  4. REMOVE HOOD STAY HOLDER


    1. Disengage the 2 claws to remove the hood stay holder.

  5. REMOVE HOOD SUPPORT ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 3 claws to remove the hood support assembly.

  6. REMOVE HOOD STAY BRACKET


    1. Remove the bolt.

    2. Disengage the guide to remove the hood stay bracket.
