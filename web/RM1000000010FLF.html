BACK-UP LIGHT BULB REMOVAL

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips


  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  • The following procedure is for the LH side.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE NO. 2 FLOOR UNDER COVER (for LH Side)


    1. Separate in this Direction

      Remove the clip.

    2. Disengage the 2 claws to separate the rear bumper assembly as shown in the illustration.

    3. Remove the 4 clips.

    4. *a Protective Tape
      Turn in this Direction
      Remove in this Direction

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, while pulling the No. 2 floor under cover as shown in the illustration, turn the clip to disengage the No. 2 floor under cover.

    5. *a Protective Tape
      Turn in this Direction
      Remove in this Direction

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, while pulling the No. 2 floor under cover as shown in the illustration, turn the clip to remove the No. 2 floor under cover.

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 FLOOR UNDER COVER (for RH Side)


    1. Separate in this Direction

      Remove the 3 clips.

    2. Separate the rear bumper assembly as shown in the illustration.

    3. Remove the 3 clips.

    4. *a Protective Tape
      Turn in this Direction
      Remove in this Direction

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, while pulling the No. 1 floor under cover as shown in the illustration, turn the clip to remove the No. 1 floor under cover.

  3. REMOVE BACK-UP LIGHT BULB


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Turn the No. 3 luggage room wire with the back-up light bulb as shown in the illustration to disconnect them as a unit.

    2. Remove the back-up light bulb from the No. 3 luggage room wire.
