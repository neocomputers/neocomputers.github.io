CHARGE CABLE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER CABLE ASSEMBLY (for Type 1)


    1. Visual inspection (*A):

      Tech Tips


      • When performing a visual inspection, compare the electric vehicle charger cable assembly with a known good one.

      • If the power source plug is damaged, replace the cable EV charger plug assembly.

      • Due to the characteristics of the electric vehicle charger cable assembly, it may become stiff or twisted. This is not a malfunction.

      • If the electric vehicle charger cable assembly is would tightly or repeatedly folded for storage, twists may form more easily and can lead to an open-circuit.


      1. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)

        Check that the electric vehicle charger cable assembly (charging connector side) is free of foreign matter.

        Tech Tips

        If there is foreign matter in the charging connector which prevents it from being securely connected, charging will not be performed.

    2. Check the latch release button (PISW):


      1. *a Latch Release Button (PISW)

        Check that the latch release button (PISW) can be pressed with no abnormal resistance.

    3. Check connection:


      1. Check that the electric vehicle charger cable assembly and inlet AC charger cable (charging inlet side) can be connected smoothly.

        OK
        The electric vehicle charger cable assembly and inlet AC charger cable (charging inlet side) connects smoothly.

        Tech Tips

        If the result is not as specified, perform the visual inspection (*A) and the following procedure.

      2. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)
        *a Terminal

        Check that the terminals of the electric vehicle charger cable assembly (charging connector side) are not bent or deformed.

        OK
        The terminals are not bent or deformed.

        Tech Tips

        If the result is not as specified, replace the electric vehicle charger cable assembly.

      3. *1 Inlet AC Charger Cable (Charging Inlet Side)

        Check that the terminals of the inlet AC charger cable (charging inlet side) are not bent or deformed.

        OK
        The terminals are not bent or deformed.

        Tech Tips

        If the result is not as specified, replace the inlet AC charger cable.

    4. Check for short:

      CAUTION:

      Wear insulated gloves.


      1. Plug in the electric vehicle charger cable assembly to a socket.

        Tech Tips

        Make sure not to connect electric vehicle charger cable assembly to the inlet AC charger cable (charging inlet side).

      2. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)

        Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Voltage
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        3 (PISW) - 5 (GND) Latch release button (PISW) pressed Below 1 V
        Latch release button (PISW) not pressed Below 1 V
        4 (CPLT) - 5 (GND)

        • Power indicator illuminated

        • Error warning indicator not illuminated

        		 11.4 to 12.6 V

    5. Check for open:


      1. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)

        Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        3 (PISW) - 5 (GND) Latch release button (PISW) pressed 430 to 530 Ω
        Latch release button (PISW) not pressed 135 to 165 Ω

      2. Insert the plug cable EV charger key to release the lock and then turn the connector and remove the cable EV charger plug assembly from the CCID (charging circuit interrupting device).

        *1 Plug Cable EV Charger Key *2 Cable EV Charger Plug Assembly
        *a Lock *b Connector
        *c CCID (charging circuit interrupting device) - -

      3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        1 - 1' Always Below 1 Ω
        2 - 2' Always Below 1 Ω
        3 - 3' Always Below 1 Ω
        4 - 5 Always 130 to 660 Ω

        Tech Tips

        If the result is not as specified, replace the cable EV charger plug assembly.

      2. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)

        The terminals are not bent or deformed.

      1. Plug in the electric vehicle charger cable assembly to a socket.

        Tech Tips

        Make sure not to connect electric vehicle charger cable assembly to the inlet AC charger cable (charging inlet side).

      2. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)

        Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Voltage
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        4 (PISW) - 2 (GND) Always Below 1 V
        5 (CPLT) - 2 (GND) Always Below 1 V
        5 (CPLT) - 2 (GND)

        • Power indicator illuminated

        • Error warning indicator not illuminated

        		 11.4 to 12.6 V

    4. Check for open:


      1. *1 Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly (Charging Connector Side)

        Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        4 (PISW) - 2 (GND) Always 646 to 714 Ω

      2. Insert the plug cable EV charger key to release the lock and then turn the connector and remove the cable EV charger plug assembly from the CCID (charging circuit interrupting device).

        *1 Plug Cable EV Charger Key *2 Cable EV Charger Plug Assembly
        *a Lock *b Connector
        *c CCID (charging circuit interrupting device) - -
      3. *a Vehicle Side
        *b Infrastructure Side

        Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        Vehicle Side Infrastructure Side
        3 (ACIC) 3 (ACIC) Always Below 0.3 Ω
        1 (ACIH) 1 (ACIH) Always Below 0.3 Ω
        2 (GND) 2 (GND) Always Below 0.3 Ω
        5 (CPLT) 5 (CPLT) Always Below 0.3 Ω
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        Vehicle Side
        4 (PISW) 2 (GND) Always 646 to 714 Ω
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        Infrastructure Side
        4 (PISW) 2 (GND) Always 646 to 714 Ω

        Tech Tips

        If the result is not as specified, replace the cable EV charger plug assembly.
