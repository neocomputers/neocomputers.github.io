OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. DTC CHECK


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the power switch on (IG), and wait for at least 10 seconds.

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Occupant Detection / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Occupant Detection > Trouble Codes

    6. Check for DTCs by following the prompts on the GTS screen.

      Tech Tips

      Refer to the GTS operator's manual for further details.

  2. DTC CLEAR


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the power switch on (IG), and wait for at least 10 seconds.

    4. Turn the GTS on.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Occupant Detection / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Occupant Detection > Clear DTCs

    6. Clear the DTCs by following the prompts on the GTS screen.

      Tech Tips

      Refer to the GTS operator's manual for further details.
