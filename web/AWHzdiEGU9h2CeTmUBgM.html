BACK DOOR OUTSIDE GARNISH REASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL BACK DOOR OUTSIDE GARNISH COVER


    1. Install in this Direction

      Install the 6 bolts as shown in the illustration.

    2. Install the back door outside garnish cover with the 10 clips.

  2. INSTALL NO. 1 BACK DOOR OUTSIDE GARNISH PROTECTOR

    Tech Tips

    When installing the No. 1 back door outside garnish protector, heat the back door outside garnish using a heat light.

    Heating Temperature
    Item Temperature
    Back Door Outside Garnish 20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)

    Note

    Do not heat the back door excessively.


    1. Clean the back door outside garnish surface.


      1. Using a heat light, heat the back door outside garnish surface.

      2. Remove any remaining double-sided tape from the back door outside garnish.

      3. Wipe off any tape adhesive residue with cleaner.

    2. Using a heat light, heat the back door outside garnish.

    3. Remove the release paper from 2 new No. 1 back door outside garnish protectors.

      Tech Tips

      After removing the release paper, keep the exposed adhesive free from foreign matter.

    4. *a Line

      Install the 2 No. 1 back door outside garnish protectors as shown in the illustration.

      Tech Tips

      Install the 2 No. 1 back door outside garnish protectors along the line on the back door.
