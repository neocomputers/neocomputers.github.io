PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE WATER OUTLET (w/ Flow Shutting Valve (Water Valve))


    1. *1 No. 9 Water By-pass Hose
      *2 No. 7 Water by-pass Hose
      *3 No. 4 Water By-pass Hose

      Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 7 water by-pass hose from the No. 2 water by-pass hose.

    2. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 4 water by-pass hose from the EGR valve assembly with EGR cooler.

    3. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 9 water by-pass hose from the purge valve.

    4. Remove the 2 nuts, water outlet and water outlet gasket from the cylinder head sub-assembly.

  2. REMOVE WATER OUTLET (w/o Flow Shutting Valve (Water Valve))


    1. *1 No. 9 water by-pass hose
      *2 No. 4 water by-pass hose

      Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 4 water by-pass hose from the EGR valve assembly with EGR cooler.

    2. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 9 water by-pass hose from the purge valve.

    3. Remove the 2 nuts, water outlet and water outlet gasket from the cylinder head sub-assembly.

  3. REMOVE EGR PIPE ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 4 bolts, EGR pipe assembly, inlet EGR gasket and EGR pipe gasket.

  4. REMOVE EGR VALVE ASSEMBLY WITH EGR COOLER


    1. Bolt
      Nut
      *a Stud bolt

      Remove the bolt and 4 nuts.

    2. Using an E8 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the 2 stud bolts and EGR valve assembly with EGR cooler from the cylinder head sub-assembly and exhaust manifold (TWC: Front Catalyst).

    3. Remove the EGR cooler gasket.
