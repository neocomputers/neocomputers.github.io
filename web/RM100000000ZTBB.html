IMMOBILISER SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR

Tech Tips

Refer to the GTS operator's manual for further details.


  1. CHECK FOR IMMOBILISER DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Entry&Start / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Entry&Start > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  2. CHECK FOR HYBRID VEHICLE CONTROL ECU DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Hybrid Control / Trouble Codes.


      Powertrain > Hybrid Control > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  3. CLEAR IMMOBILISER DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / Entry&Start / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > Entry&Start > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.

  4. CLEAR HYBRID VEHICLE CONTROL ECU DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Hybrid Control / Trouble Codes.


      Powertrain > Hybrid Control > Clear DTCs

    5. Clear the DTCs.
