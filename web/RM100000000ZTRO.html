INNER REAR VIEW MIRROR REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE INNER REAR VIEW MIRROR STAY HOLDER COVER (w/o Toyota Safety Sense)


    1. Slide

      Disengage the 2 guides and slide the inner rear view mirror stay holder cover as shown in the illustration.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 6 claws to remove the inner rear view mirror stay holder cover as shown in the illustration.

  2. REMOVE INNER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY (w/o Toyota Safety Sense)


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Using a T20 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the screw.

    3. Remove in this Direction

      Slide the inner rear view mirror assembly as shown in the illustration to remove it.

  5. REMOVE INNER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY (w/ Toyota Safety Sense)


    1. Disconnect the connector.

    2. Disengage the clamp.

    3. Using a T20 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the screw.

    4. Remove in this Direction

      Slide the inner rear view mirror assembly as shown in the illustration to remove it.
