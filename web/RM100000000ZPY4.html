OIL LEVEL SENSOR INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT ENGINE OIL LEVEL SENSOR


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Engine Oil Level Sensor)
      *b Engine Oil Level Sensor Body
      *c ON
      *d OFF

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition

      OFF 10 kΩ or higher
