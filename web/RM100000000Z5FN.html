WASHER NOZZLE REMOVAL

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Tech Tips


  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.

  • The following procedure is for the LH side.

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE WASHER NOZZLE SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this Direction (1)
      Remove in this Direction (2)

      Using a screwdriver, disengage the 2 claws as indicated by the arrows, in the order shown in the illustration to separate the washer nozzle sub-assembly.

      Note

      Be careful not to damage the windshield glass.

      Tech Tips

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Disconnect the washer hose to remove the washer nozzle sub-assembly.

      Note

      Washer nozzle sub-assemblies cannot be reused.
