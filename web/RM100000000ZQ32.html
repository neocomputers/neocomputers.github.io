VEHICLE PROXIMITY NOTIFICATION SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. VEHICLE PROXIMITY NOTIFICATION SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


    1. System operation check (vehicle stopped)


      1. Apply the parking brake.

      2. Turn the power switch on (READY).

      3. Make sure that the vehicle approaching speaker switch indicator light is not illuminated.

      4. With the brake pedal depressed, confirm that the vehicle proximity notification system does not operate (does not produce a sound) when the shift lever is moved to N with park (P) selected..

      5. When the brake pedal is released, confirm that the vehicle proximity notification system operates for approximately 3 seconds (produces a sound).

    2. System operation check (vehicle being driven)


      1. Turn the power switch on (READY).

      2. Press the EV drive mode switch to select EV drive mode.

      3. Make sure that the vehicle approaching speaker switch indicator light is not illuminated.

      4. When the shift lever is moved to D with park (P) selected, confirm that the vehicle proximity notification system operates (produces a sound) when the vehicle is driven at speeds of 25 km/h (16 mph) or less using EV drive mode.

    3. System cancellation check


      1. Turn the power switch on (READY).

      2. Press the EV drive mode switch on the pattern select switch assembly to select EV drive mode.

      3. Make sure that the vehicle approaching speaker switch indicator light on the pattern select switch assembly is not illuminated.

      4. When the shift lever is moved to D with park (P) selected, confirm that the vehicle proximity notification system operates (produces a sound) when the vehicle is driven at speeds of 25 km/h (16 mph) or less using EV drive mode.

      5. Press the vehicle approaching speaker switch on the pattern select switch assembly and check that the indicator light illuminates.

      6. Confirm that the vehicle operates in EV drive mode.

      7. When the shift lever is moved to D with park (P) selected, confirm that the vehicle proximity notification system does not operate (does not produce a sound) when the vehicle is driven at speeds of 25 km/h (16 mph) or less using EV drive mode.
