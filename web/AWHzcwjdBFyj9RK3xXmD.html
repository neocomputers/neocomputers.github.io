MAINTENANCE SERVICE DATA

Coolant (for Engine)
Capacity Classification
6.0 liters (6.3 US qts, 5.3 Imp. qts) TOYOTA vehicles are filled with TOYOTA SLLC at the factory. In order to avoid damaging the engine cooling system and other technical problems, only use TOYOTA SLLC or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, nonamine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology is a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).
Coolant (for Inverter)
Capacity Classification
1.5 liters (1.6 US qts, 1.3 Imp. qts) Use only "TOYOTA Super Long Life Coolant" or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology consists of a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).
