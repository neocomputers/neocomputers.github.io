  5. REMOVE NO. 2 OUTER MIRROR COVER


    1. Disengage the 2 claws and guide to remove the No. 2 outer mirror cover.

  6. REMOVE OUTER MIRROR RETRACTOR (w/ Automatic Retractable Mirror)


    1. *a Cut here

      Cut the wire harness sub-assembly at the position shown in the illustration.

    2. Disengage the 3 guides and remove the gasket sub-assembly.

      Note

      Make sure to replace the gasket sub-assembly with a new one.

    3. Disengage the 5 claws and guide to remove the lower cover.

    4. Using a T25 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the 3 screws and base sub-assembly.

    5. Remove the 3 screws.

    6. Disconnect the connector.

    7. Disengage the 2 clamps.

    8. Disconnect the connector to remove the actuator sub-assembly.

    9. Remove in this Direction

      Pull the wire harness sub-assembly through the frame sub-assembly as shown in the illustration to remove the wire harness sub-assembly.

      Note

      Make sure to replace the wire harness sub-assembly with a new one.

    10. Remove the 2 screws.

    11. Remove the 2 screws and frame sub-assembly from the housing.

      Note

      Make sure to replace the frame sub-assembly with a new one.
