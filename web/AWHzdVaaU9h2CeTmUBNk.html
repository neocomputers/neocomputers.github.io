SRS SEAT CUSHION AIRBAG ASSEMBLY ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

CAUTION:

Be sure to correctly follow the removal and installation procedures for the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH.

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT SEAT CUSHION AIRBAG ASSEMBLY RH (for Vehicle not Involved in Collision)


    1. Perform a diagnostic system check.

      Click here

    2. Visually check for defects with the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH installed to the vehicle.

      Tech Tips

      The defects are as follows:


      • Scratches, broken or frayed threads on the surface of the front seat cushion assembly around the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH

      • Small cracks on the front seat cushion assembly around the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH

      • Significant discoloration on the front seat cushion assembly around the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH

      OK
      No defects are found.

      If any defects are found, replace the separate type front seat cushion pad or separate type front seat cushion cover with a new one.

  2. INSPECT FRONT SEAT CUSHION AIRBAG ASSEMBLY RH (for Vehicle Involved in Collision and Airbag not Deployed)


    1. Perform a diagnostic system check.

      Click here

    2. Visually check for defects with the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH removed from the vehicle.

      Tech Tips

      The defects are as follows:


      • Cuts

      • Small cracks

      • Significant discoloration

      • Marks from being dropped

      • Cracks or other damage to the wire harness or connector

      • Deformation of the installation surface of the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH

      OK
      No defects are found.

      If any defects are found, replace the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH with a new one.
