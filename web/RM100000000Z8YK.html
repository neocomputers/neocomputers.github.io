  9. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION AIRBAG ASSEMBLY RH

    CAUTION:

    When storing the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH, keep the airbag deployment side facing upward.

    Deployment Side

    1. Disengage the 2 clamps and claw.

      Tech Tips

      Disengage the clamps first.

    2. Remove the 3 nuts.

      Note


      • The nuts must not be reused.

      • Make sure that the front seat adjuster assembly RH is not deformed. If it is deformed, replace it with a new one.

    3. Pass the airbag wire harness through the hole to remove the front seat cushion airbag assembly RH.
