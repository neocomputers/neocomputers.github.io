AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B1383

DTC Code DTC Name
B1383 Middle Pressure Magnetic Valve Malfunction

DESCRIPTION

The No. 1 air conditioning accessory assembly (middle pressure magnetic valve) is installed to the accumulator and accessory assembly and is open when the power switch is off. When the power switch is on (IG), the No. 1 air conditioning accessory assembly (middle pressure magnetic valve) opens or closes according to the heat pump air-conditioning control. When the heat pump air-conditioning control is in gas injection heating mode, the No. 1 air conditioning accessory assembly (middle pressure magnetic valve) is closed based on the signal from the heat pump ECU assembly. When the heat pump air-conditioning control is not in gas injection heating mode, the No. 1 air conditioning accessory assembly (middle pressure magnetic valve) is open.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Memory
B1383 Middle Pressure Magnetic Valve Malfunction Open or short in No. 1 air conditioning accessory assembly (middle pressure magnetic valve) circuit

  • No. 1 air conditioning accessory assembly (middle pressure magnetic valve)

  • Harness or connector

  • FAN NO. 2 relay

  • Heat pump ECU assembly

Memorized

(4 sec. or more)*

  • *: The heat pump ECU assembly stores this DTC if the malfunction has occurred for the period of time indicated in the brackets.
