PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (BRAKE BOOSTER WITH MASTER CYLINDER ASSEMBLY - BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY)


    1. Make sure that there is no looseness at the locking part and the connecting part of the connectors.

      OK
      The connector is securely connected.

    2. Disconnect the A32 skid control ECU (brake booster with master cylinder assembly) connector.

    3. Disconnect the A33 brake actuator assembly connector (for LHD).

    4. Disconnect the A34 brake actuator assembly connector (for RHD).

    5. Check both the connector case and the terminals for deformation and corrosion.

      OK
      No deformation or corrosion.
