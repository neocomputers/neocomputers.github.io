PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE REAR WHEEL

  3. REMOVE NO. 1 FLOOR UNDER COVER ASSEMBLY (for RH Side)

    w/ Canister Pump Module: Click here

    w/o Canister Pump Module: Click here

  4. REMOVE REAR HEIGHT CONTROL SENSOR SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (for LH Side)

    Click here

  5. SEPARATE REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 2 bolts and separate the rear shock absorber assembly from the vehicle.

  6. LOOSEN REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY


    1. *1 Rear Axle Carrier Pin

      Loosen the nut of the rear shock absorber assembly.

      Note

      Hold the rear axle carrier pin while rotating the nut.

  7. REMOVE REAR STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  8. SEPARATE REAR UPPER CONTROL ARM ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the bolt and nut, and separate the rear upper control arm assembly from the rear axle carrier sub-assembly.

      Note

      Because the nut has its own stopper, do not turn the nut. Loosen the bolt with the nut secured.

  9. REMOVE REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY


    1. *1 Rear Axle Carrier Pin

      Remove the nut, plate washer and rear shock absorber assembly from the rear axle carrier sub-assembly.

      Note

      Hold the rear axle carrier pin while rotating the nut.

  10. REMOVE REAR SHOCK ABSORBER CAP


    1. *a Protective Tape

      Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped with protective tape, remove the rear shock absorber cap from the rear shock absorber assembly.

  11. REMOVE REAR SUSPENSION SUPPORT ASSEMBLY


    1. *a Turn
      *b Hold

      Secure the rear shock absorber assembly in a vise using aluminum plates.

      Note

      Do not overtighten the vise.

    2. Using SST and a 6 mm hexagon socket wrench, hold the rear shock absorber rod and remove the rear support to rear shock absorber nut.

      SST
      09729-97202

    3. Remove the rear suspension support assembly from the rear shock absorber assembly.
