  • A new No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH cannot operate until the vehicle information is registered.

  1. SYNCHRONIZE VEHICLE INFORMATION (When using the GTS)

    Note


    • Make sure to synchronize the vehicle information before performing initialization.

    • If synchronization fails, make sure to turn the power switch off then back on (IG) before performing synchronization again.


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    4. Check that the "Headlight System Uninitialized Visit Your Dealer" warning message is displayed on the multi-information display.

    5. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / HL AutoLeveling*1 or AHS*2 / Utility / ECU Information Sync.


      • *1: w/o Adaptive High Beam System

      • *2: w/ Adaptive High Beam System


      Body Electrical > HL AutoLeveling > Utility
      Tester Display
      ECU Information Sync

      Body Electrical > AHS > Utility
      Tester Display
      ECU Information Sync

    6. Check the display on the GTS and press "Next".

    7. Check that the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message continuously blinks 6 times at 2 Hz on the multi-information display.

      Tech Tips


      • If the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message does not start blinking on the multi-information display after perform the procedure again.

      • If the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message changes from blinking to displayed on the multi-information display, the rear height control sensor sub-assembly LH may be malfunctioning.

    8. Press "Exit".

    9. Turn the power switch off.

      Note

      After synchronizing the vehicle information, initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.

  2. Note


    • Make sure to synchronize the vehicle information before performing initialization.

    • If synchronization fails, make sure to turn the power switch off then back on (IG) before performing synchronization again.

    SYNCHRONIZE VEHICLE INFORMATION (When not using the GTS)


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    2. Check that the "Headlight System Uninitialized Visit Your Dealer" warning message is displayed on the multi-information display.

    3. Using the headlight dimmer switch, turn the low beam headlights on and off 8 times within 20 seconds after turning the power switch on (IG).

      Note

      If the low beam headlights illuminate when the power switch is on (IG), shine a light on the automatic light control sensor to turn the low beam headlights off before operating the headlight dimmer switch. (w/o Light Control Switch off Position)

      Tech Tips

      If this step cannot be completed within 20 seconds, turn the power switch off. Then, turn the power switch on (IG) and restart the procedure.

    4. Check that the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message continuously blinks 6 times at 2 Hz on the multi-information display.

      Tech Tips


      • If the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message does not start blinking on the multi-information display after perform the procedure again.

      • If the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message changes from blinking to displayed on the multi-information display, turn the power switch off and then on (IG). If "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" is displayed immediately after the power switch is turned on (IG), the rear height control sensor sub-assembly LH may be malfunctioning.

    5. Turn the power switch off.

      Note

      After synchronizing the vehicle information, initialize the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH.

  3. NO. 1 HEADLIGHT ECU SUB-ASSEMBLY LH INITIALIZATION (When using the GTS)

    Note

    Make sure to synchronize the vehicle information before performing initialization.


    1. Prepare vehicle for initialization


      • Unload the vehicle, ensuring that the spare tire, tools and jack are in their original positions.

      • Check that there are no occupants in the vehicle.

      • Turn the light control switch to the off position.

      • Stop the vehicle on a level surface and keep the vehicle height unchanged.

    2. Check the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display.


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG), and check the condition of the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display.

        Tech Tips

        If the condition of the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message is different than listed in the table above, the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH or combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate) may be malfunctioning.

      2. Perform initialization.


        1. Turn the power switch off.

        2. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

        3. Turn the power switch on (IG).

        4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / HL AutoLeveling*1 or AHS*2 / Utility / Height Sensor Initialization.


          • *1: w/o Adaptive High Beam System

          • *2: w/ Adaptive High Beam System


          Body Electrical > HL AutoLeveling > Utility
        3. Tester Display
        Height Sensor Initialization

Body Electrical > AHS > Utility
Tester Display
Height Sensor Initialization

  • Check the display on the GTS and press "Next".

  • *a Fuel Receiver Gauge

    Check the proper "Position ID" by the fuel level according to the table below.

    Tech Tips

    The "Position ID" is determined by the present fuel level as shown below.

    Fuel Level Position ID Number of Times Headlight Leveling Warning Blinks
    Within A range EMPTY 1
    Within B range 1/4 to over EMPTY 2
    Within C range 1/2 to over 1/4 3
    Within D range 3/4 to over 1/2 4
    Within E range FULL to over 3/4 5

  • Select the proper "Position ID" and press "Next".

  • Press "Exit".

  • Check the condition of the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display.

    Tech Tips


    • *: The number of times that "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message blinks is determined by the "Position ID" selected.

    • If the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message is displayed on the multi-information display, the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH may be malfunctioning.

    • If initialization does not finish normally, restart the initialization procedure from step (i).

  • Check that initialization finishes normally and turn the power switch off.

    Tech Tips

    The warning message will blink in the pattern indicating that initialization has completed successfully continuously until the power switch is turned off.

  • NO. 1 HEADLIGHT ECU SUB-ASSEMBLY LH INITIALIZATION (When not using the GTS)

    Note

    Make sure to synchronize the vehicle information before performing initialization.


    1. Prepare vehicle for initialization


      • Unload the vehicle, ensuring that the spare tire, tools and jack are in their original positions.

      • Check that there are no occupants in the vehicle.

      • Turn the light control switch to the off position.

      • Stop the vehicle on a level surface and keep the vehicle height unchanged.

    2. Check the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display.


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG), and check the condition of the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display.

    • Tech Tips

    If the condition of the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message is different than listed in the table above, the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH or combination meter assembly (meter circuit plate) may be malfunctioning.

  • Perform initialization.


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    • *a Fuel Receiver Gauge

    • Check the specified number of times to turn the low beam headlights on and off.

    Tech Tips

    The number of times the low beam headlights need to be turned on and off is determined by the present fuel level as shown below.

    Fuel Level Number of Times to Turn Low Beam Headlights On and Off
    Within A range 1
    Within B range 2
    Within C range 3
    Within D range 4
    Within E range 5

  • Enter initialization mode. (w/ Light Control Switch off Position)


    1. Turn the power switch and light control switch off.

    2. Cover the automatic light control sensor with a lightproof object.

    3. Perform the following steps within 5 seconds.


      1. Press the power switch 2 times.

        Tech Tips

        Turn the power switch on (IG).

      2. While pulling the headlight dimmer switch, turn it from off to AUTO to off again 3 times or more.

        Tech Tips

        Check that the clearance lights turn from off to on and back off again.

      3. Turn the light control switch off.

    Tech Tips


    • Perform the above operation within 5 seconds.

    • When initialization mode has been entered, "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display starts blinking at 1 Hz.

  • Enter initialization mode. (w/o Light Control Switch off Position)


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Shine a light on the automatic light control sensor.

    3. Perform the following steps within 5 seconds.


      1. Press the power switch 2 times.

        Tech Tips

        Turn the power switch on (IG).

      2. While pulling the headlight dimmer switch, turn it from AUTO to TAIL to AUTO again 3 times or more.

        Tech Tips

        Check that the clearance lights turn from off to on and back off again.

      3. Turn the light control switch AUTO position.

    Tech Tips


    • Perform the above operation within 5 seconds.

    • When initialization mode has been entered, "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display starts blinking at 1 Hz.

  • After entering initialization mode, turn the low beam headlights from off to on and back off again N* times.

    Tech Tips


    • *: The number of times to turn the light control switch on and off (N) is determined by the fuel level of the fuel receiver gauge.

    • Turn the light control switch on and off within 20 seconds of entering initialization mode.

    • Turn the light control switch on and off at intervals of approx. 3 seconds.

  • Check the condition of the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message on the multi-information display.

    Tech Tips


    • *: The number of times that "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message blinks is determined by the number of times the low beam headlights are turned on and off.

    • If the "Headlight System Malfunction Visit Your Dealer" warning message is displayed on the multi-information display, the No. 1 headlight ECU sub-assembly LH may be malfunctioning.

    • If initialization does not finish normally, restart the initialization procedure from step (i).

  • Check that initialization finishes normally and turn the power switch off.

    Tech Tips

    The warning message will blink in the pattern indicating that initialization has completed successfully continuously until the power switch is turned off.
