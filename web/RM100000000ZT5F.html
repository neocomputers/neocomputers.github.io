GRILLE SHUTTER DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE MOTOR COVER


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 4 claws as shown in the illustration to remove the motor cover.

  2. REMOVE SWING GRILLE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove in this Direction

      Remove the swing grille actuator assembly as shown in the illustration.

  3. REMOVE NO. 5 RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL


    1. Remove the No. 5 radiator grille seal.

  4. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL


    1. Remove the radiator grille seal.

  5. REMOVE NO. 3 RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL


    1. Remove the No. 3 radiator grille seal.

  6. REMOVE NO. 4 RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL


    1. Remove the No. 4 radiator grille seal.

  7. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL


    1. Remove the No. 2 radiator grille seal.

  8. REMOVE NO. 6 RADIATOR GRILLE SEAL


    1. Remove the No. 6 radiator grille seal.
