VSC OFF SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT VSC OFF SWITCH (COMBINATION SWITCH ASSEMBLY)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (VSC OFF Switch (Combination Switch Assembly))

      Make sure that there is no looseness in the locking part and the connecting part of the connector.

      OK
      The connector is securely connected.

    2. Disconnect the VSC OFF switch (combination switch assembly) connector.

    3. Check both the connector case and the terminals for deformation and corrosion.

      OK
      No deformation or corrosion.

    4. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      2 (+) - 6 (E) Switch pushed Below 1 Ω
      2 (+) - 6 (E) Switch not pushed 10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the VSC OFF switch (combination switch assembly).
