TIRE PRESSURE WARNING SYSTEM ECU Power Source Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The IG circuit is the power source for the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver.

WIRING DIAGRAM

*a from IG Circuit
*b ECU-IG1 NO. 4
*c Tire Pressure Warning ECU and Receiver

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note


  • When replacing the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver, read the transmitter IDs stored in the old ECU using the GTS and write them down before removal.

  • It is necessary to perform initialization after registration Click here of the transmitter IDs into the tire pressure warning ECU and receiver if the ECU has been replaced.

Tech Tips

Inspect the fuses for circuits related to this system before performing the following inspection procedure.
