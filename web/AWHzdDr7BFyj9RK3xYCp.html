REAR SHOCK ABSORBER DISPOSAL

PROCEDURE


  1. DISPOSE OF REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY


    1. Extend the piston rod and secure the rear shock absorber assembly at an angle in a vise.

    2. Using a hacksaw, slowly make a hole at the position indicated by the arrow in the illustration to discharge the gas inside.

      CAUTION:


      • Always use a cloth to prevent shards of metal flying about due to the release of pressurized gas.

      • Always wear safety glasses.

      Tech Tips

      The gas is colorless, odorless and non-poisonous.
