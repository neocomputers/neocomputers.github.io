FRONT DOOR LOCK INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT DOOR LOCK WITH MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH (w/o Double Locking System)


    1. *a Unlock
      *b Lock
      *c

      Component without harness connected

      (Front Door Lock with Motor Assembly LH)

      Check the operation of the door lock motor.


      1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage and check the operation of the door lock motor.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Result

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 4

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Locks

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 4

        		 Unlocks

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front door lock with motor assembly LH.

    2. Check the operation of the door unlock detection switch.


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        7 - 8 Locked 10 kΩ or higher
        7 - 8 Unlocked Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front door lock with motor assembly LH.

    3. *a Lock
      *b Neutral
      *c Unlock
      *d

      Component without harness connected

      (Front Door Lock with Motor Assembly LH)

      Check the operation of the door key lock and unlock switch.


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        9 - 7 Held to lock side Below 1 Ω
        9 - 7 Neutral or held to unlock side 10 kΩ or higher
        10 - 7 Held to unlock side Below 1 Ω
        10 - 7 Neutral or held to lock side 10 kΩ or higher

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front door lock with motor assembly LH.

  3. INSPECT FRONT DOOR LOCK WITH MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Double Locking System)


    1. *a Unlock
      *b Lock
      *c

      Component without harness connected

      (Front Door Lock with Motor Assembly LH)

      Check the operation of the front door lock motor.


      1. Apply auxiliary battery voltage and check the operation of the front door lock motor and double lock motor.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Result

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 4

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 3

        		 Locks

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 3

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 4

        		 Unlocks

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Sets

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

        		 Unsets

        Tech Tips


        • Move the door lock motor to the lock position and set the double locking system. Check that the doors cannot be unlocked by operating the control cable.

        • Move the door lock motor to the lock position and unset the double locking system. Check that the doors can be unlocked by operating the control cable.

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front door lock with motor assembly LH.

    2. Check the operation of the door unlock detection switch and double lock position switch.


      1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

        Standard Resistance
        Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
        8 - 7 Locked 10 kΩ or higher
        8 - 7 Unlocked Below 1 Ω
        6 - 5 Unset 10 kΩ or higher
        6 - 5 Set Below 1 Ω

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front door lock with motor assembly LH.
