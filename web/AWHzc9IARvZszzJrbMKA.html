PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE INTAKE VALVE


    1. *a Wooden Block

      Using SST and wooden blocks, compress the inner compression spring and remove the 8 valve spring retainer locks.

      SST
      09202-70020   ( 09202-01010, 09202-01020 )
      09202-00021

      Tech Tips

      Arrange the removed parts in such a way that they can be reinstalled to their original locations.

    2. Remove the 8 valve spring retainers, 8 inner compression springs and 8 intake valves from the cylinder head sub-assembly.

      Tech Tips

      Arrange the removed parts in such a way that they can be reinstalled to their original locations.

  2. REMOVE EXHAUST VALVE


    1. *a Wooden Block

      Using SST and wooden blocks, compress the inner compression spring and remove the 8 valve spring retainer locks.

      SST
      09202-70020   ( 09202-01010, 09202-01020 )
      09202-00021

      Tech Tips

      Arrange the removed parts in such a way that they can be reinstalled to their original locations.

    2. Remove the 8 valve spring retainers, 8 inner compression springs and 8 exhaust valves from the cylinder head sub-assembly.

      Tech Tips

      Arrange the removed parts in such a way that they can be reinstalled to their original locations.

  3. REMOVE VALVE STEM OIL SEAL


    1. Using needle-nose pliers, remove the 16 valve stem oil seals.

  4. REMOVE VALVE SPRING SEAT


    1. Using compressed air and a Magnet Hand, remove the 16 valve spring seats from the cylinder head sub-assembly by blowing air onto them.

  5. REMOVE NO. 2 STRAIGHT SCREW PLUG

    Note

    If coolant leaks from the No. 2 straight screw plug or the No. 2 straight screw plug is corroded, replace it.


    1. Using a 10 mm straight hexagon wrench, remove the 3 No. 2 straight screw plugs and 3 gaskets from the cylinder head sub-assembly.
