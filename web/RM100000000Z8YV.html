SIDE AIRBAG SENSOR(for Front Door) INSTALLATION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSTALL DOOR SIDE AIRBAG SENSOR


    1. Check that the power switch is off.

    2. Check that the cable is disconnected from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal.

      CAUTION:

      Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) auxiliary battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    3. Before connecting the connector, check that the position of the white housing lock is as shown in the illustration.

      *a Incorrect *b Correct
    4. *a Stopper

      Insert the door side airbag sensor stopper into the door hole, engage the claw and install the door side airbag sensor to the front door panel with the nut.

      Torque:
      9.0 N*m  { 92 kgf*cm, 80 in.*lbf }

      Note


      • If the door side airbag sensor has been dropped, or there are any cracks, dents or other defects in the case or connector, replace it with a new one.

      • When installing the door side airbag sensor, make sure that the SRS wiring does not interfere with or is not pinched between other parts.

      • Make sure that the door side airbag sensor stopper is securely inserted into the door hole.

      • Keep the stud bolt and waterproof rubber seal free of foreign matter.

    5. Connect the connector to the door side airbag sensor.

      *a White Housing Lock *b Connection is completed

      Note

      When connecting any airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.

      Tech Tips


      • Be sure to connect the connector until it is locked (when locking, make sure that a click sound can be heard).

      • When the connector is locked, the white housing lock will slide. Do not hold the white housing lock or the part (A) as it may result in an insecure connection.

    6. Check that there is no looseness in the installed parts of the door side airbag sensor.
