SIMPLE INTELLIGENT PARKING ASSIST SYSTEM DTC CHECK / CLEAR


  1. CHECK DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar > Trouble Codes

    5. Check for DTCs.

  2. CLEAR DTC


    1. Connect the GTS to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the GTS on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Body Electrical / IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar / Trouble Codes.


      Body Electrical > IPA/ICS/Clearance Sonar > Trouble Codes

    5. Clear the DTCs.
