AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM, Diagnostic DTC:B1385

DTC Code DTC Name
B1385 Cooling Electric Expansion Valve Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The electric expansion valve (cooling) is installed to the inlet pipe of the No. 1 cooler evaporator assembly and is closed when the power switch is off. When the power switch is on (IG), the heat pump air-conditioning control operates the stepping motor to adjust the opening amount of the electric expansion valve (cooling). When the heat pump air-conditioning control is in heating/gas injection heating mode, the electric expansion valve (cooling) is closed based on the signal from the heat pump ECU assembly. When the heat pump air-conditioning control is in cooling/series heating/parallel heating mode, the electric expansion valve (cooling) is adjusted to optimally control the opening amount based on the signal from the heat pump ECU assembly.

DTC No. Detection Item DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area Memory
B1385 Cooling Electric Expansion Valve Circuit When the motor is being operated, there is an open or short in the electric expansion valve (cooling) circuit or the electric expansion valve (cooling) is overheating
