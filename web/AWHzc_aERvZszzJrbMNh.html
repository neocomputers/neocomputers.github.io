OIL PUMP DISASSEMBLY

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE OIL PUMP RELIEF VALVE


    1. *1 Oil Pump Relief Valve Plug
      *2 Oil Pump Relief Valve Spring
      *3 Oil Pump Relief Valve

      Using a 27 mm socket wrench, remove the oil pump relief valve plug from the oil pump body.

    2. Remove the oil pump relief valve spring and oil pump relief valve from the oil pump body.

  2. REMOVE OIL PUMP COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Remove the 5 bolts and oil pump cover sub-assembly from the oil pump body.
