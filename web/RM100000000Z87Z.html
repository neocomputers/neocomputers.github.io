NAVIGATION SYSTEM(for Navigation Receiver Type) Navigation Receiver Assembly Power Source Circuit

DESCRIPTION

This is the power source circuit to operate the navigation receiver assembly.

WIRING DIAGRAM

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

Note

Inspect the fuses for circuits related to this system before performing the following procedure.

PROCEDURE


  1. CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (NAVIGATION RECEIVER ASSEMBLY POWER SOURCE)


    1. Disconnect the I34 and I33 navigation receiver assembly connectors.

    2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      I34-7 (GND1) - Body ground Always Below 1 Ω

    3. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Voltage
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      I34-4 (+B1) - I34-7 (GND1) Power switch off 11 to 14 V
      I34-3 (ACC1) - I34-7 (GND1) Power switch on (ACC) 11 to 14 V
      I33-1 (IG) - I34-7 (GND1) Power switch on (IG) 11 to 14 V
