  9. REMOVE MOTOR COOLING COOLER


    1. Slide the clip and disconnect the No. 1 motor cooling hose from the No. 1 motor cooling pipe.

    2. Remove the bolt and No. 1 oil cooler tube clamp from the hose bracket.

    3. Remove the union bolt and gasket to disconnect the No. 2 motor cooling pipe from the hybrid vehicle transaxle assembly.

    4. Slide the clip and remove the No. 2 motor cooling pipe from the No. 2 motor cooling hose.

    5. Remove the 3 bolts and motor cooling cooler from the hybrid vehicle transaxle assembly.

      Note

      Make sure not to remove the No. 1 motor cooling hose and No. 2 motor cooling hose from the motor cooling cooler as the motor cooling cooler may be deformed.
