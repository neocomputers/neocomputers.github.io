LIGHTING SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


  1. AUTOMATIC LIGHT CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


    1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    2. Turn the light control switch to the AUTO position.

    3. Cover the automatic light control sensor.

    4. Check that the taillights and low beam headlights come on.

    5. Uncover the automatic light control sensor.

    6. Check that the low beam headlights and taillights turn off.

  2. LIGHT AUTO TURN-OFF SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK (except Driver Door-linked Auto Light Turn-off System)


    1. When the light control switch is in the AUTO position


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      2. Turn the taillights, low beam headlights, high beam headlights, front fog lights or rear fog light* on.

      3. Turn the power switch off.

        Tech Tips

        If the vehicle speed was not less than 10 km/h (6 mph) when the power switch was turned off, open the driver door.

      4. Check that all of the lights turn off immediately.


        • *: w/ Rear Fog Light

    2. When the light control switch is in the tail or head position


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      2. Turn the low beam headlights, high beam headlights, or front fog lights on.

      3. Turn the power switch off.

        Tech Tips

        If the vehicle speed was not less than 10 km/h (6 mph) when the power switch was turned off, open the driver door.

      4. Check that the low beam headlights, high beam headlights and front fog lights turn off immediately.

        Tech Tips

        The taillights, clearance lights, license plate lights and rear fog light* remain on.


        • *: w/ Rear Fog Light

  3. LIGHT AUTO TURN-OFF SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK (for Driver Door-linked Auto Light Turn-off System)


    1. Close all of the doors.

    2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the taillights, low beam headlights, high beam headlights, front fog lights and rear fog lights* on.

    4. Turn the power switch off.

    5. Open the driver door.

    6. Check that all of the lights turn off immediately.


      • *: w/ Rear Fog Light

  4. LIGHT AUTO TURN-OFF SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK (w/ Delay Function)


    1. When the taillights are on (low beam headlights are not on)


      1. Close all of the doors.

      2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      3. Turn the taillights on.

      4. Turn the power switch off.

      5. Open the driver door.

      6. Check that all of the lights turn off immediately.

    2. When the low beam headlights are on


      1. Close all of the doors.

      2. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      3. Turn the low beam headlights on.

      4. Turn the power switch off.

      5. Open any of the doors.

      6. Close the door.

      7. Check that all of the lights turn off after 30 seconds (delay function).

        Tech Tips

        Before the lights turn off, if the lock button on the key is pushed after all of the doors are locked, all of the lights will turn off immediately.

  5. DAYTIME RUNNING LIGHT SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK


    1. Turn the power switch on (READY).

    2. Turn the light control switch to the off* or AUTO position (make sure the taillights not turned on by the automatic light control system).

    3. Release the parking brake.

    4. Check that the daytime running lights come on.


      • *: w/ Light Control Switch off Position

  6. FOLLOW ME HOME SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK (w/ Follow Me Home System)


    1. Turn the power switch off.

    2. Turn the light control switch to the off* or AUTO position.

    3. Pull the light control switch to flash the headlights once.

    4. Check that the low beam headlights come on for approximately 30 seconds.


      • *: w/ Light Control Switch off Position
