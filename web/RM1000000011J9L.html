HIGH PRESSURE MAGNETIC VALVE INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT HIGH PRESSURE MAGNETIC VALVE


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (High Pressure Magnetic Valve)

      Connect the auxiliary battery positive (+) lead to terminal 2 of the high pressure magnetic valve connector and the auxiliary battery negative (-) lead to terminal 1. Then check that the high pressure magnetic valve operates.

      OK
      Apply compressed air to the air inlet and check that air not comes out from the air outlet.

      • If the result is not as specified, replace the high pressure magnetic valve.
