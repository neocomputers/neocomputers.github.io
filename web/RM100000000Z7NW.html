FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY(for Power Seat) INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT FRONT SEAT ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY (DRIVER SEAT)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Slide Motor (Front Seat Adjuster Assembly (Driver Seat))

      Check the operation of the slide motor.


      1. Check that the seat cushion moves smoothly when the auxiliary battery is connected to the slide motor connector terminals.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Operational Direction

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Seat cushion moves forward

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

        		 Seat cushion moves backward

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front seat adjuster assembly (driver seat).

  2. INSPECT POWER SEAT TILT MOTOR ASSEMBLY (DRIVER SEAT)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Power Seat Tilt Motor Assembly (Driver Seat))

      Check the operation of the power seat tilt motor assembly (driver seat).


      1. Check that the seat cushion moves smoothly when the auxiliary battery is connected to the power seat tilt motor assembly (driver seat) connector terminals.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Operational Direction

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

        		 Seat cushion moves upward

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Seat cushion moves downward

        If the result is not as specified, replace the power seat tilt motor assembly (driver seat).

  3. INSPECT POWER SEAT LIFTER MOTOR ASSEMBLY (DRIVER SEAT)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Power Seat lifter Motor Assembly (Driver Seat))

      Check the operation of the power seat lifter motor assembly (driver seat).


      1. Check that the seat cushion moves smoothly when the auxiliary battery is connected to the power seat lifter motor assembly (driver seat) connector terminals.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Operational Direction

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 2

        		 Seat cushion moves upward

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 2

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Seat cushion moves downward

        If the result is not as specified, replace the power seat lifter motor assembly (driver seat).

  4. INSPECT FRONT SEATBACK FRAME SUB-ASSEMBLY (DRIVER SEAT)


    1. *a

      Component without harness connected

      (Reclining Motor (Front Seatback Frame Sub-assembly (Driver Seat)))

      Check the operation of the reclining motor.


      1. Check that the seatback moves smoothly when the auxiliary battery is connected to the reclining motor connector terminals.

        OK
        Auxiliary Battery Connection Operational Direction

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 1

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 4

        		 Seatback moves forward

        Auxiliary battery positive (+) → Terminal 4

        Auxiliary battery negative (-) → Terminal 1

        		 Seatback moves backward

        If the result is not as specified, replace the front seatback frame sub-assembly (driver seat).
