WIPER SWITCH REMOVAL

PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE LOWER STEERING COLUMN COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Note

    Removing the lower steering column cover sub-assembly in the incorrect order will cause the parts to break.


    1. Release the tilt and telescopic lever and fully extend and lower the steering column assembly.

    2. Lock the tilt and telescopic lever.

    3. Turn the steering wheel assembly to the left and remove the screw.

    4. Turn the steering wheel assembly to the right and remove the screw.

    5. Push

      Push the upper steering column cover and disengage the 2 claws as shown in the illustration.

    6. Disengage the 2 claws.

    7. Release the tilt and telescopic lever and fully extend and lift the steering column assembly.

    8. Lock the tilt and telescopic lever.

    9. Place Hand Here
      Remove in this Direction

      Pull the lower steering column cover sub-assembly toward the rear of the vehicle to disengage the claw and remove the lower steering column cover sub-assembly.

  2. REMOVE UPPER STEERING COLUMN COVER


    1. Release the tilt and telescopic lever and fully extend and lower the steering column assembly.

    2. Lock the tilt and telescopic lever.

    3. Separate in this Direction

      Disengage the claw to separate the upper steering column cover as shown in the illustration.

    4. Disengage the 2 claws and 4 clips to remove the upper steering column cover from the lower instrument cover sub-assembly.

  3. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH ASSEMBLY


    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    2. *a Protective Tape
      Remove in this Direction

      Using a screwdriver, disengage the claw and remove the windshield wiper switch assembly as shown in the illustration.

      Note

      If the claw is pulled with excessive force, it may break.

      Tech Tips

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
