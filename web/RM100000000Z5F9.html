FRONT WIPER MOTOR INSPECTION

CAUTION / NOTICE / HINT

CAUTION:

Ensure that fingers or articles of clothing do not get caught in moving parts when performing this test.

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR ASSEMBLY (for LHD)

    *a

    Component without harness connected

    (Windshield Wiper Motor Assembly)

    1. Check the LO operation.


      1. Connect a positive (+) lead from the auxiliary battery to terminal 1 (+1) and a negative (-) lead to terminal 5 (E), and check that the motor operates.

        OK
        Motor operates at low speed (LO).

    2. Check the HI operation.


      1. Connect a positive (+) lead from the auxiliary battery to terminal 4 (+2) and a negative (-) lead to terminal 5 (E), and check that the motor operates.

        OK
        Motor operates at high speed (HI).

        If the result is not as specified, replace the windshield wiper motor assembly.

  2. INSPECT WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR ASSEMBLY (for RHD)

    *a

    Component without harness connected

    (Windshield Wiper Motor Assembly)

    1. Check the LO operation.


      1. Connect a positive (+) lead from the auxiliary battery to terminal 5 (+1) and a negative (-) lead to terminal 4 (E), and check that the motor operates.

        OK
        Motor operates at low speed (LO).

    2. Check the HI operation.


      1. Connect a positive (+) lead from the auxiliary battery to terminal 3 (+2) and a negative (-) lead to terminal 4 (E), and check that the motor operates.

        OK
        Motor operates at high speed (HI).

        If the result is not as specified, replace the windshield wiper motor assembly.
