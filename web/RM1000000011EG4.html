PROCEDURE


  1. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY

    Click here

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 CLEARANCE WARNING BUZZER


    1. for LHD:


      1. Using a clip remover, disengage the clamp.

      2. Disconnect the connector to remove the No. 1 clearance warning buzzer.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Using a clip remover, disengage the clamp.

      2. Disconnect the connector to remove the No. 1 clearance warning buzzer.

  3. REMOVE NO. 2 CLEARANCE WARNING BUZZER


    1. for LHD:


      1. Using a clip remover, disengage the clamp.

      2. Disconnect the connector to remove the No. 2 clearance warning buzzer.

    2. for RHD:


      1. Using a clip remover, disengage the clamp.

      2. Disconnect the connector to remove the No. 2 clearance warning buzzer.
