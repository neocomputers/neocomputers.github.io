PARKING BRAKE SWITCH INSPECTION

PROCEDURE


  1. INSPECT PARKING BRAKE SWITCH ASSEMBLY


    1. *a Terminal
      *b Nut
      *c ON
      *d OFF

      Measure the resistance between the connector terminal and the nut inside the parking brake switch assembly.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the parking brake switch assembly.

      Standard Resistance
      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      Terminal - Nut On (When shaft is not pressed) Below 1 Ω
      Terminal - Nut Off (When shaft is pressed) Below 1 Ω
