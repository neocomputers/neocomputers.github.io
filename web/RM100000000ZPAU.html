  23. REMOVE GLOVE COMPARTMENT DOOR ASSEMBLY


    1. *1 Glove Compartment Door Stopper Sub-assembly

      Disengage the claw to disconnect the glove compartment door stopper sub-assembly.

    2. Slightly bend the stoppers (A) and (B) in the directions indicated by the arrows shown in the illustration and open the glove compartment door assembly until the stoppers are released.

    3. Open the glove compartment door assembly to approximately 64.2° from its closed position. Pull it horizontally as shown in the illustration to disengage the 2 hinges and remove the glove compartment door assembly.

      *a 64.2° - -
      Remove in this Direction - -

      Note

      Pulling the glove compartment door assembly upward when removing it will cause the hinges to deform. Be sure to pull out the glove compartment door assembly horizontally.

  32. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY


    1. Disengage the 2 claws and 4 clips to disconnect the lower instrument cover sub-assembly from the upper steering column cover.

    2. Remove in this Direction

      Disengage the 2 claws to disconnect the cooler (room temp. sensor) thermistor as shown in the illustration.

    3. Disconnect each connector.

      *A w/ Digital Audio Broadcasting Antenna or Television Antenna - -

    4. Disengage each clamp.

    5. Remove the 2 clips.

      *a Bolt <A> *b Bolt <B>
      *c Screw <C> or <G> *d Screw <D>
      *e Clip - -

    6. Remove the 2 bolts <A>, 4 bolts <B>, 3 screws <C> or <G>, screw <D> and lower instrument panel sub-assembly.

      Note


      • Do not damage the lower instrument panel sub-assembly.

      • Do not allow the wire harnesses to interfere with the surrounding parts.
