LANE DEPARTURE ALERT SYSTEM(w/ Steering Control) OPERATION CHECK


  1. CHECK LANE DEPARTURE ALERT MAIN SWITCH (STEERING PAD SWITCH ASSEMBLY)


    1. Check the lane departure alert main switch (steering pad switch assembly) on/off operation.


      1. Turn the power switch on (IG).

      2. *a Lane Departure Alert Indicator

        Confirm that the lane departure alert indicator (green) in the combination meter assembly illuminates or turns off when the lane departure alert main switch (steering pad switch assembly) is pressed.

        Tech Tips

        The ON/OFF status of the lane departure alert system remains unchanged when the power switch is turned off and back to on (IG).
